## Overview

Qlik is a leader in **analytics and business intelligence** — its platform, **Qlik Sense** (client-managed or
**Qlik Cloud** SaaS), lets people explore data and build interactive visualizations and analytics apps. Qlik's
signature is its **Associative Engine** — an in-memory associative model letting users explore data freely and
see not just what is related but what is *not* ("the power of gray"). Qlik also champions **data literacy**. It
is the associative-analytics peer of [Tableau (CLIV)](../volume-154-tableau-certifications/README.md); **Qlik vs
Tableau** is the defining BI comparison.

Chapter 02 covers **the associative model** — Qlik's differentiator. Chapters 03–08 take the platform: **Qlik
Sense and Qlik Cloud** (apps, master items), **Data Architect** (loading and modeling data), **Business Analyst**
(building visualizations), **set analysis** (the distinctive expression skill), **System Administrator**
(deploying and governing), and **data literacy and AI** (Insight Advisor, AutoML, Qlik Answers). Chapter 09
closes on choosing a path.

A theme runs through it: **the associative engine** lets users explore freely and see the negative space, while
**master items**, **set analysis**, and **governance** make Qlik consistent and trustworthy at scale.

## The credentials

Two tiers — fundamental **Qualifications** and expert **Certifications**:

| Tier | Credentials |
| --- | --- |
| **Qualifications** (fundamental) | Data Literacy (30Q/1hr, product-agnostic) · QS Business Analyst (build app + MCQ) · QS Data Architect (build app + MCQ) |
| **Certifications** (expert) | QS Business Analyst (QSBA) · QS Data Architect (QSDA) — **50Q/90min/62%**, platform-neutral · QS System Administrator (QSSA) |

Platform-neutral means the Certifications apply to both client-managed Qlik Sense and Qlik Cloud.

## What you will be able to do

- Read the two-tier program (Qualifications vs expert Certifications) and its mechanics.
- Explain the associative engine — green/white/gray selections and "the power of gray."
- Navigate Qlik Sense and Qlik Cloud — apps, sheets, stories, and master items for governed self-service.
- Model data as a Data Architect — the Data Load Editor, clean associations, avoiding synthetic keys, QVD loading.
- Build visualizations and analysis as a Business Analyst — the right chart, selection-driven exploration, stories.
- Write set analysis — defining data sets independent of selections for comparative analytics.
- Deploy and govern as a System Administrator — the QMC, streams/spaces, security rules, and reload tasks.
- Apply data literacy and Qlik's AI (Insight Advisor, AutoML, Qlik Answers) across the data-to-insight platform.

## Prerequisites

- Familiarity with data, spreadsheets, and analytics concepts helps.
- A Linux or macOS host with `python3`. **Qlik certifications** are delivered via the Qlik learning portal.
