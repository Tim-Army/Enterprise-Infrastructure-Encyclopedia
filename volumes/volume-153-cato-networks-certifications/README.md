## Overview

Cato Networks is the **pioneer of SASE** (Secure Access Service Edge) — a **single-vendor converged SASE cloud** that
delivers *both* networking (SD-WAN) *and* security (FWaaS, SWG, CASB, ZTNA) as one cloud service from a **global
private backbone**. Where the [Zscaler (XXXV)](../volume-035-zscaler-zero-trust-exchange/README.md),
[Netskope (CXXVII)](../volume-127-netskope-certifications/README.md), and
[Cloudflare (CXLII)](../volume-142-cloudflare-certifications/README.md) volumes cover other SASE/SSE players, **Cato
is the converged pure-play**, and its program teaches SASE as a discipline.

Chapter 02 covers **what SASE is** — the convergence of networking and security into one cloud service. Chapter 03
covers **the traditional stack versus converged SASE**. Chapter 04 covers **single-pass architecture and the global
backbone**. Chapter 05 covers **SASE networking** — SD-WAN and the edge. Chapter 06 covers **SASE security** — FWaaS,
SWG, and CASB. Chapter 07 covers **ZTNA** — zero trust network access. Chapter 08 covers **SSE and advanced
security**. Chapter 09 closes on choosing a path.

A theme runs through it: **convergence** — networking and security delivered as one cloud service, near the user,
in a single pass, replaces the point-product stack with its gaps and its backhaul.

## What you will be able to do

- Read the free, level-based program and sequence courses by role from SASE Expert Level 1.
- Explain SASE as the convergence of networking and security into one cloud-delivered, identity-driven service.
- Articulate why the point-product stack fails (complexity, seams, backhaul) and convergence wins.
- Explain single-pass architecture and the global private backbone as the pillars of a fast converged cloud.
- Describe SD-WAN active-active application-aware routing as the networking half.
- Apply FWaaS, SWG, and CASB (with shadow-IT discovery) as the converged security functions.
- Replace the VPN with ZTNA — per-application, identity-based, least-privilege access.
- Place SSE as the security subset and add advanced threat prevention and TLS inspection.
