# Volume XXXVIII Glossary

Definitions for terms used in **Volume XXXVIII — Microsoft Certifications
Beyond Azure**, alphabetized. See also the [volume index](INDEX.md) and the
[master glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md) for cross-volume terminology.

**Associate** — The certification tier validating the core skills of a job
role (for example MS-102, SC-300, DP-700); the mainstream working credential
above Fundamentals. Used in Chapter 01.

**Beta exam** — A newly launched exam offered at a discount whose results are
delivered only after the beta period closes and the passing score is set; how
the newest content enters the program. Used in Chapter 09.

**Catalog API** — The Microsoft Learn REST endpoint
(`learn.microsoft.com/api/catalog`) that returns every credential and exam as
JSON, used to enumerate the program and detect renames, betas, and
retirements. Used in Chapters 01 and 09.

**Credly** — Microsoft's digital-badging partner; passing an exam issues a
shareable Credly badge and a certificate. Used in Chapter 01.

**Customer Engagement (CE)** — The Dynamics 365 track built on Dataverse and
Power Platform (Sales, Customer Service, Field Service, Customer Insights),
whose functional exams overlap the PL family. Used in Chapter 05.

**Expert** — The senior certification tier validating cross-domain roles (for
example MS-102 Administrator Expert, SC-100 Cybersecurity Architect, PL-600
Solution Architect). Used in Chapter 01.

**Finance and Operations (F&O)** — The Dynamics 365 ERP track (Finance,
Supply Chain Management, Commerce) with its own developer (MB-500) and
architect (MB-700) exams. Used in Chapter 05.

**Fundamentals** — The entry tier (the `x-900` exams — MS-900, SC-900,
PL-900, DP-900, AI-901, MB-910/MB-920, GH-900) proving foundational knowledge
of a family, with no prerequisites. Used in Chapter 01.

**GH- code** — The exam-code prefix for GitHub certifications (GH-900, GH-100,
GH-300, GH-500, GH-600), which are part of the Microsoft catalog on Microsoft
Learn. Used in Chapter 08.

**Microsoft Fabric** — Microsoft's unified analytics platform; its
certifications (DP-700 Data Engineer, DP-600 Analytics Engineer) absorbed much
of the retired Azure Synapse/Data Factory scope (DP-203). Used in Chapter 06.

**Microsoft Learn** — The authoritative site (`learn.microsoft.com/
credentials`) for certification pages, exam blueprints, study guides, practice
assessments, and status. Used in Chapter 01.

**Renewal** — The free, unproctored online assessment on Microsoft Learn that
extends a role-based certification for another year, available in the six
months before expiry. Used in Chapters 01 and 09.

**Role-based certification** — A credential that validates the skills of a job
role (administrator, developer, analyst, architect) rather than a single
product, earned by passing one or more coded exams. Used in Chapter 01.

**Skills measured (blueprint)** — The weighted list of skill domains an exam
tests, published on the certification page; the authoritative study scope.
Used in Chapters 01–08.

**Specialty** — A certification tier covering a focused technology (for
example DP-420 Azure Cosmos DB Developer). Used in Chapter 06.
