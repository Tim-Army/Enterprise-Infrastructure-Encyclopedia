## Overview

Delinea is a leader in **Privileged Access Management (PAM)** — securing, controlling, and monitoring
**privileged access** (administrator, root, and service accounts), the highest-leverage defensive control
since privileged-credential abuse runs through nearly every breach. Delinea was formed from the **2021 merger
of Thycotic and Centrify**, combining Thycotic's **Secret Server** vault and **Privilege Manager** with
Centrify's **server PAM** and Active Directory bridging, now unified as the **Delinea Platform**. It is one of
the three PAM leaders this shelf covers, alongside [BeyondTrust (CLVI)](../volume-156-beyondtrust-certifications/README.md)
and [CyberArk (LXXVII)](../volume-077-cyberark-certifications/README.md) — the **PAM trio**.

Chapter 02 frames Delinea and the PAM landscape (the Thycotic + Centrify heritage and the unified platform).
Chapters 03–08 take the portfolio: **Secret Server** (the vault), **Privilege Manager** (endpoint least
privilege), **Server PAM** (server privilege and AD bridging), **DevOps Secrets Vault** (machine secrets),
**Account Lifecycle Manager** (service-account governance), and **the Delinea Platform and identity security**
(ITDR, ISPM, analytics). Chapter 09 closes on choosing a path.

A theme runs through it: **eliminate standing privilege** across humans, servers, machines, and service
accounts — then **govern** and **detect**, because prevention is necessary but not sufficient.

## The certifications

Three **Security Academy** tiers, awarding digital badges + printable certificates:

| Tier | For | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Associate** | Building technical understanding toward Engineer | Self-paced, online-only: e-learning + online exam |
| **Engineer** | Install, configure, manage to best practice | Hands-on lab challenges assessed by a live Delinea expert (incl. break-fix) |
| **Consultant** | Customizations, integrations, extensibility | Coursework + online exam + hands-on labs — **by invitation only** |

Free training via **Delinea University**; **NFR license keys** by request for hands-on practice.

## What you will be able to do

- Read the Security Academy tiers and understand the expert-assessed Engineer model.
- Explain PAM and the Thycotic + Centrify heritage unified under the Delinea Platform.
- Operate Secret Server — policy-driven vaulting, rotation, session recording, keystroke logging.
- Apply endpoint least privilege with Privilege Manager (remove local admin, elevate the app not the user).
- Apply server privilege with Server PAM — granular PEDM, AD bridging, MFA at the server.
- Secure machine identities with DevOps Secrets Vault — short-lived, API-retrieved secrets.
- Govern service accounts across their lifecycle with Account Lifecycle Manager.
- Extend PAM into identity security — Privileged Behavior Analytics, ITDR, and ISPM on the unified platform.

## Prerequisites

- Familiarity with identity, authentication, and systems administration helps.
- A Linux or macOS host with `python3`. **Delinea training** is free via Delinea University; certifications via the Security Academy.
