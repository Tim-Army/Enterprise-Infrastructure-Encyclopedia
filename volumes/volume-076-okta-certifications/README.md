# Volume LXXVI — Okta Certification Tracks

> The whole Okta certification program in one volume — the Professional → Administrator → Consultant
> → Technical Architect ladder plus Developer, Workflows, Access Gateway, and Auth0 — across the
> Workforce and Customer identity clouds, with hands-on, defensive labs, verified against okta.com.

## Overview

Volume LXXVI maps the **Okta** certification program — the credentials for deploying and operating
Okta's **identity and access management (IAM)** platform across the **Workforce Identity Cloud (WIC)**
and the **Customer Identity Cloud (CIC, built on Auth0)**. The role ladder runs **Certified
Professional → Administrator → Consultant → Technical Architect**, with **Certified Developer**,
specialties (**Workflows**, **Access Gateway**), and the **Auth0 Certified Developer**. Exams are
**proctored via ProctorU** and valid **two years** (the Technical Architect is three, with a
board-defense component). It joins the encyclopedia's security volumes (ISC2 XL, CyberArk LXXVII,
Zscaler XXXV, Microsoft Entra XXXVIII, Enterprise Cybersecurity X).

This is a **certification-tracks** volume, like the other vendor volumes (XXXIX–LXXV): it maps the
program — the ladder and specialties — and teaches each with a hands-on walkthrough. Every credential
and platform area was **verified against okta.com on 28 July 2026**.

Chapters follow the ladder:

- **Chapter 01** frames the program — WIC and CIC/Auth0, the ladder, and the ProctorU exam model.
- **Chapter 02** takes the **Certified Professional** core (Universal Directory, users/groups, SSO basics).
- **Chapters 03–04** take the **Certified Administrator**: SSO/app integration, then security (MFA and policies).
- **Chapter 05** takes **Lifecycle Management and Universal Directory** (provisioning, SCIM).
- **Chapter 06** takes the **Certified Consultant** and **API Access Management** (OAuth scopes).
- **Chapter 07** takes the **Developer** and **Auth0** customer identity (OIDC flows, Actions).
- **Chapter 08** takes **Workflows, Access Gateway, and Identity Governance**.
- **Chapter 09** takes the **Technical Architect**, plus currency and career.

Every chapter follows the standard structure defined in
[templates/chapter.md](../../templates/chapter.md) and enforced by
[EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md](../../EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md), including per-topic hands-on labs and
knowledge checks.

> **Scope.** Identity and access management is a defensive discipline. Every lab is **authorized
> administration** — configuring directories, SSO, MFA, policies, lifecycle, and governance to
> protect access — never an attack on an identity system.
