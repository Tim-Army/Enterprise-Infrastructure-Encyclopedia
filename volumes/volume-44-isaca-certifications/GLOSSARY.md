# Volume XLIV Glossary

Definitions for terms used in **Volume XLIV — ISACA Certification Tracks**,
alphabetized. See also the [volume index](INDEX.md) and the
[master glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md) for cross-volume terminology.

**AAIA (Advanced in AI Audit)** — ISACA's AI-audit credential extending CISA;
three domains led by AI Governance & Risk. Used in Chapter 08.

**AAIR (Advanced in AI Risk)** — ISACA's AI-risk credential extending CRISC
(launched 2026); three domains across governance, program, and lifecycle. Used in
Chapter 08.

**AAISM (Advanced in AI Security Management)** — ISACA's AI-security-management
credential extending CISM; three domains weighted 31/31/38. Used in Chapter 08.

**CCOA (Certified Cybersecurity Operations Analyst)** — ISACA's hands-on SOC-analyst
credential; a hybrid exam with performance-based labs across five domains. Used in
Chapter 07.

**CDPSE (Certified Data Privacy Solutions Engineer)** — ISACA's privacy-engineering
credential; four domains (June 2025 refresh) led by Privacy Engineering. Used in
Chapter 06.

**CGEIT (Certified in the Governance of Enterprise IT)** — ISACA's enterprise
IT-governance credential; four domains led by Governance of Enterprise IT. Used in
Chapter 05.

**CISA (Certified Information Systems Auditor)** — ISACA's benchmark IS-audit
credential; five domains. Used in Chapter 02.

**CISM (Certified Information Security Manager)** — ISACA's security-management
credential; four domains led by Program and Incident Management. Used in Chapter
03.

**CMMC roles** — The ISACA-administered Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification
credentials: CCP (Professional), CCA (Assessor), LCCA (Lead Assessor), CCI
(Instructor). Used in Chapter 09.

**COBIT** — ISACA's framework for the governance and management of enterprise IT,
underpinning CGEIT and much of the governance material. Used in Chapters 01, 05,
and 09.

**CPE (Continuing Professional Education)** — Credits earned to maintain an ISACA
certification over its three-year cycle, with an annual minimum. Used in Chapters
01 and 09.

**CRISC (Certified in Risk and Information Systems Control)** — ISACA's IT-risk
credential; four domains (2025 update) led by Risk Response and Reporting. Used in
Chapter 04.

**Exam content outline** — ISACA's published blueprint for a certification, listing
its weighted job-practice domains. Used in Chapters 01 and 09.

**Job-practice domain** — A weighted area of an ISACA exam derived from a
job-practice analysis of the role. Used throughout.

**KRI (Key Risk Indicator)** — A metric with a threshold that signals changing
risk, distinct from a performance KPI. Used in Chapter 04.

**Pseudonymization** — Replacing identifiers with reversible tokens (via a key or
mapping), as opposed to irreversible anonymization; a CDPSE privacy-engineering
control. Used in Chapter 06.

**Risk appetite** — The amount and type of risk an organization is willing to
accept in pursuit of objectives; may vary by risk category. Used in Chapter 04.

**RTO / RPO** — Recovery Time Objective (max tolerable downtime) and Recovery Point
Objective (max tolerable data loss), assessed in resilience audits. Used in Chapter
02.
