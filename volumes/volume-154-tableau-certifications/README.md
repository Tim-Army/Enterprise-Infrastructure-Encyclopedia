# Volume CLIV — Tableau Certification Tracks

> The Tableau certification program — verified 5 August 2026 (Tableau/Salesforce). Tableau is owned by **Salesforce**,
> and its certifications form a three-step track: **Tableau Desktop Specialist** (a.k.a. Desktop Foundations —
> entry-level; a **proctored** exam of **45 questions / 60 minutes / 750 to pass / ~$75**, validating connecting to
> and preparing data and basic visualization), the **Salesforce Certified Tableau Data Analyst** (mid-level and most
> recognized; **40–45 questions plus 8–10 hands-on labs / 120 minutes**, validating visualization and analysis
> proficiency), and the **Salesforce Certified Tableau Architect** (advanced; enterprise deployment, Server/Cloud
> architecture, and governance). The recommended progression runs Desktop Specialist → Certified Data Analyst →
> Certified Architect. Tableau publishes its exam mechanics. Every lab runs free in Python; Tableau Public and a free
> trial make the practice real at no cost.

## Overview

Tableau is the leading **data visualization and business-intelligence** platform — helping people **"see and understand
their data"** by turning tables of numbers into interactive visualizations. Where the
[Snowflake (XLIX)](../volume-049-snowflake-certifications/README.md) and
[Databricks (XLVIII)](../volume-048-databricks-certifications/README.md) volumes cover the data *platforms* that store
and process data, **Tableau is the analytics and visualization layer on top** — where humans explore data and make
decisions. Tableau has been owned by Salesforce since 2019.

Chapter 02 covers **what data visualization is** — why pictures reveal what tables hide. Chapter 03 covers
**connecting and preparing data** — live vs extract, and the data model. Chapter 04 covers **dimensions, measures,
and the grammar** — discrete vs continuous and VizQL. Chapter 05 covers **building visualizations** — the right chart
for the question. Chapter 06 covers **calculations** — calculated fields, LOD, and table calcs. Chapter 07 covers
**dashboards and interactivity**. Chapter 08 covers **sharing, governance, and the Server/Cloud**. Chapter 09 closes
on choosing a path.

A theme runs through it: **turn data into understanding** — the right chart, honest encodings, real analysis, and
governed, trusted data, so numbers become decisions.

## The certification track

| Certification | Level | Mechanics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tableau Desktop Specialist** | Entry | 45 Q / 60 min / 750 / ~$75 / proctored |
| **Salesforce Certified Tableau Data Analyst** | Mid | 40–45 Q + 8–10 hands-on labs / 120 min |
| **Salesforce Certified Tableau Architect** | Advanced | Enterprise deploy, Server/Cloud, governance |

## What you will be able to do

- Read the three-step track and sequence Desktop Specialist → Data Analyst → Architect by role.
- Explain why visualization reveals patterns tables hide, and choose perceptually-accurate encodings.
- Choose live vs extract connections and model data with relationships (avoiding join duplication).
- Apply the dimension/measure and discrete/continuous grammar, and understand VizQL.
- Choose the right chart for each analytical question.
- Write calculated fields, LOD expressions (FIXED/INCLUDE/EXCLUDE), and table calculations.
- Build interactive dashboards with actions, and stories for guided narrative.
- Publish and govern with certified data sources as a single source of truth.

## Prerequisites

- Familiarity with data and basic analysis helps; SQL knowledge deepens it.
- A Linux or macOS host with `python3`. **Tableau Public** and a **free trial** make hands-on practice cost-free.
