# Volume LXXIV — GIAC (SANS) Certification Tracks

> The whole GIAC certification program in one volume — the eight focus areas (Essentials, Cyber
> Defense, Offensive Operations, DFIR, Cloud, ICS, Leadership, and AI) across 60-plus SANS-aligned,
> CyberLive-tested credentials — with hands-on, defensive labs, verified against giac.org.

## Overview

Volume LXXIV maps the **GIAC (Global Information Assurance Certification)** program — the
**SANS Institute's** technical certification portfolio, among the most hands-on in the industry.
GIAC offers **60-plus** certifications organized into **eight focus areas**, delivered as
**proctored, open-book** exams, many with **CyberLive** hands-on practical testing, and valid for
**four years**. It joins the encyclopedia's security volumes (OffSec XLIII, ISC2 XL, ISACA XLIV,
CompTIA XXXIX, Check Point LXXIII, Enterprise Cybersecurity X).

This is a **certification-tracks** volume, like the other vendor volumes (XXXIX–LXXIII): it maps the
program — the focus areas and their credentials — and teaches each with a hands-on walkthrough. Every
focus area and code was **verified against giac.org on 28 July 2026**, including the recent
Artificial Intelligence additions (GMLE, GAIPS, GASAE, GOAA) and new entries such as GISF, GSOA,
GLIR, and GCTD.

Chapters follow the focus areas:

- **Chapter 01** frames the program — SANS, the eight focus areas, CyberLive, and currency.
- **Chapter 02** takes **Cybersecurity & IT Essentials** (GFACT, GISF, GSEC).
- **Chapter 03** takes **Cyber Defense** — the blue team (GCIA, GMON, GCDA, GDSA, GDAT).
- **Chapter 04** takes **Offensive Operations** as authorized methodology (GPEN, GWAPT, GXPN, GMOB, GEVA).
- **Chapter 05** takes **Digital Forensics and Incident Response** (GCIH, GCFA, GNFA, GREM, GCTI).
- **Chapter 06** takes **Cloud Security** (GCLD, GCSA, GCFR, GWEB).
- **Chapter 07** takes **Industrial Control Systems Security** (GICSP, GRID, GCIP).
- **Chapter 08** takes **Cybersecurity Leadership** (GSLC, GSTRT, GSOM, GCCC).
- **Chapter 09** takes **Artificial Intelligence** and currency/career (GMLE, GAIPS, GASAE, GOAA).

Every chapter follows the standard structure defined in
[templates/chapter.md](../../templates/chapter.md) and enforced by
[EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md](../../EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md), including per-topic hands-on labs and
knowledge checks.

> **Scope.** Every lab is **defensive or authorized** — detection, monitoring, forensics, incident
> response, hardening, and *authorized* assessment methodology (scope, rules of engagement, safe
> local commands). The Offensive Operations and offensive-AI material is authorized/educational
> methodology only — never an operational attack.
