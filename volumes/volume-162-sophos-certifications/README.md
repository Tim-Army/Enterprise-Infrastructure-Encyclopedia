## Overview

Sophos is a **cybersecurity vendor** spanning **endpoint protection**, **network security (firewall)**, and
**managed detection and response (MDR)**, unified under **Sophos Central**. Its signature idea is
**Synchronized Security** — products that share threat intelligence and respond together. Sophos is a defensive
peer of the endpoint vendors ([SentinelOne CLI](../volume-151-sentinelone-certifications/README.md),
[CrowdStrike L](../volume-050-crowdstrike-certifications/README.md)) and firewall vendors
([Fortinet XIX](../volume-019-fortinet-network-security/README.md),
[Check Point LXXIII](../volume-073-check-point-certifications/README.md)) this shelf covers.

Chapter 02 covers **Sophos Central** (the unified console). Chapters 03–08 take the platform: **Intercept X**
(endpoint protection and EDR), **CryptoGuard and ransomware defense**, **Sophos Firewall** (NGFW),
**Synchronized Security** (the Security Heartbeat), **Sophos MDR and XDR**, and **operating Sophos**
(administration and deployment). Chapter 09 closes on choosing a path.

A theme runs through it: **behavior-based, defense-in-depth security** — detect the file, block the technique,
stop the encryption, inspect the network — with products that **work together** (Synchronized Security) and a
**24/7 managed SOC** (MDR) for what automation misses.

## The certifications

Four **Sophos Academy** role tiers, earned per product:

| Tier | Validates |
| --- | --- |
| **Technician** | Support and troubleshooting |
| **Administrator** | Day-to-day administration and operations |
| **Engineer** | Configuration and demonstration |
| **Architect** | Deployment and design at scale (e.g., AT15 — Central Endpoint, Intercept X & Server) |

Per-product (Firewall, Central Endpoint/Intercept X & Server, MDR, and more); instructor-led or self-study eLearning; free foundational training.

## What you will be able to do

- Read the role-plus-product program and match a tier to your role.
- Manage the Sophos estate from Sophos Central, the unified cloud console.
- Apply Intercept X endpoint protection — Deep Learning detection, Exploit Prevention, and EDR.
- Defend against ransomware with CryptoGuard (behavior detection and rollback), WipeGuard, and Adaptive Attack Protection.
- Operate Sophos Firewall — TLS inspection, IPS, web/app control, and the Xstream architecture.
- Apply Synchronized Security — the Security Heartbeat and automatic isolation to stop lateral movement.
- Use Sophos MDR/XDR — 24/7 managed detection and response with cross-domain correlation and threat hunting.
- Design and deploy Sophos at scale — segmented policy, update caches, message relays, and AD sync.

## Prerequisites

- Familiarity with endpoint and network security concepts helps.
- A Linux or macOS host with `python3`. **Sophos training** is free (foundational) via Sophos Academy; certifications validate role skill.
