# Volume LXXXVIII — MongoDB Certification Tracks

> The whole MongoDB certification program in one volume — the Associate Developer, Data Modeler, Atlas
> Administrator, and Database Administrator — across the document database, the aggregation framework,
> data modeling, replication and sharding, and MongoDB Atlas, with hands-on `mongosh` labs, verified
> against mongodb.com.

## Overview

Volume LXXXVIII maps the **MongoDB** certification program — the credentials for building on and
operating the leading **document database**. MongoDB University offers four **Associate** certifications:
the **Associate Developer** (building applications with MongoDB and a driver), the **Associate Data
Modeler** (schema design), the **Associate Atlas Administrator** (MongoDB **Atlas**, the managed cloud
service), and the **Associate Database Administrator** (building, supporting, and securing self-managed
MongoDB). This volume continues the encyclopedia's Data, storage & backup cluster.

This is a **certification-tracks** volume, like the other vendor volumes (XXXIX–LXXXVI): it maps the
program — the credentials and the exam objectives — and teaches each with a hands-on `mongosh`
walkthrough. Every certification and exam detail was **verified against mongodb.com and MongoDB
University on 29 July 2026** (the certification-program hub and the individual exam pages; third-party
exam-dump sites were excluded as sources).

Chapters follow the certification ladder:

- **Chapter 01** frames the program — the four Associate certifications, exam formats, ProctorU delivery, Credly badges, and free MongoDB University training with Learning Path discounts.
- **Chapter 02** covers the **document model and CRUD** — BSON documents, collections, and `mongosh`.
- **Chapter 03** takes the **Associate Developer** — querying and drivers.
- **Chapter 04** takes the **Associate Developer** — the aggregation pipeline.
- **Chapter 05** takes the **Associate Data Modeler** — schema design, embedding versus referencing, and patterns.
- **Chapter 06** takes the **Associate DBA** — indexes and performance.
- **Chapter 07** takes the **Associate DBA** — replication and sharding.
- **Chapter 08** takes the **Associate Atlas Administrator** — clusters, backup, monitoring, and security.
- **Chapter 09** covers **security, prep, and career**.

Every chapter follows the standard structure defined in
[templates/chapter.md](../../templates/chapter.md) and enforced by
[EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md](../../EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md), including per-topic hands-on labs and
knowledge checks.

> **Scope.** MongoDB development and administration are authorized platform work — building on,
> modeling, operating, and securing your own databases. The security material (authentication, RBAC,
> TLS, encryption, network isolation) is framed as defensive protection of your own data.
