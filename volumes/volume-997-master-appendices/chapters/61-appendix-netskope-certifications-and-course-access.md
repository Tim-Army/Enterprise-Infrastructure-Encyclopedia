# Chapter 61: Appendix — Netskope Certifications and Course Access

The **Netskope** certification program — the SASE/SSE credentials, their sequence, and the
training/access model. Verified on **3 August 2026** from **netskope.com** (the certification program,
training, and SASE Accreditation pages) and Pearson VUE, the sources that anchor
[Volume CXXVII — Netskope Certification Tracks](../../volume-127-netskope-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** The **SASE Accreditation** is a **free** (limited-time), on-demand,
**vendor-agnostic** SASE-architecture course with an optional online exam (45 minutes, two attempts,
80% to pass) yielding a certificate and LinkedIn badge — register on netskope.com. The platform
certifications are earned through **Netskope Academy** (customer training) with recommended courses —
**Netskope One Administrator** (for **NCCSA**) and **Netskope One Professional** (for **NCCSI**) — and
the exams are delivered by **Pearson VUE**. Partner training runs through the Partner Portal.

> **Currency.** Exam codes churn — **NSK100 was replaced by NSK101** for the NCCSA — and the SASE
> Accreditation's free window is time-limited; the platform rebranded to **Netskope One** (formerly
> Netskope Security Cloud). Re-verify the current exam codes, the free-accreditation availability, and
> validity periods on netskope.com before booking. **NCCSA is valid 2 years.**

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[Netskope certification program](https://www.netskope.com/certification-program)** — the
  authoritative certification page
- **[Netskope Training](https://www.netskope.com/training)** — customer and partner training (Netskope
  Academy login)
- **[The SASE Accreditation](https://www.netskope.com/sase-accreditation)** — the free, vendor-agnostic
  SASE course + exam
- **Netskope Community** — Q&A and study discussion
- **Free study lab:** any Linux host with `squid`/`nginx`, `nftables`, and `python3` models the SSE
  concepts (steering, DLP, ZTNA broker) at no cost — see the volume's labs

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** the **SASE Accreditation is free** (limited time). NCCSA and NCCSI exams are delivered by
  **Pearson VUE** (paid); the Netskope One Administrator/Professional courses are Netskope Academy
  offerings — confirm current pricing on netskope.com.
- **Delivery:** SASE Accreditation is on-demand self-paced with a 45-minute online exam (80% pass, two
  attempts). NCCSA is a Pearson VUE proctored exam (**NSK101**, ~70 questions, ~2 hours, 70% pass).
  NCCSI is a Pearson VUE exam on the integration curriculum.
- **Prerequisites:** none formal for the SASE Accreditation (security/network/architecture basics
  recommended); the Netskope One Administrator course is recommended for NCCSA; NCCSI assumes
  NCCSA-level knowledge plus the Professional course.
- **Validity/renewal:** **NCCSA is valid 2 years**; confirm NCCSI's validity on netskope.com.

## The certifications

Verified against netskope.com on 3 August 2026.

| Credential | Exam / format | Prerequisite | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SASE Accreditation | On-demand course + optional exam (45 min, 80% pass, 2 attempts); **FREE** (limited time) | Security/network/architecture basics | Vendor-agnostic SASE architecture: origin, Zero Trust, SD-WAN, SSE, deployment |
| NCCSA (Certified Cloud Security Administrator) | **NSK101** (replaced NSK100), Pearson VUE, ~70 Q, ~2 hr, 70% pass, **valid 2 yr** | Netskope One Administrator course recommended | Configure, monitor, and troubleshoot the Netskope platform: steering, CASB, SWG, DLP, ZTNA |

  it is a useful first credential regardless of which SASE/SSE vendor you deploy.

  Cloud." Course names track the current branding.
