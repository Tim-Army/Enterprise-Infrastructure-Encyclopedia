# Chapter 49: Appendix — Veeam Certifications and Course Access

The **Veeam** certification program — the credentials for protecting, recovering, and securing data
with the **Veeam Data Platform** — organized by credential, with the exam model, the required-training
model, and the recertification model. The program was verified on **29 July 2026** from **veeam.com**
(the VMCE certification and Veeam University Pro pages) and the official **Veeam Community Resource
Hub** — the same sources that anchor
[Volume LXXXV — Veeam Certification Tracks](../../volume-85-veeam-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** Veeam's training is delivered through a **Veeam University Pro** subscription,
sold through the **Veeam channel and Authorized Education Centers** (not directly from Veeam).
**Attending the required trainings is a hard prerequisite** to sit the exam. Exams are delivered
**proctored through Pearson VUE (Pearson Professional Assessments)**, and passing earns a **Credly**
digital badge. Hands-on practice uses the **free Veeam Backup & Replication Community Edition**, which
runs real jobs and the Veeam PowerShell module without a license. Certifications carry a validity period
and are re-earned by passing the current version; because Veeam ties exams to product versions (VMCE+ is
aligned to **v13**), plan to re-certify as the platform advances.

> **Currency.** The program was rebuilt for 2026: **VMCA** retired **30 November 2025**, the **VMCE**
> exam retires **31 March 2026**, the new **VMCE+** flagship is **live**, and **VMCSE** arrives **Q2
> 2026**. VMCE holders should migrate to VMCE+; confirm the catalog on veeam.com.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[Veeam Certification (VMCE)](https://www.veeam.com/support/training/vmce-certification.html)** —
  the authoritative certification and exam information
- **[Veeam University Pro](https://www.veeam.com/support/training/veeam-university-pro.html)** — the
  subscription delivering the required trainings for VMCE+ and VMCSE
- **[Veeam Community Resource Hub](https://community.veeam.com/)** — official program updates,
  study halls, and announcements
- **Veeam Backup & Replication Community Edition** — a **free** full-function server for hands-on
  practice and PowerShell automation
- **Veeam Training and Certification FAQ** — exam policy, validity, and delivery details

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** the Veeam University Pro subscription (via the channel) plus the per-exam fee at Pearson
  VUE; Community Edition practice is **free**. Confirm current pricing through a Veeam partner.
- **Delivery:** **Pearson VUE** proctored; VMCE+ is **100 multiple-choice questions in 150 minutes**
  (plus 30 minutes if English is not your first language).
- **Prerequisites:** **required trainings** are a hard prerequisite for VMCE+ (three courses) and VMCSE
  (valid VMCE+ plus the Enterprise Data Security training).
- **Validity and renewal:** re-certify by passing the current, product-version-aligned exam (VMCE+ = v13).

## Notes

- **Training is required.** You must complete the Veeam University Pro trainings before the exam.
- **Free Community Edition.** All hands-on practice and PowerShell automation is available at no cost.
- **v13 alignment.** Study the current VMCE+ (v13) material, not retired VMCE/v12 content.
- **Defensive framing.** Backup, replication, recovery, immutability, and ransomware detection protect
  and recover your own data; the VMCSE security content is defensive.
- **Related coverage** in the encyclopedia: NetApp (**LXXXIV**) for storage-side data protection, and
  VMware (**V**, **LXXI**, **LXXII**) for the virtualization platforms Veeam most often protects.
