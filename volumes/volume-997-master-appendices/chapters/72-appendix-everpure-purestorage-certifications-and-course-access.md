The **Everpure** certification program — levels, exams, and access. Verified on **4 August 2026** from
**academy.purestorage.com** and **everpuredata.com**, the sources that anchor [Volume CXXXVIII — Everpure
(formerly Pure Storage) Certification

>   **`academy.purestorage.com`**. An old-looking URL that is entirely current.

**How access works.** **IT Professional Certifications** run through **Everpure Academy**. You prepare
using study guides and prep training, schedule the exam, take it online under proctoring, review your
results, and claim a **Credly** badge. Training is **not required** — Everpure states that "each exam is
designed to test your **on-the-job experience**." A separate **Partner Sales Certification** track exists
for partners.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[Everpure Academy — IT Professional Certifications](https://academy.purestorage.com/student/activity/2133577-it-certifications)** — the authoritative certification page
- **[Everpure Academy catalog](https://academy.purestorage.com/student/catalog)** — training and education
- **Study Guides** and **Certification Prep Training** — per-exam preparation
- **Everpure PEAK Customer Course Catalog** — on-demand, instructor-led, and certification-prep training
- **Certification Experience Guide** — preparing, scheduling, claiming badges, and maintaining certification
- **Everpure Knowledge Portal** and the Everpure Community
- **Free study lab:** any host with `python3` models the examinable disciplines — non-disruptive upgrade sequencing and controller headroom, volume/host mapping, file-versus-object selection, data-reduction arithmetic, replication RPO, immutability against dwell time, and Kubernetes persistent volumes (see the volume's labs); no Everpure hardware needed

## Levels, pricing, and mechanics

| Level | Price | Certifications |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Associate** | **$200** | Data Storage (DSA) |
| **Professional** | **$300** | FlashArray Storage · FlashBlade Storage · Portworx Enterprise · Cyber Resilience |
| **Specialist** | **$300** | FlashArray Implementation · FlashBlade Implementation · FlashArray Support · FlashBlade Support · Cloud · Migration |
| **Expert** | **$400** | Platform Architect |

- **Format:** multiple choice, delivered **online and proctored** — a webcam is required and you are monitored for the duration.
- **Open book?** **No.** External materials are not permitted.
- **Training required?** **No** — but prep resources are provided, and the exams target hands-on experience.
- **Results:** available after the exam; **badges via Credly** by email.
- **Pricing is confirmed in the exam registration storefront before checkout.**

## Recertification

All Everpure certifications are **valid for three years** and must be renewed before expiry. Renewal
options:

1. **Retake the updated exam** — always available.
2. **Automatic renewal of the Associate (DSA)** — earning any **Professional, Specialist, or Expert**
   certification renews it. Sitting the Associate first therefore costs $200 once and maintains itself
   for anyone progressing.
3. **Continuing Everpure Education (CEE) credits** — available for **select FlashArray exams** only.
   Check the individual exam page rather than assuming it applies.

Exam issues and accommodation requests go through the **Everpure Certification Exam Support &
Accommodation Request Form**.

## Notes

- **Sit the Associate first.** It is the cheapest credential, covers the foundations the others assume,
  and renews itself as you progress — the single most useful planning fact in this program.
- **Implementation and Support are deliberately separate** Specialist certifications for the same
  products. Deploying a system and diagnosing a misbehaving one are different jobs; hold the one that
  matches your work.
- **Cyber Resilience is a Professional-level certification in its own right**, reflecting how far
  primary storage has moved into ransomware defense — converging with
  [Rubrik CXXX](../../volume-130-rubrik-certifications/README.md),
  [Commvault CXXXIII](../../volume-133-commvault-certifications/README.md), and
