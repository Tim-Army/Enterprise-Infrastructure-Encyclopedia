# Chapter 70: Appendix — GitLab Certifications and Course Access

The **GitLab** certification program — certifications, exam mechanics, free learning paths, and access.
Verified on **4 August 2026** from **university.gitlab.com** and GitLab's official **Candidate Handbook &
Exam Guide (v1.1, 2025)**, the sources that anchor [Volume CXXXVI — GitLab Certification
Tracks](../../volume-136-gitlab-certifications/README.md).

**How access works.** Certification runs in **GitLab University**. You register, choose a certification,
pay the fee, and gain immediate access for unproctored (Associate) exams or schedule through
**Certiverse** for proctored (Professional) exams. Every exam has **free, self-paced learning content**
with interactive elements and practice opportunities. Badges issue through **Credly**, together with a
certificate carrying a verification code.

GitLab publishes its exam mechanics unusually openly — duration, question counts, passing score, retake
rules, and accommodation policy are all stated in the handbook rather than left to inference.

> **Currency — a different model.** **GitLab certifications do not expire.** They are governed by
> **GitLab product versioning** rather than time-based expiration. Recertification may be required when a
> major product version significantly changes the skills a certification validates; GitLab commits to
> advance notice, multiple recertification options, and existing certifications remaining valid during
> transition periods. Practically: there is **no renewal date to diary** — track GitLab's **major
> releases** instead. Contrast the time-based cycles of
> [Confluent CXXXV](../../volume-135-confluent-certifications/README.md) and
> [SailPoint CXXXII](../../volume-132-sailpoint-certifications/README.md) (two years) and
> [SolarWinds CXXXIV](../../volume-134-solarwinds-certifications/README.md) (three years).

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[GitLab University](https://university.gitlab.com/)** — the training and certification hub
- **[Certifications catalog](https://university.gitlab.com/pages/certifications)** — every exam with its scope
- **[Candidate Handbook & Exam Guide](https://university.gitlab.com/learn/article/gitlab-certification-candidate-handbook)** — the authoritative policy document
- **Free self-paced learning paths:** GitLab Fundamentals · GitLab CI Fundamentals · Agile Portfolio Management · GitLab Security Essentials · GitLab Duo
- **GitLab itself is free to try:** GitLab.com has a free tier and self-managed Community Edition costs nothing
- **Free study lab:** any host with `python3` models the examinable concepts — pipeline DAG scheduling, `rules` first-match evaluation, artifact-versus-cache classification, scanner findings triage, approval-policy gates, agent tool permissions, and runner and cache sizing (see the volume's labs)

## Fees, format, and policies

| | **Associate** | **Professional** (defined) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fee | **$150 USD** | **$150 USD** |
| Duration | **75 minutes** | 90 minutes |
| Questions | **50** | 60 |
| Question types | Multiple choice, multiple select | Multiple choice, multiple select |
| Delivery | **Unproctored online** | **Proctored online** (Certiverse) |
| Prerequisites | None | Associate recommended |
| Recommended experience | 3–6 months hands-on GitLab | 1–2 years professional development or DevSecOps |

- **Passing score: 75%** for all levels, criterion-referenced, counting scored questions only.
- **Access period: 14 days** from purchase, with **automatic unenrollment** afterward regardless of completion — the clock starts when you pay, so buy when you can sit it.
- **Attempts: 2 per 14-day window.** Failing both means waiting out the window before repurchasing; purchases are unlimited but **retakes are full price with no discount**.
- **Results immediately** on completion, then Credly badge and certificate.
- **Requirements:** government photo ID (proctored exams), agreement to the Terms & Conditions, fee payment, and a valid email for badge delivery.
- **Prohibited during exams:** mobile phones, smartwatches and electronic devices; reference materials, notes, and books; other people in the testing area — and explicitly **"using artificial intelligence or automated tools."**
- **Accommodations:** email `university@gitlab.com` with documentation from a qualified professional; allow **5 business days** for review. Note the 14-day access window begins on the **approval date**.
- **Corporate vouchers** (365-day validity) and **partner pricing** are available.
- **Support:** `university@gitlab.com`, 2 business days, Monday–Friday 8 AM–5 PM PST.

## The certifications

Verified against the GitLab University catalog on 4 August 2026. All currently published certifications
are **Associate** level.

| Certification | Validates |
| --- | --- |
| **Certified Fundamentals Associate** | Core GitLab workflows for merge requests and issues, basic CI/CD pipelines, security scanning setup, and agile planning across the SDLC. **GitLab recommends taking this first.** |
| **Certified CI/CD Associate** | Defining CI/CD, identifying pipeline components, and using GitLab's CI/CD functions |
| **Certified Agile Portfolio Management Associate** | Organizational structure, issue lifecycles with labels and dependencies, boards, epics and roadmaps, and team velocity |
| **Certified Security Associate** | SAST, DAST, secret detection, dependency and container scanning; security policies and merge-request approval workflows; license compliance |
| **Certified GitLab Duo Agent Platform Associate** *(new)* | Agentic chat and agent selection, configuring and publishing custom agents and flows, connecting external tools via **MCP**, and AI-assisted code creation, review, and security workflows |

## Notes

- **Work the free learning path before buying.** Exams are $150 with no discounted retake, and the paths
  cover the same material at no cost — there is no scenario where skipping them is economical.
- **The 14-day window is the common expensive mistake:** enrollment expires whether or not you sat the
  exam, so purchasing to create commitment can simply forfeit the fee.
- **Associate exams are unproctored**, resting on the Code of Conduct rather than surveillance;
  violations carry score invalidation, certification revocation, and program bans.
- **A pointed detail:** the Code of Conduct prohibits AI tools during exams — including for the
  certification that is itself about operating AI agents.
- **Position in the encyclopedia:** GitLab is the **integrated single-application** counterpart to
  [GitHub LXXXIX](../../volume-089-github-certifications/README.md), alongside
  [Docker XCII](../../volume-092-docker-certifications/README.md),
  [CNCF/Kubernetes XLI](../../volume-041-cncf-kubernetes-certifications/README.md), and
  [HashiCorp XLII](../../volume-042-hashicorp-certifications/README.md).
