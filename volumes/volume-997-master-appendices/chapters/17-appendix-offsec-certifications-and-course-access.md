# Chapter 17: Appendix — OffSec Certifications and Course Access

The **OffSec** (Offensive Security) certification program — the hands-on,
practical security credentials — organized by discipline, with each course's
certification, exam format, and the "+" renewal model. The lineup and course
codes were harvested on **26 July 2026** from **offsec.com/courses** — the same
source that anchors
[Volume XLIII — Offensive Security (OffSec) Certification Tracks](../../volume-43-offensive-security-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources, and this appendix records
certification facts only — it teaches nothing offensive.

**How access works at OffSec.** Training is delivered through **OffSec's Learn
subscriptions** (Learn One, Learn Unlimited, Learn Enterprise) bundling **courses,
lab time, and exam attempts**, or per-course. Each course earns a certification by
passing a **proctored practical exam** (many hours of hands-on work) followed by a
**professional report**. **Kali Linux** is the standard platform. Credentials
appear as verifiable badges.

> **Currency.** OffSec updates course codes and adds tracks — confirm the current
> course and status on offsec.com before registering. Recent changes captured
> here: **OSCP became OSCP+** (passing PEN-200 grants both the lifetime OSCP and
> the renewable three-year OSCP+), and OffSec added the **CyberCore (OSCC,
> SEC-100/SJD-100)** foundational track and the **AI Red Teamer (OSAI, AI-300)**.

## Free training and entry points

- **[OffSec Courses & Certifications](https://www.offsec.com/courses/)** — the
  authoritative catalog with course codes, credentials, and exam formats
- **OffSec Learn subscriptions** — Learn One / Unlimited / Enterprise (courses +
  lab time + exam attempts)
- **Proving Grounds / practice labs** — hands-on practice targets (authorized)
- **The OffSec code of conduct** — the ethics and authorization rules governing
  all practice

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fee model (confirm at registration):** OffSec sells **subscriptions** (Learn
  One/Unlimited/Enterprise) that bundle a course, lab time, and exam attempts,
  rather than a flat per-exam fee; individual courses/exams are also available.
  Confirm current pricing at offsec.com.
- **Delivery:** **proctored practical exams** — the candidate compromises or
  defends machines in a controlled lab over many hours (e.g., **~24 hours** for
  OSCP+), then submits a **professional report**. There are no multiple-choice
  domains.
- **Prerequisites:** recommended experience per course; the harder courses assume
  the fundamentals of the earlier ones (CKS-style hard prerequisites are rare, but
  OSEE assumes OSED-level skill).
- **Validity and renewal:** a **two-track model** — **OSCP+, OSCC, OSTH, OSIR,**
  and **OSAI+** are valid **three years** and renewed via the **OffSec CPE
  program**, a recertification exam, or another qualifying exam; **OSCP
  (lifetime), OSEP, OSWE, OSED,** and **OSEE** do **not** expire.

## Notes

- **Exams are practical, not multiple-choice.** Prepare by living in authorized
  labs and practicing report writing — the report is a graded part of the exam.
- **OSCP vs OSCP+.** Passing PEN-200 grants both; the lifetime **OSCP** never
  expires, while **OSCP+** is the renewable three-year version.
- **New tracks.** **CyberCore (OSCC)** and **AI Red Teamer (OSAI, AI-300)** are
  recent additions an older program map will miss.
- **Ethics and authorization are mandatory.** OffSec's code of conduct and the law
  require explicit authorization for any offensive activity; practice only against
  your own systems or enrolled OffSec labs.

  (Cybersecurity)**, vendor security in **Volumes XVI/XIX/XXV/XXXV**, and the

  **Volume XXXIX**.
