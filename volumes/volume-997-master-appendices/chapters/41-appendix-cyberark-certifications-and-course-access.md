# Chapter 41: Appendix — CyberArk Certifications and Course Access

The **CyberArk** certification program — the credentials for administering CyberArk's Privileged
Access Management and Identity Security platform — organized by the level progression and products,
with the exam model, the platform architecture, and the delivery and renewal model. The program was
verified on **28 July 2026** from **cyberark.com** and **Pearson VUE** — the same sources that anchor
[Volume LXXVII — CyberArk Certification Tracks](../../volume-77-cyberark-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** CyberArk delivers training through **CyberArk University** (self-paced and
instructor-led courses) and authorized partners, paired with the certifications. Exams are delivered
by **Pearson VUE** (proctored). Practice happens on **authorized CyberArk lab environments and
trials** — never a production Vault. The level progression runs **Trustee → Defender → Sentry →
Guardian**, with a **Certified Delivery Engineer (CDE)** track for implementation partners.

> **Currency.** As of 2026, CyberArk operates as **part of Palo Alto Networks**, and the platform is
> progressively rebranding toward **"Idira."** The component architecture (Vault, CPM, PVWA, PSM) and
> the **Defender/Sentry/Guardian** credential names are **unchanged**. Confirm current exam names,
> delivery (proctoring/in-person), and prerequisites on cyberark.com before registering.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[CyberArk Training & Certification](https://www.cyberark.com/services-support/training-certification/)**
  — the authoritative levels, products, and exam model

- **[CyberArk Docs](https://docs.cyberark.com/)** — product documentation and admin guides
- **CyberArk Community / Marketplace** — plugins, platforms, and discussion
- **Pearson VUE** — exam scheduling and delivery

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** per-exam fees via Pearson VUE; course fees via CyberArk University/partners. Confirm
  current pricing on cyberark.com.
- **Delivery:** proctored exams via **Pearson VUE** (verify current online/in-person options).
- **Prerequisites:** **Sentry** requires **Defender**; **Guardian** assumes advanced multi-solution
  experience.
- **Validity and renewal:** recertify per CyberArk policy as product versions evolve.

## The progression and products

Verified against cyberark.com on 28 July 2026.

| Level | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Trustee | Foundational privileged-access concepts and platform basics |
| Defender | Daily maintenance and operation (per product: PAM, EPM, Privilege Cloud) |
| Sentry | Deployment, installation, and configuration (prerequisite: Defender) |
| Guardian | Advanced multi-solution skills plus Identity Security architecture and strategy |
| CDE | Certified Delivery Engineer (implementation partners) |

## Platform areas

- **PAM Self-Hosted** — Vault, CPM (rotation), PVWA (UI/API), PSM (session isolation/recording), PTA (threat analytics).
- **Privilege Cloud** — SaaS PAM delivered via connectors.
- **Endpoint Privilege Manager (EPM)** — endpoint least privilege and application control.
- **Secrets Manager** — Conjur and Credential Providers for machine-identity secrets.
- **Identity** — workforce and customer identity (SSO, adaptive MFA).
- **Secure Cloud Access** — just-in-time, zero-standing-privilege cloud entitlements.
