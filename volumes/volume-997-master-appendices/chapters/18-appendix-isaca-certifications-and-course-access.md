# Chapter 18: Appendix — ISACA Certifications and Course Access

The **ISACA** certification program — the vendor-neutral audit, governance, and
risk credentials — organized by discipline, with each certification's weighted
job-practice domains, exam mechanics, and the CPE maintenance model. The lineup,
domains, and weights were harvested on **26 July 2026** from **isaca.org** — the
same source that anchors
[Volume XLIV — ISACA Certification Tracks](../../volume-044-isaca-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works at ISACA.** Official preparation is delivered through **ISACA
review manuals**, the **QAE** (Questions, Answers & Explanations) databases,
online review courses, and virtual instructor-led training. Free resources include
the downloadable **exam content outline** (the weighted job-practice domains) on
each credential page. Exams are delivered through **PSI** (test center or online
proctored). Certifications require **experience verification** after passing and
are maintained with **CPE** and an **annual maintenance fee**.

> **Currency.** ISACA updates job-practice outlines on a periodic cycle and has
> been adding credentials — confirm the current outline and its effective date on
> isaca.org before registering. Recent changes captured here: the hands-on **CCOA**
> analyst certification, the **Advanced in AI family** (AAIA, AAISM, AAIR), the
> **CDPSE** refresh from three to four domains (2 June 2025), and the **CRISC**
> update (2025).

## Free training and entry points

- **[ISACA Credentialing](https://www.isaca.org/credentialing)** — the
  authoritative credential pages, each linking an exam content outline
- **Exam content outlines** — free per credential; the definitive weighted domains
- **ISACA review manuals and QAE databases** — the official (paid) study materials
- **COBIT** and the **Fundamentals certificate** series — focused knowledge credentials
- **CPE policy** — the maintenance requirements and annual fee

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fee band (member vs non-member pricing; confirm at registration):** the
  certification exams (CISA, CISM, CRISC, CGEIT, CDPSE, CCOA) sit in a similar
  band with **member discounts**; the **Advanced in AI** exams and **certificates**
  are priced separately. ISACA **membership** reduces exam and maintenance costs.
- **Delivery:** **PSI**, test center or online-proctored. Most exams are
  **150 questions** (CDPSE 120; AAISM 90); **CCOA** is a **hybrid, 4-hour** exam
  with **performance-based labs**.
- **Requirements beyond the exam:** relevant **work experience** (verified after
  passing) is required to certify; waivers apply for some education/credentials.
- **Validity and renewal:** certifications are maintained on a **three-year CPE
  cycle** (an annual minimum plus a three-year total) with an **annual maintenance
  fee** — no re-exam. **Certificates** are standalone and do not require CPE.

## Notes

- **Certifications vs certificates.** Certifications (above) require experience and
  CPE; ISACA **certificates** (COBIT, Fundamentals series) are standalone knowledge
  credentials.
- **The exam content outline is the currency signal.** ISACA re-scopes on a
  job-practice cycle; confirm the current domains and effective date — CDPSE moved
  to four domains and CRISC was updated in 2025.
- **CCOA is hands-on.** Prepare with the lab practice sets, not reading alone.
- **The Advanced in AI family is new.** AAIA/AAISM/AAIR extend CISA/CISM/CRISC into
  AI governance, audit, security management, and risk.
- **Related practice** in the encyclopedia: ISC2 governance/architecture in
  **Volume XL**, general security in **Volume X**, and vendor-neutral security in
  **Volume XXXIX (CompTIA)**.
