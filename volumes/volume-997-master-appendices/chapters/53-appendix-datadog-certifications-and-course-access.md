# Chapter 53: Appendix — Datadog Certifications and Course Access

The **Datadog** certification program — the credentials for monitoring, tracing, logging, and securing
systems on the Datadog observability platform — organized by certification, with the exam model, the
training model, and the retake model. The program was verified on **29 July 2026** from
**datadoghq.com** and the **Datadog Learning Center** — the same sources that anchor
[Volume XC — Datadog Certification Tracks](../../volume-90-datadog-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** Datadog's training is **free** through the **Datadog Learning Center**
(learn.datadoghq.com) — self-paced courses, hands-on labs, and **practice exams** aligned to each
certification — paired with a **14-day free trial** to practice on a live account (plus the free Agent).
Datadog **relaunched** its certification program on a new learning platform. Each exam costs **US$100**
and has **90 multiple-choice questions**; you may retake it, limited to **three attempts within a
180-day** window from your first attempt.

> **Currency.** The lineup is Datadog Fundamentals, APM & Distributed Tracing Fundamentals, and Log
> Management Fundamentals, plus the newer Database Monitoring and Cloud SIEM for AWS certifications
> (some as pilot exams). Because the program was relaunched and evolves quickly, confirm the current
> lineup on datadoghq.com.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[Datadog Certification](https://www.datadoghq.com/certification/overview/)** — the authoritative
  certifications and exam details
- **[Datadog Learning Center](https://learn.datadoghq.com/)** — **free** courses, labs, and practice
  exams
- **Free 14-day trial** — a live Datadog account for hands-on practice
- **Free Datadog Agent** — install locally to practice Agent configuration and data collection
- **Practice exams** — per-certification, to gauge readiness

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** **US$100** per exam; training on the Learning Center is **free**. Confirm current pricing on
  datadoghq.com.
- **Delivery:** online; **90 multiple-choice questions**; **three attempts within 180 days** of the first.
- **Prerequisites:** none; Datadog Fundamentals is the recommended starting point.
- **Validity and renewal:** re-check the lineup and refresh skills as the relaunched platform evolves.

## The certifications

Verified against datadoghq.com on 29 July 2026.

| Certification | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Datadog Fundamentals | Agent, metrics, tags, data collection, and visualization (foundation) |
| APM & Distributed Tracing Fundamentals | Instrumentation, traces/spans, and troubleshooting with APM |
| Log Management Fundamentals | Logging and log management with Datadog |
| Database Monitoring Fundamentals | Query metrics and explain plans (newer/pilot) |
| Cloud SIEM for AWS Fundamentals | Log-based detection and security signals (newer/pilot) |

## Notes

- **Free Learning Center + trial.** All preparation and hands-on practice is available at no cost.
- **Start with Fundamentals.** It grounds the Agent, metrics, tags, and visualization the others build on.
