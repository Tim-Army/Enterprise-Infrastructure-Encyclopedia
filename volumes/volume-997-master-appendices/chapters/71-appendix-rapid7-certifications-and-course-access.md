# Chapter 71: Appendix — Rapid7 Certifications and Course Access

The **Rapid7** certification program — exams, Academy content, and access. Verified on **4 August 2026**
from **academy.rapid7.com** and **rapid7.com/services/training-certification**, the sources that anchor
[Volume CXXXVII — Rapid7 Certification Tracks](../../volume-137-rapid7-certifications/README.md).

**How access works.** Training and certification run through the **Rapid7 Academy**. Rapid7 product
certifications "demonstrate your knowledge of using our solutions as a cybersecurity professional," and
successful candidates receive an **electronic certificate** to post to LinkedIn.

> **Enrollment has an unusual step.** In Rapid7's own words: *"You must purchase certification exams via
> a purchase order. When you enroll for an exam below, you will be prompted for the promo code provided
> in your registration email."* This is **not** a card-and-go checkout. Individuals without a
> procurement relationship will generally find the **virtual instructor-led course route** more
> practical, since those courses **include one exam attempt**.
>
> **Currency.** Rapid7 does **not publish** exam duration, question counts, passing scores, validity
> periods, or retake policy on its Academy pages. This appendix therefore does not state them — confirm
> at enrollment via the Academy and your registration email, and treat third-party figures as unsourced.
> Note too that **InsightCloudSec** and **Metasploit** have Academy training but **no certification
> exam**; a product is not a certification.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[Rapid7 Academy](https://academy.rapid7.com/)** — the training and certification hub
- **[Certification exams](https://academy.rapid7.com/page/rapid7-certified-professional)** — the authoritative exam list
- **[Training calendar](https://academy.rapid7.com/calendar)** — scheduled sessions
- **[Training & certification overview](https://www.rapid7.com/services/training-certification/)** — course detail and CPE information
- **Exam preparation:** *InsightVM Certified Administrator Exam Preparation* and *InsightVM Exam Overview and Sample Questions*
- **On-demand training** and **product workshops** (one hour or less) for self-paced study
- **Free study lab:** any host with `python3` models the disciplines the exams assess — scan coverage and blind spots, credentialed-scan uplift, context-aware prioritization, SLA aging, log attribution and parse-failure detection, deception trip-wires, precision/recall measurement, and SOAR playbook gating (see the volume's labs)

## The four certification exams

Verified against the Academy's certification page on 4 August 2026. All four cost **US$215**.

| Exam | Credential | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **InsightVM (Rapid7 Vulnerability Management)** | Certified **Administrator** | Environment architecture, deployment and scaling; scanning scope optimization; endpoint vulnerability detection and remediation with Insight Agents; operationalizing compliance reporting; remediation prioritization; SOC enablement through custom analytics; workflow automation and orchestration |
| **InsightIDR (Rapid7 SIEM)** | Certified **Specialist** | Log data collection from multiple sources; log query language; **deception technology**; endpoint detection with Insight Agents; alert framework optimization; threat intelligence correlation; SOC enablement through custom analytics; incident-response workflow automation |
| **InsightAppSec (Rapid7 Application Security)** | Certified **Specialist** | Dynamic application security testing of running web applications |
| **InsightConnect (Rapid7 Automation)** | Certified **Specialist** | Security orchestration, automation, and playbooks |

Note the naming asymmetry — InsightVM produces a **Certified Administrator**, the other three
**Certified Specialists** — and that Rapid7 includes a category gloss in each exam name ("Rapid7 SIEM",
"Rapid7 Automation"), which helpfully identifies the discipline for readers who know the category but
not the product brand.

## Rapid7 Academy content types

| Type | Shape |
| --- | --- |
| **On-Demand Training** | Self-paced e-learning, always available |
| **Product and Technology Demonstrations** | Video presentations of product offerings and their value |
| **Virtual Instructor-Led Training** | 1–2 day courses over Zoom, restricted class sizes, **virtual lab environment**, and **one exam attempt included** |
| **Certification Exams** | The four exams above |
| **Product Workshops** | Live, one hour or less, configuration-focused |

Courses carry **16–24 CPE credits** on completion, which feed
[ISC2](../../volume-040-isc2-certifications/README.md) and
[ISACA](../../volume-044-isaca-certifications/README.md) renewal requirements — the same
training-pays-twice arrangement noted for
[Commvault CXXXIII](../../volume-133-commvault-certifications/README.md).

## Notes

- **Choose by deployment, not by ladder.** The four exams are peers covering different products, with no
  prescribed sequence; take the one matching what your organization runs and what you do.
- **The VILT route bundles the attempt**, which for many candidates is both the cheaper and the
  administratively simpler path to the credential.
- **Rapid7 completes the vulnerability-management trio** in this encyclopedia with
  [Tenable LXXVIII](../../volume-078-tenable-certifications/README.md) and
  [Qualys LXXIX](../../volume-079-qualys-certifications/README.md). The discipline transfers between all
  three; learn one properly and the others are largely vocabulary.
- **Distinctive contribution:** deception technology as a first-class detection method, and vulnerability
  management coupled directly to detection and automation on one platform.
