# Chapter 38: Appendix — GIAC Certifications and Course Access

The **GIAC (Global Information Assurance Certification)** program — the SANS Institute's technical
cybersecurity certifications — organized by the eight focus areas, with the CyberLive practical
model, the SANS course alignment, and the delivery and renewal model. The program was verified on
**28 July 2026** from **giac.org** — the same source that anchors
[Volume LXXIV — GIAC (SANS) Certification Tracks](../../volume-074-giac-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** GIAC is owned by the **SANS Institute**. Each certification aligns to a SANS
course (**SEC/FOR/LDR/ICS** numbers) delivered live or on-demand with hands-on labs, but the course
is **not required** to sit the exam. Exams are **proctored** (Pearson VUE testing centers or remote
proctoring) and **open-book** (a printed personal index is permitted), and many include **CyberLive**
— hands-on practical testing on live virtual machines with real tools. Certifications are valid
**four years** and renew through continuing professional education (CPE) or re-examination.

> **Currency.** GIAC adds certifications frequently — recent additions include the **Artificial
> Intelligence** focus area (GMLE, GAIPS, GASAE, GOAA) and new entries such as **GISF, GSOA, GLIR,
> and GCTD**. Confirm the current catalog, codes, and course alignment on giac.org before
> registering.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[GIAC Certifications](https://www.giac.org/certifications/)** — the authoritative catalog, codes,
  and focus-area pages
- **[SANS Institute](https://www.sans.org/)** — the aligned courses, free webcasts, and roadmaps
- **SANS free resources** — posters, "Holiday Hack" challenges, and community tools for hands-on
  practice
- Authorized lab systems (your own VMs) for the defensive and authorized-methodology practice in the
  volume

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** per-exam fees via GIAC; SANS course fees are separate (courses are optional). Confirm
  current pricing on giac.org and sans.org.
- **Delivery:** proctored (Pearson VUE / remote), **open-book**, many with **CyberLive** practical
  testing.
- **Prerequisites:** none formally required, but a foundation (GFACT/GSEC) is recommended before
  specialist and experienced (GX-) exams.
- **Validity and renewal:** **four years**; renew via CPE credits or re-examination.

## Notes

- **CyberLive.** Many GIAC exams are hands-on practical — practice real tasks on authorized lab VMs,
  not just multiple choice.
- **Course optional.** The **exam** is the credential; SANS courses are recommended but not required.
- **Four-year validity.** Renew via CPE or re-exam before expiry.
- **Defensive scope.** Offensive Operations and offensive-AI content is taught as **authorized,
  educational methodology** — scope, rules of engagement, and safe local practice only.
- **Related practice** in the encyclopedia: OffSec in **Volume XLIII**, ISC2 in **Volume XL**, ISACA
  in **Volume XLIV**, CompTIA in **Volume XXXIX**, Check Point in **Volume LXXIII**, and Enterprise
  Cybersecurity in **Volume X**.
