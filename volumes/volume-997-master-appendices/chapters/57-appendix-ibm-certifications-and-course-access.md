# Chapter 57: Appendix — IBM Certifications and Course Access

The **IBM Professional Certification Program** — all current certifications, organized by portfolio,
with the training and access model. The catalog was verified on **3 August 2026** from the **IBM
Training credentials catalog** (ibm.com/training/search, Learning type: Certification — 62 results),
ibm.com/training/credentials, and **Pearson VUE** (pearsonvue.com/us/en/ibm.html) — the same sources
that anchor [Volume CXXIII — IBM Certification Tracks](../../volume-123-ibm-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** Certifications are cataloged on IBM Training (each entry carries a `Cert-` catalog
code, its C1000-series exam, objectives, and recommended courses). Exams register through **Pearson
VUE** — in person at a test center or online with **OnVUE** proctoring; vouchers through the IBM
certification marketplace. Passing issues a **Credly** badge; history lives in **IBM My Learning**.
Distinguish the proctored certifications from **TechXchange badges** (training/advocacy/event) and from
Coursera **"IBM Professional Certificates"** (course-completion certificates) — neither is a
certification. Six **PLUS** combinations additionally require a **Red Hat Certified Specialist**
(OpenShift) exam through Red Hat's program.

> **Currency.** Certifications are **version-pinned** (QRadar V7.5, Db2 13, Maximo v9.1…) and the
> catalog flags credentials **"Retiring soon"** — five at verification time. Re-check the live catalog
> (and toggle "Show Retired/Withdrawn") before targeting any credential.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[IBM Training credentials](https://www.ibm.com/training/credentials)** — the authoritative program
  hub; the certification catalog is the search app filtered to Learning type: Certification
- **[Pearson VUE — IBM](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/ibm.html)** — registration, test centers, OnVUE
- **Free runnable engines for study:** **Qiskit** (`pip install qiskit`), **Db2 Community Edition**
  (container), **IBM MQ Advanced for Developers** (container), WebSphere Liberty (free developer use)
- **IBM SkillsBuild / IBM Developer** — free learning content
- **IBM Redbooks** — free deep-dive technical publications

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** per-exam pricing is published at Pearson VUE registration; confirm there before budgeting.
  Study engines above are free.
- **Delivery:** Pearson VUE test centers or OnVUE online proctoring; C1000-series exam codes.
- **Prerequisites:** most certifications have none; Professional levels assume product experience; PLUS
  combos require the Red Hat exam as part of the credential.
- **Validity and renewal:** per-credential — certifications are version-pinned, and staying current
  generally means certifying on the successor version. Verify on each certification's catalog page.

## Notes

- **The QRadar V7.5 ladder** (Associate → Analyst → Administrator → Deployment Professional, plus the
  CompTIA-combined SOC Analyst) is the program's clearest role progression.
- **Maximo v9/v9.1** shows the modular direction: per-discipline Associate credentials instead of one
  monolithic exam.
- **PLUS combinations** are one credential requiring two vendors' exams — schedule the Red Hat
  Certified Specialist exam through Red Hat's program alongside the IBM exam.
