# Chapter 27: Appendix — Cribl Certifications and Course Access

The **Cribl** certification program — the free, self-study ladder across the observability
pipeline (Stream, Edge, Search, Lake) — organized by level, with each credential's focus,
topic areas, and the training and delivery model. The lineup was verified on **27 July
2026** from **cribl.io/university** — the same source that anchors
[Volume LXI — Cribl Certification Tracks](../../volume-61-cribl-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works at Cribl.** Training and certification are delivered **free** through
**Cribl University** as **online self-study** courses with an assessment. Practice happens
on the **Cribl free tier / Cribl.Cloud**. Certifications are issued as digital badges and
are **valid three years**.

> **Currency.** Cribl revises the program as its portfolio grows (Stream, Edge, Search,
> Lake), adding courses and certifications — confirm the current ladder on
> cribl.io/university before registering.

## Free resources and entry points

- **[Cribl University](https://cribl.io/university/)** and
  **[university.cribl.io](https://university.cribl.io/)** — the authoritative catalog and
  free courses
- **Cribl.Cloud free tier** — a free environment to practice Stream/Edge/Search/Lake
- **Digital badges** — issued on passing; valid three years

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** **free** (CCSC is partner-only).
- **Delivery:** **online self-study** through Cribl University, with an assessment per
  certification. Topic areas are published per credential.
- **Prerequisites:** **CC User** is required for the Admin certs; **CC Engineer** requires
  the Admin certs; **CCSC** requires all prior certs.
- **Validity and renewal:** valid **three years**; recertify to renew.

## Topic areas

- **CC User:** deployment options; Stream/Edge/Search/Lake components; basic configuration;
  queries; pipeline configuration and operation.
- **CC Admin - Stream:** sources and destinations; routes; pipelines and functions (reduce/
  enrich/mask/lookup); packs; worker groups; optimization.
- **CC Admin - Edge:** Edge nodes; fleets and hierarchical config; source-side collection;
  forwarding.
- **CC Engineer:** core data-management concepts (schema-on-read, tiering, reduction vs
  fidelity, replay); end-to-end solution design; optimization strategies.
- **CCSC:** deployment readiness across the portfolio; distributed leader/worker deployment;
  sizing and rollout.

## Notes

- **Free and self-study.** All certifications are free through Cribl University (CCSC is

- **Topic areas are the study scope.** Follow the matching Cribl University course.

- **Recertify** within the three-year window.
