# Chapter 44: Appendix — ServiceNow Certifications and Course Access

The **ServiceNow** certification program — the credentials for administering, developing, and
architecting on the Now Platform — organized by tier, with the exam model, the product
specializations, and the training and practice model. The program was verified on **28 July 2026**
from **servicenow.com** and the ServiceNow Community — the same sources that anchor
[Volume LXXX — ServiceNow Certification Tracks](../../volume-80-servicenow-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** ServiceNow delivers training through **ServiceNow University** (Now Learning) —
courses paired with each certification — and requires **CSA** as the prerequisite for nearly every
other credential. Exams are **online-proctored**. Hands-on practice uses a **free Personal Developer
Instance (PDI)** from developer.servicenow.com. The platform ships **named releases roughly twice a
year**, and certifications require **delta exams** to stay current with each major release.

> **Currency.** ServiceNow releases twice a year (named releases) and adds credentials frequently —
> notably the new **Now Assist (GenAI)** micro-certifications. Take the **delta exam** after each
> named release to keep a certification current, and confirm the catalog on servicenow.com.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[ServiceNow Training & Certification](https://www.servicenow.com/services/training-and-certification.html)**
  — the authoritative program and exams
- **[ServiceNow Developer / PDI](https://developer.servicenow.com/)** — a **free** Personal Developer
  Instance and developer docs
- **[Now Learning](https://nowlearning.servicenow.com/)** — courses and learning paths
- **ServiceNow Community** — the consolidated, current certification list and study discussion

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** CSA ~**$210**; CIS exams ~$210 each; architect tiers priced higher. Confirm current
  pricing on servicenow.com.
- **Delivery:** **online-proctored** exams (CSA is 60 questions / 90 minutes); CTA/CMA include
  practical, multi-day evaluation.
- **Prerequisites:** **CSA** is required (or strongly recommended) for CAD and the CIS family.
- **Validity and renewal:** keep current with **delta exams** at each named release.

## The certification tiers

Verified against servicenow.com on 28 July 2026.

| Tier | Certification |
| --- | --- |
| Foundation | Certified System Administrator (CSA) |
| Development | Certified Application Developer (CAD) |
| Implementation Specialist (CIS) | ITSM, Discovery, Event Management, Service Mapping, CMDB/Data Foundations, CSM, HR (HRSD), FSM, SAM, HAM, Risk & Compliance, Vulnerability Response, Security Incident Response, TPRM, SPM, Service Provider, Performance Analytics, … |
| Architect | Certified Technical Architect (CTA), Certified Master Architect (CMA) |
| Micro-certifications | Now Assist (GenAI), IntegrationHub, Platform Analytics, Virtual Agent, … |

## Platform areas

- **CSA foundation** — tables/inheritance, forms/lists, users/groups/roles, ACLs, Import/Update Sets,
  Service Catalog, CMDB.
- **CAD development** — GlideRecord/GlideSystem, Business Rules, Client Scripts, scoped apps, REST.
- **ITSM/ITOM** — incident/problem/change, Discovery (MID Server), Event Management.
- **CSM/HR** — Customer Service Management, HR Service Delivery, Flow Designer, Service Portal.
- **SecOps/GRC** — Vulnerability Response, Security Incident Response, Integrated Risk Management
  (defensive).
- **Integration/analytics/AI** — IntegrationHub, Performance Analytics, Now Assist.

## Notes

- **CSA first.** It gates the CAD and CIS credentials — start there.
- **Free PDI.** developer.servicenow.com provides a free instance for all hands-on practice.
