# Chapter 37: Appendix — Check Point Certifications and Course Access

The **Check Point** certification program — the credentials for deploying, operating, and
troubleshooting Check Point Quantum network security — organized by the certification ladder, with
the exam codes, the training model, and the delivery and renewal model. The program was verified on
**28 July 2026** from **checkpoint.com** and **Pearson VUE** — the same sources that anchor
[Volume LXXIII — Check Point Certification Tracks](../../volume-073-check-point-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** Check Point delivers training through **Check Point authorized training
centers (ATCs)** and the **Infinity Portal / Mind** learning platform, with instructor-led and
self-paced courses that pair with the certifications. Exams are delivered by **Pearson VUE**. The
ladder runs **CCSA → CCSE → CCSM → CCSM Elite**, with the **CCTE** for deep troubleshooting; CCSM
and CCSM Elite are earned by accumulating **Infinity Specialist Accreditations (ISAs)**. Practice
happens on authorized **Check Point Quantum** gateways/management (including evaluation VMs and
CloudGuard trials).

> **Currency.** The program tracks the current software release. **R82** is current; the **R81.20**
> CCSA (156-215.81.20) and CCSE retire **30 June 2026**, and the **CCTE 156-587** retires
> **30 September 2026** in favor of the R82 **156-588**. Confirm the current certifications and exam
> codes on checkpoint.com/training-certification before registering.

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** per-exam fees via Pearson VUE; course fees via authorized training centers. Confirm
  current pricing on checkpoint.com and Pearson VUE.
- **Delivery:** instructor-led (ATC) and self-paced courses; certification exams via **Pearson VUE**.
- **Prerequisites:** CCSA precedes CCSE; CCSE precedes the ISA path to CCSM; CCTE recommends CCSA/CCSE
  experience.
- **Validity and renewal:** **CCSM is valid two years**; ISAs carry validity periods; exams renumber
  with releases — recertify on the current release before expiry.

## Product and specialization focus

- **Quantum:** the network-security platform (Security Gateway + Management Server) on **Gaia**,
  managed with **SmartConsole** and the **Management API**.
- **CloudGuard:** cloud network security, posture management (CSPM), and workload protection.
- **Harmony:** user and access security — endpoint, email/collaboration, browse, and SASE.
- **Maestro:** hyperscale orchestration bundling appliances into elastic Security Groups.
- **VSX:** many virtual gateways on one appliance for consolidation and multi-tenancy.
- **ISAs:** focused accreditations on the above, building toward **CCSM / CCSM Elite**.

## Notes

- **R82 currency.** Study the current release; the R81.20 CCSA/CCSE and CCTE 156-587 retire in 2026.
- **CCSM is ISA-based.** It is earned by accumulating Infinity Specialist Accreditations after CCSE,
  not by a single exam.
- **Verify codes.** Confirm current exam codes on checkpoint.com — third-party dumps are neither
  authoritative nor permitted.
- **Defensive scope.** Check Point is a defensive platform; study and practice authorized firewall
