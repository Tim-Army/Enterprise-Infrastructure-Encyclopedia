# Chapter 45: Appendix — SUSE Certifications and Course Access

The **SUSE** certification program — the credentials for administering and engineering SUSE's
enterprise open-source portfolio — organized by level and product, with the exam model, the training
model, and the practice options. The program was verified on **29 July 2026** from **suse.com** — the
same source that anchors
[Volume LXXXI — SUSE Certification Tracks](../../volume-081-suse-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** SUSE delivers training through **SUSE Training** (instructor-led and digital
courses) paired with each certification, and exams are delivered remotely through **Questionmark**
(proctored). Hands-on practice is accessible at no cost: **openSUSE Leap** (a free community
distribution close to SLES) for Linux administration, **K3s** (free lightweight Kubernetes) for
cloud-native, and free trials for SLES and Rancher. The program spans three levels — **SCA**
(Administrator), **SCDS** (Deployment Specialist), and **SCE** (Engineer) — each tied to a product
(SUSE Linux Enterprise Server, Rancher, NeuVector, Longhorn, SUSE Manager).

> **Currency.** SUSE ties exams to **product versions** (SLES 15 and later, Rancher releases), so a
> certification tracks a version and should be renewed as products advance. Confirm the current exams
> and product versions on suse.com/training/certification before registering.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[SUSE Training & Certification](https://www.suse.com/training/certification/)** — the authoritative
  levels, products, and exams
- **[openSUSE Leap](https://www.opensuse.org/)** — a free community distribution close to SLES for
  hands-on Linux practice
- **[K3s](https://k3s.io/)** — free lightweight Kubernetes for cloud-native practice
- **[SUSE documentation](https://documentation.suse.com/)** — SLES, Rancher, SUSE Manager, and
  NeuVector docs
- Free **SLES** and **Rancher** trials for product-specific practice

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** per-exam fees via SUSE; training courses priced separately. Confirm current pricing on
  suse.com.
- **Delivery:** remote, proctored via **Questionmark**; exams test conceptual knowledge and practical
  decision-making.
- **Prerequisites:** none strict, but SLES administration grounds the cloud-native tracks.
- **Validity and renewal:** exams are **product-version-tied**; renew as products advance.

## The levels and products

Verified against suse.com on 29 July 2026.

| Level | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| SCA (Certified Administrator) | Day-to-day administration (per product) |
| SCDS (Certified Deployment Specialist) | Deployment and configuration |
| SCE (Certified Engineer) | Advanced engineering |

| Product | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| SUSE Linux Enterprise Server (SLES) | Enterprise Linux administration/engineering |
| SUSE Manager | Fleet patch and configuration management (Salt) |
| Rancher (RKE2/K3s) | Kubernetes management and distributions |
| Longhorn | Cloud-native Kubernetes storage |
| NeuVector | Container security (defensive) |

## Notes

- **SLES first.** The SCA in SUSE Linux Enterprise grounds the whole portfolio.
- **Free practice.** openSUSE Leap and K3s cover most hands-on preparation at no cost.
- **Version-tied.** Exams track product versions; renew as SLES/Rancher advance.
- **Defensive NeuVector.** Container security is defensive protection of authorized clusters.
- **Related coverage** in the encyclopedia: RHEL 10 (**XIV**), Ubuntu (**XXI**), CNCF/Kubernetes
  (**XLI**), and Containers/Platform Engineering (**VIII**).
