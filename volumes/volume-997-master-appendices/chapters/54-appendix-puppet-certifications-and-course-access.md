# Chapter 54: Appendix — Puppet Certifications and Course Access

The **Puppet** certification program — the credential for automating and administering system
infrastructure with Puppet — organized by the exam, its domains, and the training model. The program was
verified on **29 July 2026** from **puppet.com** (the certification and exam-details pages) and the
**Perforce** certification portal — the same sources that anchor
[Volume XCI — Puppet Certification Tracks](../../volume-091-puppet-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** Puppet (a **Perforce** company) offers training and certification through
puppet.com and the Perforce learning portal. The certification is the **Puppet Certified Professional
(PPT-PCP-24)**, delivered **online-proctored through Questionmark** — **60 multiple-choice questions in
90 minutes** for **$200 USD**, in English — and based on **Open Source Puppet 8.9.0+** and **Puppet
Enterprise 2023.8.0+**. There are no formal prerequisites, though candidates should understand Puppet
automation, basic module development, and the Puppet Language **Style Guide**. Hands-on practice uses the
**free open-source Puppet**, whose language and `puppet apply` behavior match Puppet Enterprise; Puppet
offers foundational and advanced training courses.

> **Currency.** The current exam is **PPT-PCP-24** ("Puppet 206"), aligned to Puppet 8 / Puppet
> Enterprise 2023.8. Confirm the current exam version and objectives on puppet.com as the platform
> advances.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[Puppet Certification](https://www.puppet.com/learning-training/certification)** — the authoritative
  certification information
- **[PCP Exam Details](https://www.puppet.com/learning-training/certification/exam-details)** — the eight
  exam domains and logistics
- **[Perforce Certification Portal](https://ondemand.questionmark.com/home/405096/catalog)** — buy and
  schedule the Questionmark exam
- **Open-source Puppet 8** — **free** for hands-on practice (same language as Puppet Enterprise)
- **Puppet training courses** — foundational and advanced (via puppet.com)

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** the exam is **$200 USD**; open-source Puppet practice is **free**. Confirm current pricing on
  puppet.com.
- **Delivery:** **Questionmark** online-proctored; **60 multiple-choice questions**; **90 minutes**;
  English.
- **Prerequisites:** none formal; Puppet automation and module-development familiarity expected.
- **Validity and renewal:** re-certify against the current exam version as Puppet/PE advance.

## Notes

- **Free open-source Puppet.** The same language and `puppet apply` behavior as Puppet Enterprise makes
  hands-on preparation free.
- **Eight domains.** Prepare broadly — the exam spans concepts, language, modules, classification,
  environments, administration, orchestration, and troubleshooting.
- **Style Guide.** The exam expects idiomatic, linted code.
- **Defensive framing.** Configuration management enforces an idempotent desired state and corrects drift
  — defensive standardization of your own infrastructure.
- **Related coverage** in the encyclopedia: Ansible (**LIX**) as the other major configuration-management
  platform, and Infrastructure Automation (**IX**) for the broader automation context.
