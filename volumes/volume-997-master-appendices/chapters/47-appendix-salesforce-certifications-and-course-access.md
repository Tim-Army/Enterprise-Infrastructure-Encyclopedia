# Chapter 47: Appendix — Salesforce Certifications and Course Access

The **Salesforce** certification program — the credentials for administering, developing, consulting
on, and architecting the Salesforce CRM/low-code platform — organized by track, with the exam model,
the Trailhead training model, and the maintenance model. The program was verified on **29 July 2026**
from **salesforce.com** and **Trailhead** — the same sources that anchor
[Volume LXXXIII — Salesforce Certification Tracks](../../volume-083-salesforce-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** Salesforce's primary learning platform is **Trailhead** — free, gamified
trails, modules, superbadges, and hands-on **Playgrounds** — paired with each certification's official
**exam guide**. Exams are delivered **online-proctored** (via Kryterion/Webassessor). Hands-on
practice uses a **free Developer Edition org** (developer.salesforce.com) that never expires, plus the
**Salesforce CLI** for scratch orgs and deployment. Salesforce ships **three releases a year** (Spring,
Summer, Winter), and certifications are **maintained by completing free Trailhead release modules** each
cycle.

> **Currency.** The program grows every release, most recently with the high-demand **Agentforce** and
> **AI** credentials (Agentforce Specialist, AI Specialist, AI Associate). Complete the **release
> maintenance** module each cycle to keep a certification current, and confirm the catalog on
> salesforce.com/Trailhead.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[Salesforce Certification](https://www.salesforce.com/services/certification/)** — the
  authoritative tracks and exam guides
- **[Trailhead](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/)** — **free** trails, modules, superbadges, and
  Playgrounds, plus certification maintenance
- **[Developer Edition org](https://developer.salesforce.com/signup)** — a **free**, non-expiring org
  for hands-on practice
- **Salesforce CLI** — scratch orgs and deployment practice
- **Trailblazer Community** — study groups and discussion

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** per-exam fees via Salesforce; training on Trailhead is **free**. Confirm current pricing on
  salesforce.com.
- **Delivery:** **online-proctored** (Kryterion/Webassessor); concepts plus applied scenarios.
- **Prerequisites:** the Administrator grounds most tracks; the Architect track requires several
  underlying exams; CTA requires a board review.
- **Validity and renewal:** maintained via **free release modules** across the three annual releases.
