# Chapter 40: Appendix — Okta Certifications and Course Access

The **Okta** certification program — the credentials for administering Okta's identity and access
management platform — organized by the role ladder and specialties, with the exam model, the
platform clouds, and the delivery and renewal model. The program was verified on **28 July 2026**
from **okta.com** — the same source that anchors
[Volume LXXVI — Okta Certification Tracks](../../volume-076-okta-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** Okta delivers training through **Okta Learning** (self-paced courses and
learning paths) and instructor-led classes, paired with the certifications. Exams are **proctored
online via ProctorU** (using the Guardian Browser). Hands-on practice uses a **free Okta
Integrator/Developer org** (developer.okta.com) and a free **Auth0** tenant for customer identity.
The role ladder runs **Certified Professional → Administrator → Consultant → Technical Architect**,
with **Certified Developer**, specialties (**Workflows**, **Access Gateway**), and the **Auth0
Certified Developer**.

> **Currency.** Standard Okta certifications are valid **two years** and renewed by re-examination as
> the platform evolves (new authenticators, Identity Governance, and Auth0 capabilities); the
> **Technical Architect** is valid **three years** (with an automatic one-year extension) and
> includes a **board-defense** exam. Confirm current certifications and prerequisites on okta.com
> before registering.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[Okta Certification](https://www.okta.com/services/certification/)** — the authoritative ladder,
  specialties, and exam model
- **[Okta Learning](https://www.okta.com/services/training/)** — self-paced courses and learning paths
- **[Okta Developer](https://developer.okta.com/)** — a free Integrator/Developer org and API docs
- **[Auth0](https://auth0.com/)** — a free tenant for customer identity (CIC) practice
- Practice exams (7 attempts) offered alongside the certification exams

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** exams **$250** (first attempt), **$100** (retakes), practice exams ~$75; the Technical
  Architect board defense is **$5,000** (first attempt). Confirm current pricing on okta.com.
- **Delivery:** online, **proctored via ProctorU** (Guardian Browser).
- **Prerequisites:** Administrator requires Professional; Consultant requires Professional +
  Administrator; Technical Architect requires Professional, Administrator, Consultant, and WIC
  Developer.
- **Validity and renewal:** **two years** for standard certifications (re-exam), **three years** for
  the Technical Architect.

## Platform areas

- **Universal Directory** — profiles, attributes, AD/LDAP integration, SCIM.
- **Single Sign-On** — SAML, OIDC, SWA, the OIN app catalog.
- **Adaptive MFA & policies** — authenticators, authentication/sign-on policies, ThreatInsight.
- **Lifecycle Management** — automated provisioning and deprovisioning.
- **API Access Management** — Okta as an OAuth 2.0 authorization server.
- **Identity Governance (OIG)** — access requests, certifications, separation of duties.
- **Workflows / Access Gateway** — automation and on-prem/legacy app SSO.
- **Auth0 (CIC)** — B2C/B2B customer identity, Actions, Organizations.

## Notes

- **Two clouds.** Workforce Identity Cloud (WIC) for employees; Customer Identity Cloud (CIC/Auth0)
  for customer apps.
- **Prerequisites matter.** The ladder gates each rung; the Architect requires the full stack plus a
  board defense.
- **Free practice org.** developer.okta.com and a free Auth0 tenant support all labs at no cost.
- **Defensive scope.** IAM is defensive — the volume's labs are authorized administration of
  directories, SSO, MFA, lifecycle, and governance.
