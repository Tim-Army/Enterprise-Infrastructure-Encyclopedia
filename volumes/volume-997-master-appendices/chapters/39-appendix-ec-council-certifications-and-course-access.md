# Chapter 39: Appendix — EC-Council Certifications and Course Access

The **EC-Council** certification program — the International Council of E-Commerce Consultants'
cybersecurity credentials, led by the **Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH)** — organized by track, with
the exam model, the accreditation and DoD alignment, and the delivery and renewal model. The program
was verified on **28 July 2026** from **eccouncil.org** — the same source that anchors
[Volume LXXV — EC-Council Certification Tracks](../../volume-75-ec-council-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** EC-Council delivers training through accredited training centers, iClass
(official self-paced/live), and academic partners, paired with the certifications. Knowledge exams
run on the **ECC exam portal**; practical exams run in the **iLabs Cyber Range** (CEH Practical,
CPENT, LPT). EC-Council programs are **ANSI 17024** accredited, and **CEH, CND, and CHFI** meet **US
DoD 8140/8570** baseline requirements. Certifications are maintained through **EC-Council Continuing
Education (ECE)** credits. The free-to-learn **Essentials Series** (Network Defense, Ethical Hacking,
Digital Forensics Essentials, and more) provides a low-cost entry.

> **Currency.** EC-Council advances its programs — the flagship is now **CEH v13, "with the power of
> AI,"** and a new **AI Security & Management** family (CAIPM, COASP, CRAGE, AI Essentials) has been
> added. Confirm the current versions and exam codes on eccouncil.org before registering.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[EC-Council Train & Certify](https://www.eccouncil.org/train-certify/)** — the authoritative
  tracks, CEH v13, and exam model
- **[EC-Council Essentials Series](https://www.eccouncil.org/academia/essentials-series/)** —
  free-to-learn foundational courses (paid exam for the certificate)
- **CodeRed / iClass** — official self-paced and live training
- **iLabs Cyber Range** — the hands-on practical-exam environment
- Authorized lab systems (your own VMs) for the defensive and authorized-methodology practice in the
  volume

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** per-exam and per-course fees via EC-Council; confirm current pricing on eccouncil.org.
- **Delivery:** knowledge exams on the **ECC exam portal**; practical exams in **iLabs** (CEH
  Practical is 6 hours / 20 challenges; CEH knowledge is 4 hours / 125 MCQ).
- **Prerequisites:** CEH requires training or documented experience; advanced credentials expect
  track experience.
- **Validity and renewal:** maintained via **ECE credits** over the certification cycle.
