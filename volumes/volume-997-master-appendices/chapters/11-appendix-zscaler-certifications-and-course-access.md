# Chapter 11: Appendix — Zscaler Certifications and Course Access

Every current Zscaler certification, organized by tier, with its official page,
exam format, delivery method, fee where published, and the training that
prepares it. The lineup and access facts were harvested on **25 July 2026** from
Zscaler's own **Customer Success Center** and **Cyber Academy**, and anchored to
Zscaler's official **Certification Program Brief** and **Certification Exam FAQs**
(last updated 7 May 2026) — the same harvest that anchors
[Volume XXXV — Zscaler Zero Trust Exchange](../../volume-035-zscaler-zero-trust-exchange/README.md),
whose nine chapters map this program to hands-on content. Third-party exam-dump
sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works at Zscaler.** Training is delivered through the **Zscaler
Cyber Academy** ([customer.zscaler.com](https://customer.zscaler.com/page/certification-exams)
for customers; [partneracademy.zscaler.com](https://partneracademy.zscaler.com)
for partners), with product documentation on the **Zscaler Help Portal**
([help.zscaler.com](https://help.zscaler.com)). Each certification is the final
step of a **learning path** that bundles **eLearning courses and a hands-on
lab** — for example, ZDTA completes *Zscaler for Users — Administrator*
(**EDU-200**), ZDTE completes *Zscaler for Users — Engineer* (**EDU-202**), and
ZDXA completes *Zscaler Digital Experience Operationalization* (**EDU-310**).
**Exams are paid**: the three professional exams (ZDTA, ZDTE, ZDXA) are
**proctored at US$300 per attempt**, while ZTCA is an **online, unproctored**
associate assessment. **Practice exams** are available too, but are unproctored
and grant no certificate.

> **Currency.** Zscaler uses **named credentials with no exam codes** (like
> Google Cloud), so a re-scope or rename leaves no version signal — confirm each
> certification's name, fee, language, and learning path on the Cyber Academy
> before registering. **Weighted exam blueprints are published for the three
> professional exams — ZDTA, ZDTE, and ZDXA** (below); only **ZTCA** (associate)
> is course-only, prepared solely by its eLearning with no published blueprint.
> Each professional exam's **Study Guide** (behind the Cyber Academy login) holds
> the expansive objective detail beneath the published weighting.

## Fees, validity, and recertification

From the **Certification Exam FAQs**:

- **Fee.** The three **proctored** certification exams — **ZDTA, ZDTE, ZDXA** —
  are **US$300 per attempt**, and registration fees are **non-refundable and
  non-transferable**. ZTCA (the unproctored associate assessment) is not covered
  by that pricing statement — confirm its fee on the Cyber Academy.
- **Validity.** Zscaler certifications are valid for **two years** from the date
  earned — a fixed-validity model like Google Cloud's (Chapter 10), not the
  free-annual-renewal model of Microsoft (Chapter 09). You may recertify starting
  **90 days before** expiration by passing the **current version** of the exam;
  there is no separate renewal path and no prerequisite, and the only cost is the
  US$300 exam fee. If a certification lapses, the Credly badge expires with it.
- **Registration & delivery.** Register on the **Pearson VUE** platform (a
  **Zscaler Cyber Academy** account and a Zscaler Client Candidate ID are the
  prerequisite). Sit the exam **in-person at a Pearson VUE test center** or
  **online-proctored with OnVUE**. OnVUE is **not offered in China** (in-person
  only there), and exams are **not offered at all** in Cuba, Iran, North Korea,
  Russia, Syria, Ukraine, or in Quebec, Canada.
- **Results.** Pass/Fail appears on screen immediately. Non-passers receive a
  score breakdown **by weighted blueprint area** — Zscaler confirms the exam
  topics are weighted per the published blueprint — while passers receive status
  only, and item-level scores are never released.

## Published exam blueprints

The three professional exams each publish a **weighted domain blueprint** on
their exam pages. The tables below map each domain to the
[Volume XXXV](../../volume-035-zscaler-zero-trust-exchange/README.md) chapters
that build the underlying skills. Each exam's **Study Guide** holds the
expansive objective list beneath the weighting; *Zscaler notes the guidelines
may change without notice.*

### ZDTA — Digital Transformation Administrator

Administrator-tier: configure and administer the Zero Trust Exchange across ZIA,
ZPA, Client Connector, identity, and monitoring. Required prerequisites are the
*Zscaler for Users — Administrator* (**EDU-200**) course **and** hands-on lab —
Zscaler states these are required, not merely recommended.

| ZDTA domain | Weight | Volume XXXV chapters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| User & Device Management | 18% | 07 (Client Connector), 08 (identity) |
| Platform Management | 18% | 01 (Zero Trust Exchange), 02 (ZIA), 05 (ZPA) |
| Policy & Security Configuration | 29% | 02–04 (ZIA policy), 05–06 (ZPA policy) |
| Monitoring, Reporting & Analytics | 13% | 09 (ZDX, logging/analytics) |
| Troubleshooting & Incident Response | 13% | 09 (ops); each chapter's troubleshooting |
| Integration & Optimization | 9% | 07 (forwarding), 08 (SAML/SCIM integration) |

The ZDTA Study Guide organizes those objectives into the platform's **core-skill
areas** — Identity Services (**ZIdentity**, SAML/SCIM/OIDC), Connectivity
Services (Z-Tunnel 2.0, GRE/IPSec, PAC, Client Connector), Platform Services
(TLS decryption, the policy framework's decision/enforcement points), Access
Control Services (URL/web filtering, cloud firewall, App Connector,
segmentation), Cyberthreat Protection (ATP, sandbox, IPS, **Deception**,
**ITDR**, browser isolation), Data Security (DLP, CASB, **DSPM/SSPM**), Risk
Management (**Risk360**, UVM, EASM, Breach Predictor), Zscaler Digital
Experience (ZDX), and **Zero Trust Automation** (APIs, **OneAPI**).

Engineer-tier: design, deploy, operate, troubleshoot, and optimize the platform,
framed around scenario judgment rather than administration. The *Zscaler for
Users — Engineer* (**EDU-202**) course and hands-on lab are strongly recommended.

| ZDTE domain | Weight | Volume XXXV chapters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Architecture & Design | 22% | 01 (ZTE), 02 (ZIA), 05 (ZPA) |
| Implementation & Deployment | 22% | 02–04 (ZIA), 05–07 (ZPA / forwarding) |
| Operations & Monitoring | 16% | 09 (ZDX, logging) |
| Troubleshooting & Support | 15% | 09 (ops); each chapter's troubleshooting |
| Security & Compliance | 14% | 03 (threat), 04 (data protection), 06 (ZPA advanced) |

ZDTE reworks the same ZIA/ZPA/ZDX surface as ZDTA into engineering scenarios:

segmentation and access-policy tracing, ZDX probe placement and root-cause
analysis, and policy-containment corrective/preventive actions.

### ZDXA — Digital Experience Administrator

Digital-experience specialist: design, install, configure, maintain, and
troubleshoot ZDX. The *ZDX Operationalization* (**EDU-310**) course and hands-on
lab are strongly recommended.

| ZDXA domain | Weight | Volume XXXV chapters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monitoring & Triaging Digital Experience | 30% | 09 (ZDX score, probes, Cloud Path) |
| Operational Configuration & Optimization | 30% | 09 (ZDX config, alerts, thresholds) |

ZDXA is entirely **ZDX-focused**, so all four domains map to Volume XXXV's ZDX

thresholds, and least-privilege ZDX API use).

## Why the exam-code column reads "none"

Like Google Cloud (Chapter 10), Zscaler identifies a certification **solely by
name** — there is no equivalent of AWS's `SAA-C03` or Azure's `AZ-104`. That
removes the fastest currency signal the coded programs provide, so checking
currency means confirming the certification still appears on the Cyber Academy
at the expected tier, and comparing the exam's Study Guide objective list (and,
for ZDTA, ZDTE, and ZDXA, the published weighting) against your study material.
Zscaler also **renames courses and certifications** as the platform evolves,
which a coded program would surface as a version bump.

## Notes

- **No exam codes** — every row's Exam code reads "none"; credentials are named.
- **Fees:** the three proctored certification exams (ZDTA, ZDTE, ZDXA) are
  **US$300 per attempt**, non-refundable and non-transferable; ZTCA's fee is not
  published — confirm it on the Cyber Academy.
- **Validity is two years**; recertify by passing the current exam version
  (eligible from 90 days before expiry, US$300 per attempt), the same
  fixed-validity, paid-retake model noted for Google Cloud (Chapter 10).
- **The three professional exams (ZDTA, ZDTE, ZDXA) publish weighted blueprints
  openly**; only **ZTCA**'s preparation is course-only (its eLearning), which is
  why its row records a learning path rather than a weighting.
- **All three professional exams run 60 questions in 90 minutes**; **ZTCA runs
  75 questions in 120 minutes** and is **online, unproctored**. The professional
  exams are delivered in-person at a Pearson VUE test center or online-proctored
  with OnVUE.
- **Languages:** ZDTA and ZDTE are offered in **English and Japanese**; **ZTCA
  and ZDXA are English only**.
- **Credentials** are Credly digital badges; ZTCA grants a **certificate of
  completion**, while the three professional exams grant a **certification**.
- **Practice exams are unproctored** and grant no certificate; they are for exam
  familiarity only.
