# Chapter 14: Appendix — ISC2 Certifications and Course Access

ISC2's **vendor-neutral** certification program — the governance, architecture,
engineering, and management tier of the security profession — organized by the
credential ladder, with each credential's weighted exam domains, mechanics,
experience requirement, and the CPE/AMF renewal model. The lineup, domains, and
weights were harvested on **26 July 2026** from the official **ISC2 exam
outlines** at **isc2.org** — the same source that anchors
[Volume XL — ISC2 Certification Tracks](../../volume-040-isc2-certifications/README.md).
Third-party brain-dump sites were excluded as sources (and violate the ISC2 Code
of Ethics).

**How access works at ISC2.** Official preparation is delivered through **ISC2
Official Training** — instructor-led and **self-paced online** courses per
credential — plus the **Official Study Guides** and **Official Practice Tests**.
Free resources include the downloadable **exam outline (blueprint)** on each
certification page (the definitive domains and weights), exam-detail pages, and
the **Certified in Cybersecurity (CC)** exam and self-paced training offered
**free** under the **One Million Certified in Cybersecurity** initiative. Exams
are delivered through **Pearson VUE** (test center or, for some, online), and
credentials appear as shareable digital badges.

> **Currency.** ISC2 refreshes its exam outlines on a **Job Task Analysis**
> cycle, so the **effective date** on the outline is the currency signal —
> confirm each credential's domains, weights, and effective date on isc2.org
> before registering. Recent changes captured in this appendix: **CISSP**
> refreshed **15 April 2024**; **SSCP** new outline **September 2024** (now CAT);
> **CGRC** refreshed **15 June 2024** (formerly **CAP**); the **ISSAP/ISSEP/ISSMP**
> concentrations all re-issued **1 August 2025** (new domain counts); **CCSP**
> new outline **effective 1 August 2026**; and **CC** new outline **effective 1
> September 2026**. A new **AI Security certification is in development**.
> **HCISPP** has been retired, and the organization's styling moved from
> **(ISC)²** to **ISC2**.

## Free training and entry points

- **[ISC2 Certifications](https://www.isc2.org/certifications)** — the
  authoritative credential pages, each linking a downloadable exam outline
- **[ISC2 Exam Outlines](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/exam-outlines)** —
  the blueprint hub; each outline carries the weighted domains and effective date
- **[Certified in Cybersecurity (CC)](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cc)** —
  **free** exam and self-paced training under the One Million initiative
- **ISC2 Official Study Guides and Practice Tests** — the official (paid) book
  and practice-question series per credential
- **ISC2 CPE and member resources** — the CPE Handbook, Annual Maintenance Fee,
  and Code of Ethics

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fee band (US pricing; varies by country — confirm at registration):** **CC**
  is **free** (One Million initiative); **SSCP** sits in the low-hundreds band;
  **CISSP**, **CCSP**, **CGRC**, and **CSSLP** are the highest single-exam band;
  the **ISSAP/ISSEP/ISSMP** concentrations are priced similarly to the
  professional exams. Confirm current pricing at Pearson VUE.
- **Delivery:** **Pearson VUE**. **CISSP**, **SSCP**, and **CC** use **Computer
  Adaptive Testing (CAT)**; the others are linear. The passing score is
  **700/1000** across the program.
- **Requirements beyond the exam:** ISC2 enforces **work experience** (see the
  ladder below), professional **endorsement** after passing, and agreement to the
  **Code of Ethics**. Candidates who pass without the experience become an
  **Associate of ISC2** with a window to earn it.
- **Accreditation and recognition:** ISC2 credentials are **ANSI/ISO/IEC 17024**
  accredited; several satisfy **U.S. DoD 8140/8570** baselines (SSCP → IAT II;
  CISSP → IAT/IAM III and IASAE; CCSP → cloud; CGRC → RMF roles).
- **Validity and renewal:** credentials are valid for **three years** and renew
  by earning **CPE credits** (an annual minimum plus a three-year total) and
  paying the **Annual Maintenance Fee (AMF)** — **no re-exam**. A single combined
  AMF covers a member holding several credentials, and concentrations lapse if
  the parent CISSP does.

## Notes

- **The effective date is the currency signal.** ISC2 re-scopes on a Job Task
  Analysis cycle; a new effective date means changed domains or weights. Changes
  captured here: CISSP (2024), SSCP (2024), CGRC (2024, from CAP), the three
  concentrations (2025), and CCSP/CC refreshes (2026).
- **Passing is step one.** **Endorsement**, the **experience** requirement, the
  **Code of Ethics**, the **AMF**, and **CPE** all apply — plan for a multi-year
  commitment, not a one-time exam.
- **CC is free** under the One Million Certified in Cybersecurity initiative
  (exam + self-paced training) — the recommended no-cost entry point.
- **Concentrations require an active CISSP.** ISSAP/ISSEP/ISSMP add depth in
  architecture, engineering, and management respectively and lapse with the
  parent CISSP.
- **Hands-on practice** for the ISC2-adjacent skills lives across the
  encyclopedia: general security under Volume X and the vendor security volumes
  (XVI, XIX, XXV, XXXV); cloud security (CCSP) under the cloud volumes (XVII,
  XXXIII, XXXIV); secure development (CSSLP) under the automation and
  platform-engineering volumes (VIII, IX); and governance/RMF (CGRC, ISSEP)
  alongside the CompTIA and vendor-neutral material in Volume XXXIX.
