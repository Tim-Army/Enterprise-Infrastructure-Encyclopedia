# Chapter 30: Appendix — HPE Aruba Networking Certifications and Course Access

The **HPE Aruba Networking** certification program — the credentials for operating, securing,
and automating Aruba's campus, wireless, security, and data-center platform (**AOS-CX**, **Aruba
Central**, **ClearPass**) — organized by tier and track, with each credential's exam code and
the training and delivery model. The program was verified on **28 July 2026** from
**certification-learning.hpe.com** and **hpepress.hpe.com** — the same sources that anchor
[Volume LXIV — HPE Aruba Networking Certification Tracks](../../volume-064-aruba-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** Certification is delivered through **HPE Networking Education Services**;
training is available as instructor-led courses and self-paced content, and exams are delivered
by **Pearson VUE**. Official study guides are published through **HPE Press** (hpepress.hpe.com).
HPE certifications typically carry a **three-year** validity, after which they are renewed by
passing a current exam.

> **Currency.** The program was **rebranded** from "Aruba Certified" to **HPE Aruba Networking
> Certified** and **renumbered** (HPE6-/HPE7- codes), and its tracks and Advanced Product
> Certifications evolve with the portfolio. Confirm the current tracks, exam codes, and
> recertification terms on certification-learning.hpe.com before registering.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[HPE Certification & Learning](https://certification-learning.hpe.com/tr/certifications/aruba)**
  — the authoritative catalog, datasheets, and datacards
- **[HPE Aruba Networking Education Services](https://www.hpe.com/us/en/networking/hpe-aruba-networking-education-services.html)**
  — courses and training paths
- **[HPE Press](https://hpepress.hpe.com/)** — official certification study guides (with exam codes)
- Practice on **virtual AOS-CX** (GNS3/containerlab), **Aruba Central** trials, and the
  **ClearPass** evaluation

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** per-exam fees via Pearson VUE; instructor-led course fees separately. Confirm current
  pricing on the HPE portal.
- **Delivery:** exams at **Pearson VUE**; training through HPE Networking Education Services
  (instructor-led and self-paced).
- **Prerequisites:** climb the tiers within a track (Associate → Professional → Expert);
  the Network Architect and Advanced Product Certifications build on that foundation.
- **Validity and renewal:** typically **three years**; renew by passing a current exam.

## Track focus

- **Campus Access:** AOS-CX access, VSX aggregation, Aruba Central management, and dynamic
  segmentation with roles.
- **Switching:** AOS-CX Layer-2/Layer-3, VSX and VSF redundancy, and the REST API.
- **Network Security:** ClearPass NAC (802.1X/MAC/portal, profiling, posture), roles, and the
  Policy Enforcement Firewall.
- **Mobility:** WLAN with APs on AOS-10, WPA3/802.1X, RF design, and gateways.
- **Data Center:** leaf-spine EVPN-VXLAN, VSX, and the CX 10000 distributed-services switch.
- **Design (Network Architect):** requirements-driven campus and data-center architecture.
