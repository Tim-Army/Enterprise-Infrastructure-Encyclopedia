# Chapter 35: Appendix — CWNP Certifications and Course Access

The **CWNP** (Certified Wireless Network Professional) program — the vendor-neutral standard for
enterprise Wi-Fi and wireless-IoT certification — organized by career level, with each
certification's focus, the expert requirements, and the training and delivery model. The program
was verified on **28 July 2026** from **cwnp.com** — the same source that anchors
[Volume LXIX — CWNP Certification Tracks](../../volume-69-cwnp-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** CWNP publishes official study guides, learning resources, and practice
tests, and training is available from CWNP and its authorized learning partners. Exams are
delivered by **Prometric** (the program transitioned from Pearson VUE on **1 August 2024**) at
testing centers worldwide and via remote proctoring. Certifications are **valid three years**. The
labs in Volume LXIX use only free tools — `python3` for RF math and **Wireshark/tshark** for
802.11 analysis.

> **Currency.** Exams moved to **Prometric** in 2024, and the 2026 content emphasizes **802.11ax
> (Wi-Fi 6/6E)** and introduces **802.11be (Wi-Fi 7)**. Confirm the current exam codes and
> requirements on cwnp.com before registering.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[CWNP Certifications](https://www.cwnp.com/certifications/)** — the authoritative program,
  career levels, and exams
- **CWNP learning resources and study guides** — the CWNA guide and per-certification materials
- **Wireshark/tshark** and `python3` — free tools for 802.11 analysis and RF math
- Practice on your **own or an authorized lab WLAN** (capture and analysis only)

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** per-exam fees via Prometric (CWNA-109 is about **US$274.99**); confirm current pricing
  on cwnp.com.
- **Delivery:** **Prometric** testing centers and remote proctoring (since 1 August 2024).
- **Prerequisites:** **CWNA** is the foundation for the professional level; the experts require
  multiple professional certs plus a third-party certification and a board review.
- **Validity and renewal:** valid **three years**; renew by re-examination or by earning higher
  credentials.

## The certification map

Verified against cwnp.com on 28 July 2026.

| Level | Certification | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry | CWSS | Wireless sales/marketing fundamentals |
| Entry | CWTS | Wireless technology specialist (install/config) |
| Administrator | CWNA (CWNA-109) | RF fundamentals, 802.11, site survey, security — the foundation |
| Professional (Wi-Fi) | CWSP | Enterprise Wi-Fi security (802.1X/EAP, WPA3, PMF, WIPS) |
| Professional (Wi-Fi) | CWAP | 802.11 protocol analysis and WLAN troubleshooting |
| Professional (Wi-Fi) | CWDP | WLAN design, site survey, capacity, validation |
| Professional (IoT) | CWISA | RF and IoT fundamentals, device integration |
| Professional (IoT) | CWICP | IoT connectivity (physical/data layers) |
| Professional (IoT) | CWIIP | IoT system and data integration |
| Professional (IoT) | CWIDP | IoT RF, network, and data-flow design |
| Expert | CWNE | Enterprise Wi-Fi expert (CWNA+CWSP+CWAP+CWDP+CWISA + 3rd-party + review) |
| Expert | CWISE | IoT expert (CWNA+CWIIP+CWICP+CWIDP + 3rd-party + review) |

## Track focus

- **Entry/CWNA:** RF math, 802.11 bands/channels/frames, WLAN architecture, and WPA2/WPA3 basics.
- **CWSP:** 802.1X/EAP, the 4-way handshake, WPA3 SAE/PMF, and WIPS — defensive security.
- **CWAP:** monitor-mode capture and 802.11 protocol analysis for troubleshooting.
- **CWDP:** requirements-driven design, site surveys, channel/capacity planning, and validation.
- **IoT track:** wireless-IoT protocols (BLE, Zigbee/Thread, LoRaWAN, cellular IoT), connectivity,
  integration, and design.

## Notes

- **CWNA first.** It is the foundation for both professional tracks and the experts.
- **Two experts.** CWNE (Wi-Fi) and CWISE (IoT), each earned through multiple certs plus a review —
  not a single exam.
- **Prometric delivery** since 1 August 2024; **Wi-Fi 6E/7** in current content.
- **Vendor-neutral.** Pair CWNP theory with a vendor certification (Aruba, Cisco, MikroTik).
- **Related practice** in the encyclopedia: HPE Aruba in **Volume LXIV**, MikroTik in **Volume
  LXVIII**, Cisco in **Volume XXV**, and Wireshark in **Volume XX**.
