# Chapter 48: Appendix — NetApp Certifications and Course Access

The **NetApp** certification program — the credentials for administering, implementing, and
architecting NetApp **ONTAP** storage and the hybrid-cloud data fabric around it — organized by path,
with the tier model, the training model, and the recertification model. The program was verified on
**29 July 2026** from **netapp.com** (the certification hub and the individual program pages) — the same
sources that anchor
[Volume LXXXIV — NetApp Certification Tracks](../../volume-84-netapp-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** NetApp's learning platform is the **Learning Center** (a NetApp **Support Site ID**
is required), paired with **free exam-prep videos** for every certification and instructor-led or
on-demand training courses. Hands-on practice uses the **free Simulate ONTAP** simulator (a downloadable
ONTAP VM) so candidates can run real clustershell commands without hardware. Exams are delivered through
**Pearson VUE** (test center or online-proctored); passing earns a **Credly** digital badge, and records
live in the **CertCenter**. As of **10 December 2024**, the certification system uses your **NetApp login
credentials** rather than a separate CertMetrics login. Certifications are generally valid for about
**two years**; you recertify by passing the current version of the exam or a higher-level exam in the
path.

> **Currency.** NetApp refreshed the core exams in **April 2026** — **NS0-163** (Data Administrator
> ONTAP), **NS0-521** (Implementation Engineer SAN), **NS0-528** (Implementation Engineer Data
> Protection), **NS0-304** (Hybrid Cloud Administrator), and **NS0-604** (Hybrid Cloud Architect) — so
> study against the current blueprint and confirm the catalog on netapp.com.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[NetApp Certification](https://www.netapp.com/support-and-training/netapp-learning-services/certifications/)**
  — the authoritative certification paths and program pages
- **[Certification Program Policies and FAQs](https://www.netapp.com/support-and-training/certification-program-policies/)**
  — validity, recertification, and exam policies
- **Free exam-prep videos** — NetApp-produced prep for each certification
- **Simulate ONTAP** — a **free** downloadable ONTAP simulator for hands-on practice
- **NetApp Learning Center** — training courses (requires a NetApp Support Site ID)
- **NetApp Community — Learning Services** — discussion and program-update announcements

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** per-exam fees via Pearson VUE; the Simulate ONTAP simulator and prep videos are **free**.
  Confirm current pricing on netapp.com.
- **Delivery:** **Pearson VUE** (test center or online-proctored); multiple-choice with scenario items.
- **Prerequisites:** the **Data Administrator ONTAP (NCDA)** grounds most Specialist paths; the Associate
  accreditations (Cloud Native, Hybrid Cloud) are recommended foundations.
- **Validity and renewal:** generally **~2 years**; recertify with the current exam or a higher-level one.
