# Chapter 43: Appendix — Qualys Certifications and Course Access

The **Qualys** certification program — the credentials for operating Qualys's cloud-based
vulnerability-management, compliance, and exposure-management platform — organized by Certified
Specialist path, with the free training model, the platform architecture, and the course structure.
The program was verified on **28 July 2026** from **qualys.com** — the same source that anchors
[Volume LXXIX — Qualys Certification Tracks](../../volume-079-qualys-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** Qualys delivers training and certification through the **Qualys Certification &
Training Center**, and — distinctively — the **Qualys Certified Specialist** courses are **free**
(self-paced or instructor-led, with hands-on labs on the live platform). Everything runs on the
**Qualys Cloud Platform**, fed by **sensors** (Cloud Agents, scanner appliances, passive network
sensors, cloud connectors, and the API), and searched with **QQL (Qualys Query Language)**. Practice
uses a **free Qualys Community Edition / trial** and authorized lab targets. The flagship is **VMDR
(Vulnerability Management, Detection and Response)**, with the platform organized under **Enterprise
TruRisk**.

> **Currency.** The platform evolves continuously — the shift to **Enterprise TruRisk** and newer
> applications such as **TotalCloud** and **TotalAppSec**. Because the training is free and
> continuously updated, revisit qualys.com/training for the current course catalog.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[Qualys Training & Certification](https://www.qualys.com/training/)** — the authoritative,
  **free** Certified Specialist courses and paths
- **[Qualys Community Edition](https://www.qualys.com/community-edition/)** — a free tier for hands-on
  practice (authorized targets only)
- **[Qualys documentation](https://docs.qualys.com/)** — product and API documentation
- **Qualys Community** — discussion, QQL examples, and best practices

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** Certified Specialist **training and certification are free**; confirm any exam specifics on
  qualys.com.
- **Delivery:** free **self-paced or instructor-led** certified training with hands-on labs on the
  live platform.
- **Prerequisites:** foundational courses (e.g., VM Foundations, Policy Compliance Foundations)
  precede the specialist courses.
- **Validity and renewal:** revisit the continuously-updated free courses as the platform evolves.

## Platform areas

- **Qualys Cloud Platform** — one SaaS backend, one inventory, one query language (QQL).
- **Sensors** — Cloud Agents, scanner appliances, passive sensors, cloud connectors, API.
- **VMDR** — asset (CSAM) → detect → prioritize (TruRisk) → respond (Patch Management / TruRisk
  Eliminate).
- **Compliance** — Policy Compliance and PCI (with ASV scans).
- **Applications** — TotalAppSec (web/API), Container Security, File Integrity Monitoring.
- **Cloud & endpoint** — TotalCloud (CSPM/CNAPP), EDR.
- **Automation** — QFlow, CAR, QQL, API.

## Notes

- **Free training.** Qualys Certified Specialist courses are free — a low-barrier way to build and
  validate skills.
- **One platform.** VM, compliance, cloud, and EDR share one inventory, agent, and query language.
- **TruRisk.** Prioritization is risk-based via the TruRisk score; the platform is organized under
  Enterprise TruRisk.
- **Defensive scope.** Vulnerability, compliance, and exposure management is defensive — the volume's
  labs scan, assess, prioritize, and remediate authorized systems.
