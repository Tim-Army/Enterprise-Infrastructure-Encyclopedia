# Chapter 26: Appendix — Infoblox Certifications and Course Access

The **Infoblox** certification program — the NIOS DDI ladder, the Universal DDI and
Threat Defense microcredentials, NetMRI Administrator, and the vendor-agnostic Industry
Learning credentials — organized by family, with each credential's focus, topic areas,
and the training and delivery model. The lineup was verified on **27 July 2026** from
**launchpad.education.infoblox.com** — the same source that anchors
[Volume LVI — Infoblox Certification Tracks](../../volume-056-infoblox-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works at Infoblox.** Training is delivered through **Infoblox Education**
(the LaunchPad learning portal) — self-paced and instructor-led courses and hands-on
labs — with the certification catalog and **topic areas** published per credential.
Credentials are issued as **digital badges**. Practice happens on NIOS/Universal DDI/
Threat Defense lab environments.

> **Currency.** Infoblox revises the program as the platform evolves — the cloud-native
> **Universal DDI** (NIOS-X + the Infoblox Portal) and expanded **Threat Defense** added
> microcredentials alongside the classic **NIOS DDI** exams. Confirm the current catalog
> and any validity terms on education.infoblox.com before registering.

## Free resources and entry points

- **[Infoblox Education](https://www.infoblox.com/infoblox-education/)** and the
  **[LaunchPad portal](https://launchpad.education.infoblox.com/)** — the authoritative
  catalog and topic areas
- **Infoblox Education courses** — self-paced and instructor-led, with hands-on labs
- **Digital badges** — issued on passing, shareable credentials

## Fees, delivery, and topic areas

- **Fees (confirm at registration):** varies by exam/microcredential; some knowledge
  checks are free.
- **Delivery:** through Infoblox Education. The program publishes **topic areas** per
  credential but **not** question counts or percentage weightings.
- **Prerequisites:** the NIOS DDI ladder is progressive (Operator → Administrator →
  Expert); the microcredentials and Industry Learning credentials stand alone.
- **Credential type:** digital badges; confirm validity/renewal terms on the education
  site.

## Topic areas

- **INO:** Grid fundamentals; DHCP; DNS; IPAM; day-to-day object management.
- **INA:** Grid member management; advanced DHCP/DNS; IPAM; Discovery; access control;
  remote authentication.
- **INE:** Grid setup and high availability; upgrades; root-cause analysis; DNS/DHCP
  troubleshooting methodology.
- **Universal DDI:** Portal navigation; NIOS-X deployment; DNS/DHCP; resource records,
  views, zones; protocol redundancy.
- **Threat Defense:** components; DNS forwarding proxies; security-policy configuration;
  endpoint integration; threat analytics (Threat Insight).
- **NetMRI (IMA):** discovery/inventory; configuration and change management;
  compliance/policy; automation.
- **Industry Learning:** DDIA/DDIP (DNS/DHCP/IPAM fundamentals → advanced); DSA/DSP
  (DNS-based threats and mitigation).

## Notes

- **Topic areas are the study scope.** Infoblox publishes topic areas, not weightings —
  prepare across all of them.
- **NIOS vs Universal DDI.** NIOS is the on-prem Grid; Universal DDI is the cloud-native
  platform (Portal + NIOS-X) — study the right one.
- **Threat Defense is defensive.** DNS-layer security (blocking, analytics) for authorized
  defensive use.
- **Related practice** in the encyclopedia: network foundations in **Volume II**,
  cybersecurity in **Volume X**, and the network source of truth in **Volume LII
  (NetBox)**.
