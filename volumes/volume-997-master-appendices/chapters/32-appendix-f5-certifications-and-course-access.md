# Chapter 32: Appendix — F5 Certifications and Course Access

The **F5** certification program — the credentials for deploying, securing, and automating
**BIG-IP** (the application delivery and security platform running **TMOS**) — organized by level
and specialization, with each credential's exams and the training and delivery model. The program
was verified on **28 July 2026** from **education.f5.com** and **clouddocs.f5.com** — the same
sources that anchor
[Volume LXVI — F5 Certification Tracks](../../volume-066-f5-certifications/README.md). Third-party
exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** Training is delivered through **F5 Education Services** (instructor-led and
digital learning via education.f5.com), with free study resources in the F5 certification community
(clouddocs.f5.com) and product documentation on **my.f5.com**. Exams are delivered by **Pearson
VUE**. F5 certifications carry a validity period after which they are renewed.

> **Currency.** F5 **rebuilt the Administrator credential in 2025** into five short, focused exams
> (F5CAB1–F5CAB5), retiring the legacy 101 + 201 path, and continues to expand into **NGINX** and
> **F5 Distributed Cloud (XC)**. Confirm the current exams and recertification terms on
> education.f5.com / my.f5.com before registering.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[F5 Education Services](https://education.f5.com/)** — the authoritative learning paths and courses
- **[F5 Certification community](https://clouddocs.f5.com/training/community/f5cert/html/intro.html)**
  — the program overview and free study materials
- **[my.f5.com](https://my.f5.com/)** — the F5 knowledge base and product documentation
- **BIG-IP Virtual Edition (VE)** — a lab platform for hands-on practice in an authorized environment

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** per-exam fees via Pearson VUE; course fees separately. Confirm current pricing on the
  portal.
- **Delivery:** exams at **Pearson VUE**; training through F5 Education Services.
- **Prerequisites:** the **F5-CA** foundation precedes the CTS specializations; the **CSE Security**
  requires the F5-CA plus all four CTS.
- **Validity and renewal:** certifications expire and are renewed per F5 policy; confirm the current
  period on the portal.

## The certification map

Verified against education.f5.com and clouddocs.f5.com on 28 July 2026.

| Credential | Level | Exams |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F5 Certified Administrator, BIG-IP (F5-CA) | Administrator | F5CAB1, F5CAB2, F5CAB3, F5CAB4, F5CAB5 (2025 five-exam path) |
| F5 Certified Technology Specialist — LTM | Specialist | 301a (Architect, Setup, Deploy) + 301b (Maintain & Troubleshoot) |
| F5 Certified Technology Specialist — DNS | Specialist | 302 |
| F5 Certified Technology Specialist — Advanced WAF (ASM) | Specialist | 303 |
| F5 Certified Technology Specialist — APM | Specialist | 304 |
| F5 Certified Solution Expert — Security (F5-CSE) | Expert | 401 (requires F5-CA + all four CTS) |

The five Administrator exams are: **F5CAB1** Install/Initial Configuration/Upgrade; **F5CAB2** Data
Plane Concepts; **F5CAB3** Data Plane Configuration; **F5CAB4** Control Plane Administration;
**F5CAB5** Support and Troubleshooting.

## Specialization focus

- **Administrator (F5-CA):** TMOS architecture, install/config/upgrade, base networking (VLANs,
  self-IPs), virtual servers/pools/profiles, HA/config sync, backups, and troubleshooting.
- **LTM:** advanced load balancing, persistence, iRules, OneConnect, and SSL.
- **DNS:** BIG-IP DNS/GTM, wide IPs, GSLB methods, and topology.
- **Advanced WAF (ASM):** WAF policy (signatures + positive security), bot defense, and tuning.
- **APM:** access policy (VPE), authentication, SSO, ZTNA, and per-request policy.
- **Solution Expert (Security):** integrating LTM, DNS, Advanced WAF, and APM.

## Notes
