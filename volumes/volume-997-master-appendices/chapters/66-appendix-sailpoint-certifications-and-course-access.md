# Chapter 66: Appendix — SailPoint Certifications and Course Access

The **SailPoint** certification program — the two credential tracks, training paths, and access model.
Verified on **4 August 2026** from **university.sailpoint.com** (Identity University), the source that
anchors [Volume CXXXII — SailPoint Certification Tracks](../../volume-132-sailpoint-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** SailPoint runs the **Professional Certification and Credentialing Program** inside
**Identity University**, with **two distinct tracks**:

- **Knowledge Credentials** — gated on completing a training path, then taken **online and immediately**
  (no scheduling). They are **adaptive** (the exam may end early once the pass/fail threshold is reached),
  must be taken in **one sitting**, and the first attempts are **free**. Badges **never expire**.
- **Professional Certifications** — **proctored**, paid, role-based credentials for experienced
  practitioners. Enrollment gives **364 days** to schedule and includes **two attempts**; status reads
  Passed, Not Complete, or Not Passed. These renew under the **Recertification Program**.

Badges issue via Identity University and **Credly** (the public
[certified professionals directory](https://www.credly.com/organizations/sailpoint/directory)).

> **Currency.** In **February 2026** SailPoint launched the **Recertification Program** (extends a
> certification **two years** through training, projects, events, and similar activity rather than a
> re-sit) together with a new certification, the **SailPoint Certified Identity Security Administrator** —
> bringing the program to **seven exams**. The community exceeds **12,000 certified professionals**, having
> **quadrupled** over the preceding year. A new product path, **SailPoint Agentic Fabric** (identity
> security for agentic AI), has training but no separate certification yet. Re-verify the catalog on
> university.sailpoint.com before planning.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[Identity University](https://university.sailpoint.com/)** — the authoritative training/certification hub
- **[Certifications and credentials catalog](https://university.sailpoint.com/page/professional-certifications-and-knowledge-credentials)** — every exam, with prep guides
- **Free [Identity Security Leader](https://university.sailpoint.com/sailpoint-identity-security-leader-credential) path** — product-agnostic foundation, **3 free attempts**, badge never expires
- **[Recertification program](https://community.sailpoint.com/t5/SailPoint-Recertification/ct-p/recertification-program)** and **[certification community](https://community.sailpoint.com/t5/SailPoint-Certifications/ct-p/product-certification-program)** (Compass)
- **Prep materials** (per exam page): the Exam Prep Guide, the Test Taker's Guide, and the Program Policy Handbook
- **Free study lab:** any host with `python3` models the IGA disciplines — identity correlation, role mining, joiner-mover-leaver provisioning, separation-of-duties policy, certification campaigns, and transforms (see the volume's labs); no SailPoint license needed

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** Knowledge Credentials are **free** for the first attempts after completing the training path
  (3 free for Leader, 2 free for Professional and Expert). Professional Certifications are **$300**
  (IdentityIQ Associate) or **$400** (the other three) — confirm current pricing.
- **Delivery:** credentials are online, immediate, adaptive, one sitting; certifications are **proctored**
  and scheduled within a **364-day** enrollment window, with **two attempts** included.
- **Prerequisites:** none formal. Experience is *recommended* — 6 months for Identity Security
  Administrator, 1 year for Identity Security Engineer and both IdentityIQ exams. Credentials require
  completing their training path first.
- **Validity/renewal:** Knowledge Credential badges **never expire**; Professional Certifications renew
  every **two years** through the Recertification Program.

## Notes

- **Two tracks are the program's defining feature:** free, never-expiring Knowledge Credentials for
  learning, and proctored, recertifying Professional Certifications for proven role experience. Studying
  the free ladder first is both cheaper and genuinely good preparation.
- **The Engineer/Administrator split is Architecture + Rules and Transforms** — the same material the
  Identity Security Expert credential covers.
- **IdentityIQ remains a first-class track:** two of the four certifications target the on-premises
  product, whose blueprint adds installation, build/deploy, and troubleshooting.
- **SailPoint completes the identity trio** in this encyclopedia alongside
  [Okta LXXVI](../../volume-076-okta-certifications/README.md) (access management) and
  [CyberArk LXXVII](../../volume-077-cyberark-certifications/README.md) (privileged access): SailPoint is
  the **governance** discipline that decides what access is appropriate and proves it.
