# Chapter 19: Appendix — Splunk Certifications and Course Access

The **Splunk** (a **Cisco company**) certification program — the SPL,
administration, architecture, security, and observability credentials — organized
by track, with each certification's blueprint focus, prerequisites, and delivery.
The lineup and blueprint topic areas were harvested on **26 July 2026** from
**splunk.com** — the same source that anchors
[Volume XLV — Splunk Certification Tracks](../../volume-45-splunk-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works at Splunk.** Official training is delivered through **Splunk
Education** — free **eLearning** (including a Power User Fast Start) and paid
instructor-led courses — plus free **test blueprints** and a **free Splunk
Enterprise trial** and **Splunk Cloud trial** for hands-on practice. Exams are
delivered through **Pearson VUE**, and credentials appear as **Credly** digital
badges.

> **Currency.** Splunk updates its blueprints and tracks — confirm the current
> blueprint and prerequisites on splunk.com before registering. Recent changes
> captured here: Splunk's acquisition by **Cisco (2024)**, the new **Core Certified
> Advanced Power User**, and the full **Cybersecurity Defense** track (Analyst,
> Engineer, Architect), alongside the Observability (O11y) Metrics User.

## Free training and entry points

- **[Splunk Training & Certification](https://www.splunk.com/en_us/training/certification-track.html)** —
  the authoritative track and certification pages
- **Test blueprints** — free PDFs per exam (weighted topic areas), at
  `splunk.com/en_us/pdfs/training/splunk-test-blueprint-<name>.pdf`
- **Splunk Education eLearning** — free foundational courses (incl. Power User Fast
  Start)
- **Free trials** — Splunk Enterprise (60-day) and Splunk Cloud trials for hands-on
  practice
- **SPL documentation** and the **Common Information Model (CIM)** reference

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fee band (confirm at registration):** the registration/entry exams (Core User)
  are the lowest; Power User, Advanced Power User, and the Admin/Security exams sit
  in the mid band; Architect/Consultant and the specialist exams are the highest.
  Some entry exams are bundled with free Fast Start eLearning.
- **Delivery:** **Pearson VUE** (test center or online-proctored), governed by a
  published **test blueprint** per exam.
- **Prerequisites:** SPL-based — **Power User** is the prerequisite for Advanced
  Power User and Admin; higher tracks build on the Admin/Analyst foundations.
- **Validity and renewal:** certifications are valid for a defined period (commonly
  **three years**) and renewed by **retaking the current exam** or earning a
  **higher** certification on the track — no continuing-education-credit model.

## Notes

- **SPL is the foundation.** Every track builds on the Core (SPL) skills — invest
  there first.
- **Blueprints are the study scope.** Download the current test blueprint per exam
  from splunk.com; the program adds and updates tracks (Advanced Power User and the
  Cybersecurity Defense track are recent).
- **Renew by exam.** Splunk has no CE model — re-test or advance the track before a
  credential lapses.
- **Practice on a trial.** Use the free Splunk Enterprise or Splunk Cloud trial to
  run the SPL and administration labs.
- **Related practice** in the encyclopedia: observability in **Volume XI**,
  security in **Volume X** and the vendor-security volumes, and visibility in
  **Volumes XVIII (Gigamon)** and **XX (Wireshark)**.
