# Volume XXXIX Glossary

Definitions for terms used in **Volume XXXIX — CompTIA Certification Tracks**,
alphabetized. See also the [volume index](INDEX.md) and the
[master glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md) for cross-volume terminology.

**A+ Core Series** — The two-exam entry credential for IT support (**220-1201**
Core 1 and **220-1202** Core 2); both must be passed to certify. Used in
Chapter 02.

**CE program (Continuing Education)** — CompTIA's mechanism for renewing a
certification without re-testing: earn Continuing Education Units over the
three-year cycle and pay the annual fee. Used in Chapter 08.

**CEU (Continuing Education Unit)** — The unit of renewal credit earned by
training, earning higher certifications, teaching, publishing, or attending
industry activities; higher-level certs require more per cycle. Used in Chapter
08.

**CloudNetX** — The flagship expert-level Xpert certification (exam **CNX-001**)
uniting cloud and network architecture across hybrid and multi-cloud
environments. Used in Chapter 06.

**CompCert (Competency Certificate)** — A CompTIA Certificate of Competency
earned by passing a course's built-in competency assessment; it validates
**specific skills** rather than a job role, as third-party-verifiable proof of
skill. Awarded by the Essentials courses (and the AI Agent Simulator work), not
a proctored coded exam. Used in Chapters 05 and 07.

**Competency assessment** — The validation attached to a CompTIA training course
(the "Pro" courses such as Hybrid Server Pro and CyberDefense Pro, and the
Essentials courses); unlike a certification exam it is not exam-coded. "Pro"
courses ladder toward a separate certification; Essentials courses award a
CompCert. Used in Chapters 05–07.

**DoD 8140/8570** — The U.S. Department of Defense directive that lists baseline
certifications (including A+, Network+, and Security+) required for information-
assurance roles. Used in Chapters 02 and 09.

**Expansion Series** — CompTIA's newer security certification line for emerging
risk areas: **SecAI+ (CY0-001)** and **SecOT+ (SOT-001)**, distinct from the
Xpert Series and using a scaled score. Used in Chapter 06.

**ISO/ANSI accreditation** — The independent accreditation (ISO/IEC 17024) that
many CompTIA certifications hold, signaling that the credential meets a
recognized quality standard. Used in Chapters 01 and 09.

**ITF+ → Tech+** — The rename of IT Fundamentals (ITF+) to **CompTIA Tech+**
(exam **FC0-U71**), the foundational digital-literacy credential. Used in
Chapter 02.

**Performance-based question (PBQ)** — An exam item that requires performing a
task in a simulated environment rather than answering multiple choice; rewards
hands-on practice. Used in Chapters 01 and 02.

**"Plus" certification** — The classic exam-based CompTIA credentials whose
names end in "+" (A+, Network+, Security+, Cloud+, Linux+, Server+, CySA+,
PenTest+, Data+, DataSys+, Project+; the business-cloud **Cloud Essentials+**
retired 25 September 2025), validating knowledge and scenario judgment. Used
throughout.

**"Pro" course** — A hands-on CompTIA CertMaster training course paired with a
competency assessment (Hybrid Server Pro, CyberDefense Pro, Ethical Hacker Pro,
Linux Pro, Security Pro) that builds skills and ladders toward a CompTIA or
vendor certification, rather than being a standalone exam. Distinct from the
exam-coded Xpert (CloudNetX, DataAI) and Expansion (SecAI+, SecOT+)
certifications. Used in Chapter 06.

**SecAI+** — CompTIA's AI-security certification (exam **CY0-001**, V1, GA
February 2026), the first in the Expansion Series; a scaled-score exam (passing
600 on 100–900) for securing AI systems and using AI in security operations.
Used in Chapter 06.

**SecOT+** — CompTIA's operational-technology (OT) security certification (exam
**SOT-001**, V1), an Expansion Series credential launching December 2026 with
six weighted domains (objectives in draft). Used in Chapter 06.

**SecurityX** — The advanced, master-level security certification (exam
**CAS-005**), renamed from **CASP+** (CompTIA Advanced Security Practitioner),
validated with hands-on questions. Used in Chapter 04.

**Stackable certification** — A credential that combines with others in a track
so that earning or renewing a higher certification renews the lower ones; the
basis of efficient renewal. Used in Chapters 01 and 08.

**Tech+** — The foundational technology and digital-literacy certification (exam
**FC0-U71**), the entry point for people new to IT (formerly ITF+). Used in
Chapter 02.

**Vendor-neutral** — Certifying concepts and skills that apply across products
and vendors, rather than one company's platform; CompTIA's defining
characteristic and the foundation beneath the vendor volumes. Used in Chapters
01 and 09.

**Xpert Series** — CompTIA's line of hands-on, performance-based "Pro"
credentials introduced in the 2024–2025 relaunch, sitting alongside the classic
"plus" exams. Used in Chapter 06.
