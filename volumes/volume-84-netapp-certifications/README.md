# Volume LXXXIV — NetApp Certification Tracks

> The whole NetApp certification program in one volume — the ONTAP, Hybrid Cloud, Cloud Services,
> Installation, Support, FlexPod, AI, and Cyber Resilience paths, from Certified Professional through
> Specialist to Expert — with hands-on ONTAP walkthroughs, verified against netapp.com.

## Overview

Volume LXXXIV maps the **NetApp** certification program — the credentials for administering,
implementing, and architecting NetApp **ONTAP** storage and the hybrid-cloud data fabric around it. The
paths run from the entry **Certified Professional** tier (Technology Solutions and the flagship **Data
Administrator ONTAP / NCDA, NS0-163**) through the **Certified Specialist** engineering credentials
(**Implementation Engineer — SAN, NS0-521**; **Data Protection, NS0-528**; **MetroCluster**; the
Cisco-and-NetApp **FlexPod** specialists) to the **Certified Expert** tier (**Hybrid Cloud Architect,
NS0-604**; **AI Expert**; **AI Data Infrastructure**; **Cyber Resiliency**). It opens the encyclopedia's
Data, storage & backup cluster.

This is a **certification-tracks** volume, like the other vendor volumes (XXXIX–LXXXIII): it maps the
program — the paths, levels, and credentials — and teaches each with a hands-on ONTAP walkthrough. Every
path and exam was **verified against netapp.com on 29 July 2026** (the program hub and the individual
certification pages; third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources).

Chapters follow the certification ladder:

- **Chapter 01** frames the program — the paths, the Professional/Specialist/Expert tiers, the Associate accreditations, Credly badges, Pearson VUE delivery, and recertification.
- **Chapter 02** takes **Technology Solutions** and the Associate foundations — the cluster, licensing, aggregates, and RAID-DP resiliency.
- **Chapter 03** takes the **Data Administrator ONTAP (NCDA)** core — storage virtual machines, volumes, and storage efficiency.
- **Chapter 04** takes **networking and protocols** — LIFs/IPspaces, NAS (NFS/SMB), SAN (iSCSI/FC), and ONTAP S3.
- **Chapter 05** takes the **Implementation Engineer** specialists — SAN, Data Protection (SnapMirror/SnapVault), and MetroCluster.
- **Chapter 06** takes the **Hybrid Cloud** and **Cloud Services** paths — FabricPool tiering, StorageGRID, and SnapMirror to the cloud.
- **Chapter 07** takes **Installation and Support** — cluster setup, AutoSupport, performance triage, and E-Series.
- **Chapter 08** takes **FlexPod** (Cisco and NetApp converged infrastructure) and security hardening.
- **Chapter 09** takes **AI, Cyber Resilience, and career** — ONTAP AI data infrastructure and Autonomous Ransomware Protection.

Every chapter follows the standard structure defined in
[templates/chapter.md](../../templates/chapter.md) and enforced by
[EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md](../../EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md), including per-topic hands-on labs and
knowledge checks.

> **Scope.** NetApp storage administration, data protection, and cyber resilience are authorized,
> defensive platform work — configuring, protecting, and recovering your own storage. The Cyber
> Resiliency material (Autonomous Ransomware Protection, immutable Snapshots, SnapLock, encryption) is
> framed as defensive protection of your own data.
