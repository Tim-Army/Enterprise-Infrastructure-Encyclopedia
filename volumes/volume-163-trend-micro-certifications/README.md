## Overview

Trend Micro is a **veteran cybersecurity vendor** that has evolved from antivirus into a platform-centric
company. Its unified platform, **Trend Vision One**, combines **XDR** (extended detection and response),
**Attack Surface Risk Management (ASRM)**, and threat intelligence across endpoints, servers, cloud, email,
network, and identity. Trend Micro's breadth — endpoint (Apex One), server/cloud workload (Deep Security),
email and network — makes it a broad defensive-security player, a peer of the endpoint vendors
([Sophos CLXII](../volume-162-sophos-certifications/README.md),
[SentinelOne CLI](../volume-151-sentinelone-certifications/README.md),
[CrowdStrike L](../volume-050-crowdstrike-certifications/README.md)).

Chapter 02 covers **Trend Vision One** (the unified platform). Chapters 03–08 take the platform: **XDR**
(cross-layer detection and response), **Apex One** (endpoint protection and EDR), **Deep Security and cloud
workload protection**, **Attack Surface Risk Management** (proactive risk), **email and network security**
(Cloud App Security, TippingPoint, Deep Discovery), and **threat intelligence and zero trust** (Smart
Protection Network, ZTSA). Chapter 09 closes on choosing a path.

A theme runs through it: **protect** (layered defense across every layer), **detect and respond** (XDR
correlation), and **reduce risk** (ASRM) — informed by global **threat intelligence**, on one platform.

## What you will be able to do

- Read the Trend Micro Certified Professional program and its scenario-based exam format.
- Explain Trend Vision One — the unified platform (XDR + ASRM + threat intelligence).
- Apply XDR — correlating cross-layer telemetry into attack stories and responding completely.
- Operate Apex One endpoint protection — layered detection and virtual patching.
- Protect workloads with Deep Security across physical, virtual, cloud, and container — plus CSPM.
- Apply Attack Surface Risk Management — discovering, risk-scoring, and reducing the attack surface.
- Defend email and network with Cloud App Security, TippingPoint, and Deep Discovery as XDR sensors.
- Use the Smart Protection Network threat intelligence and Zero Trust Secure Access.

## Prerequisites

- Familiarity with endpoint, server, cloud, and network security concepts helps.
- A Linux or macOS host with `python3`. **Trend Micro training** is available on-demand and instructor-led via Trend Micro Education.
