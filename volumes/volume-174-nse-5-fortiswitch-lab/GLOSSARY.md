# Volume CLXXIV Glossary

Definitions for terms introduced in **Volume CLXXIV — NSE 5 FortiSwitch Lab (Real Switch and FortiGate-Only)**.
See the [master glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md) for cross-volume terms.

- **802.1p (CoS)** — the three-bit priority field (class of service, 0–7) in an IEEE 802.1Q VLAN tag; a FortiSwitch port that trusts 802.1p maps it to an egress queue with a dot1p map.
- **802.1X** — the IEEE standard for port-based network access control: a supplicant on the endpoint authenticates through the switch port (the authenticator) to an authentication server, normally RADIUS, before the port passes its traffic.
- **802.1X security policy** — a FortiOS object (`config switch-controller security-policy 802-1X`), the FortiSwitch "security profile", that sets the mode, user group, MAB, guest and auth-fail VLANs, and timeouts, and is applied to a managed port with `port-security-policy`.
- **Access VLAN** — in FortiSwitch terms, a VLAN with intra-VLAN traffic blocked (`switch-controller-access-vlan enable` on the FortiGate, `access-vlan enable` on the switch), such as the quarantine VLAN; feature matrix note 7 limits the FS-1xxE, FS-108F, and FS-124F to 16 of them.
- **access-vlan-mode** — a per-FortiLink-interface setting (`config switch-controller fortilink-settings`) that decides whether intra-VLAN traffic is allowed (`fail-open`) or blocked (`fail-close`) when the switches lose the FortiGate; `legacy` is the default.
- **ACL group** — a FortiOS object (`config switch-controller acl group`) that lists ACL ingress policies by ID and is bound to managed-switch ports with `acl-group`; on the switch its policies go into ACL group 3.
- **ACL ingress policy** — a FortiOS object (`config switch-controller acl ingress`) with a classifier (MAC addresses, VLAN, IPv4 prefixes) and actions (`drop`, `count`) that a managed FortiSwitch applies to traffic arriving on a port; the only ACL stage available in FortiLink mode (feature matrix note 16).
- **ACL remark action** — a standalone FortiSwitchOS ingress ACL action (`remark-cos`, `remark-dscp`) that rewrites the 802.1Q CoS or the DSCP value of matching packets; QoS marking, a 200 Series standalone feature, which does not change the marking switch's own queuing.
- **ACL service (FortiSwitchOS)** — a named protocol and port definition (`config switch acl service custom`) that a standalone ACL classifier matches with `set service`.
- **Advanced Features license (FortiSwitch)** — the FortiSwitchOS license under which the 7.6.7 feature matrix lists OSPF, RIP, VRRP, BGP, and policy-based routing; none of them is available on the FS-108F or FS-124F.
- **allow-traffic-redirect** — a FortiOS global setting (`config system global`), enabled by default, that forwards traffic leaving on the interface it arrived on without a policy check; the FortiLink Guide requires it disabled when intra-VLAN traffic is blocked.
- **Allowed DHCP server list** — the DHCP-snooping option (`dhcp-server-access-list` globally and per switch, `config dhcp-snooping-server-list` on the VLAN) that lets only listed servers answer clients, even on trusted ports.
- **Allowed VLANs** — the VLANs a managed-switch port carries tagged in addition to its native VLAN (`allowed-vlans`); `set` replaces the list and `append` adds to it.
- **assign-vlan** — an LLDP-MED network policy option (`config med-network-policy` in an LLDP profile) that makes the switch add the policy's VLAN to the allowed VLANs of every port using the profile.
- **Auth-fail VLAN** — the VLAN (`auth-fail-vlan`, `auth-fail-vlan-id`) in which an 802.1X security policy places clients that tried to authenticate and failed.
- **Authenticator** — the 802.1X role that controls access to a port and relays EAP between supplicant and server; on a managed FortiSwitch it is the switch port, which also sends the RADIUS requests.
- **Authorization (FortiSwitch)** — setting `fsw-wan1-admin enable` on a managed-switch entry so that the controller manages the switch; the switch then reboots in FortiLink mode.
- **Auto topology (auto-network)** — the FortiSwitchOS 7.2.0 and later default (`config switch auto-network`) that forms ISLs automatically and sets the native VLAN of ISL ports and of `internal`; every factory reset enables it again.
- **Auto-created VLANs** — the VLANs FortiOS creates from its initial-config templates when the switch controller is enabled: default (1), quarantine (4093), rspan (4092), voice (4091), video (4090), onboarding (4089, for NAC), and the NAC segment.
- **Auto-ISL trunk** — a trunk that a managed FortiSwitch forms on its own toward its FortiLink peer or another FortiSwitch; `diagnose switch trunk summary` shows `auto-isl` in its mode.
- **auto-isl-port-group** — a FortiSwitchOS setting (`config switch auto-isl-port-group`), configured on the switch itself, that groups the ports toward a lower-tier MCLAG pair into one MCLAG when the ISLs form; used for tier-2 and tier-3 MCLAGs.
- **Backup port** — an STP port role for a port connected to another port of the same switch (directly or through shared media); one of the ports is designated and the others are backup ports, blocking.
- **BPDU (bridge protocol data unit)** — the frame that spanning tree bridges exchange to elect a root and assign port roles.
- **BPDU guard** — a per-port protection (`stp-bpdu-guard`) that takes an edge port down for `stp-bpdu-guard-timeout` minutes (default 5; 0 means until reset) when any BPDU arrives; it needs `edge-port` and `stp-state` enabled.
- **C1, C2, C3** — this volume's controller options: a hardware FortiGate 40F or 60F (C1), the FGT-1 FortiGate-VM evaluation (C2), and a licensed FortiGate-VM (C3).
- **Cable test (TDR)** — a time-domain reflectometer test of a copper switch port's pairs (`Open`, `Short`, `Ok`, and others, with a length); it interrupts traffic on a port whose link is up.
- **Capability matrix** — Chapter 01's table of which labs each switch option (and Track 2) runs hands-on, checked against the FortiSwitchOS 7.6.7 feature matrix.
- **CIST (common and internal spanning tree)** — the single spanning tree formed by the common spanning tree between regions and the internal spanning tree inside each region; its root is the CIST root.
- **Configuration synchronization** — the controller keeping a managed switch's configuration in step with its own; flag `C` in `get-conn-status` marks a synchronization and `E` an error, and `diagnose switch-controller trigger config-sync` forces one.
- **Custom command** — FortiSwitchOS CLI text stored on the controller (`config switch-controller custom-command`, `%0a` for line breaks) and executed once on a switch or bound to its entry.
- **Default auto-FortiLink ports** — the switch ports the FortiLink Guide 7.6.7 lists, per model, as FortiLink auto-discovery ports at factory defaults (for example `port7`–`port10` on the FS-108F and `port21`–`port28` on the FS-124F); this volume takes `<FSW_FL_PORT>` from that list.
- **Default CoS** — the CoS value a FortiSwitch port assumes for untagged frames (`default-cos`, 0 by default); it is used only when the port trusts 802.1p, and some 200 and 400 Series models do not support it.
- **default-virtual-switch-vlan** — a FortiOS setting under `config switch-controller global` in a tenant VDOM that names the VLAN that becomes the native VLAN of switch ports exported to that VDOM.
- **Device detection** — the FortiGate's identification of devices behind a managed switch, from its own traffic fingerprinting (`device-identification`) and the switch's MAC cache, LLDP, DHCP-snooping, and network-monitor databases (`update-user-device`); read with `diagnose user device list`.
- **DHCP snooping** — a switch feature, enabled per VLAN, that accepts DHCP server messages only on trusted ports and builds a binding database of MAC address, VLAN, IP address, lease, and port for DAI and IP source guard.
- **DHCP-snooping static entry** — a manual binding of one IPv4 address to one MAC address on one port and VLAN (`config dhcp-snooping-static-client` under the managed-switch entry), so that DAI accepts a statically addressed host; at most 64 per switch.
- **disable-discovery** — the list under `config switch-controller global` of switch serial numbers for which FortiLink auto-discovery is disabled; changed with `append` and `unselect`.
- **discard-mode** — a managed-switch port option that discards all tagged (`all-tagged`) or all untagged (`all-untagged`) incoming frames; `none` is the default.
- **Dot1p map** — a QoS map (`config switch-controller qos dot1p-map` on the FortiGate, `config switch qos dot1p-map` on the switch) from 802.1p priorities 0–7 to egress queues; unlisted priorities go to queue 0.
- **DSCP (Differentiated Services Code Point)** — the six-bit priority field (0–63) in the IP header, for example EF (46) for voice and CS3 (24) for signaling; tools such as `iperf -S` set it through the type-of-service byte (DSCP times four).
- **DSCP map** — a QoS map (`config switch-controller qos ip-dscp-map`) whose entries send DSCP values, DiffServ names, or IP precedence values to egress queues; unlisted values go to queue 0.
- **Dual-homed FortiLink** — a FortiLink aggregate with one member cabled to each of two switches in the stack; without an MCLAG, `fortilink-split-interface enable` keeps one link active and the other standby so that the ISL does not form a loop.
- **Dynamic ARP inspection (DAI)** — a switch feature that drops ARP packets on untrusted ports whose sender IP and MAC addresses do not match a DHCP-snooping binding; it needs DHCP snooping on the VLAN.
- **Dynamic firewall address (NAC)** — a firewall address of `type dynamic` and `sub-type swc-tag` that a NAC policy fills with the MAC addresses of the devices it matched, so firewall policies can refer to them.
- **Dynamic port policy (DPP)** — a FortiOS object (`config switch-controller dynamic-port-policy`) whose ordered rules match a device on a port with `access-mode dynamic` and apply a VLAN policy, LLDP profile, QoS policy, or 802.1X policy to that port.
- **Dynamic VLAN** — a VLAN that RADIUS returns in its Access-Accept with `Tunnel-Type`, `Tunnel-Medium-Type`, and `Tunnel-Private-Group-Id`; port-based mode changes the port's native VLAN, MAC-based mode assigns it to the client's MAC address only.
- **EAP (Extensible Authentication Protocol)** — the framework that carries 802.1X authentication between supplicant and server; the managed FortiSwitch supports EAP-PEAP, EAP-TTLS, and EAP-TLS, but not EAP-MD5.
- **Edge port** — a switch port with no bridge behind it (`edge-port`), which goes straight to forwarding; the FortiOS 7.6.0 default for managed ports is `enable`.
- **Egress queue** — one of the eight output queues (`queue-0` lowest to `queue-7` highest) on each FortiSwitch port; queue 7 carries the switch's own control traffic and should not carry data.
- **ERSPAN** — encapsulated remote SPAN: a FortiSwitch mirror that sends copies of traffic in GRE to a collector IP address (`traffic-sniffer`, `set mode erspan-auto`); not available on the FS-1xx models and not combinable with SPAN or RSPAN.
- **Evaluation license (FortiGate-VM permanent trial)** — the free FortiGate-VM license, capped at three interfaces, three firewall policies, three routes, and two VDOMs of which only one can be a traffic VDOM, with low encryption only.
- **Exported port** — a managed-switch port given to a tenant VDOM with `export-to` (or claimed from a virtual port pool); the tenant configures it, and the controlling VDOM's view shows only the export.
- **Feature matrix** — Fortinet's per-release table of FortiSwitchOS features by model series; a chain icon means standalone and managed mode, a check mark standalone mode only, and a dash not supported. Its first mark column is GUI support, not a model.
- **Flow export (flow tracking)** — sampling of IP packets on managed FortiSwitch ports and export of the flow records in NetFlow 1, 5, 9, or IPFIX format to one or more collectors (`config switch-controller flow-tracking`).
- **FortiEdge Cloud** — Fortinet's cloud service for managing FortiSwitch units without a FortiGate; the switch side is the `config system flan-cloud` client (FortiLAN Cloud in the FortiSwitchOS 7.6.7 GUI and CLI), and the account side is done only in the FortiCloud portal.
- **FortiLink** — the protocol and link by which a FortiGate discovers, authorizes, and manages FortiSwitch units.
- **FortiLink go/no-go test** — Chapter 02's test of whether a controller can discover, authorize, and keep a switch Authorized/Up; required on C2, because FortiLink on the FortiGate-VM evaluation license is unconfirmed.
- **FortiLink interface** — the FortiGate interface (here alias `fortilink`, `10.255.1.1/24`) enabled for FortiLink; the managed switch's VLANs are subinterfaces of it.
- **FortiLink LAG** — an 802.3ad aggregate FortiLink interface whose members all connect to the same FortiSwitch, so that all links carry traffic; it requires `fortilink-split-interface disable`.
- **FortiLink management VLAN** — the VLAN that carries a managed switch's management traffic on the FortiLink interface, set by `switch-controller-mgmt-vlan`; 4094 by default.
- **FortiLink MCLAG** — a dual-homed FortiLink whose two switches form an MCLAG peer group, so that the split interface can be disabled and both FortiGate links are active.
- **FortiLink mode (managed mode)** — a FortiSwitch configured entirely from a FortiGate acting as switch controller.
- **FortiLink neighbor detection** — how a FortiGate detects FortiSwitch neighbors on a FortiLink interface, set by `fortilink-neighbor-detect` to `lldp` or `fortilink`; LLDP is the default from FortiOS 7.6.1.
- **FortiLink over Layer 3 (FortiSwitch island)** — managing one or more FortiSwitch units that reach the FortiGate across a routed network (DHCP option 138 or static discovery); FortiSwitch NAC is not supported and no VLAN spans the Layer 3 network to the FortiGate.
- **FortiLink secure fabric** — LLDP-profile settings (`auto-isl-auth`, `auto-isl-auth-encrypt`) that authenticate, and optionally encrypt with MACsec, inter-switch links between managed switches; the FortiGate-to-switch link stays unencrypted.
- **FortiLink split interface** — the `fortilink-split-interface` setting of an aggregate FortiLink interface (enabled by default) that keeps one member active and the rest standby; with `lldp` neighbor detection the standby link stays up with LACP inactive.
- **fortilink-p2p** — a FortiSwitch port setting that runs an ISL over a point-to-point Layer 2 network, such as a wireless bridge, by sending LLDP to the broadcast MAC address; `fortilink-p2p-tpid` sets the TPID in FortiLink mode.
- **fortilinkify.py** — the Fortinet Developer Network utility that the FortiLink Guide offers for translating standalone FortiSwitch configurations into FortiGate configuration before the switches are converted to FortiLink mode.
- **FortiSwitch VLAN** — a VLAN interface on the FortiLink interface (`config system interface` with `interface` set to the FortiLink port and a `vlanid`); the FortiGate holds its address, DHCP, routing, and policy, and the switch forwards it at Layer 2.
- **FortiView Internal Hubs** — a FortiOS monitor of connections between private addresses behind managed switches, fed by flow export to the FortiLink interface; it needs a FortiGate with a hard disk and is not available on the FS-1xx models.
- **FreeRADIUS** — an open-source RADIUS server; this volume's `radius-174` VM (`10.200.0.174`) runs it for the controller's and the switch's RADIUS tests.
- **FSW-LAN** — this volume's zone holding the lab VLANs 1741–1744 (`fsw-users`, `fsw-voice`, `fsw-iot`, `fsw-guest`), with `intrazone deny`, so one policy entry covers all four.
- **fsw-wan1-admin** — the managed-switch attribute that holds the authorization state: `discovered` (waiting, the default), `disable` (unauthorized), or `enable` (authorized).
- **get-conn-status** — `execute switch-controller get-conn-status`, the controller's table of managed switches with their version, status (for example `Discovered/Down` or `Authorized/Up`), flags, address, and join time; FortiSwitchOS has a switch-side command of the same name.
- **group_fwd_mask** — a Linux bridge bitmask that lets the bridge forward frames sent to link-local addresses `01:80:C2:00:00:0X`, bit X per address; 16384 (bit 14) forwards LLDP.
- **Guest VLAN** — the VLAN (`guest-vlan`, `guest-vlan-id`) in which an 802.1X security policy places clients that do not answer 802.1X within `guest-auth-delay` seconds (default 30).
- **ICL (inter-chassis link)** — the trunk between the two switches of an MCLAG peer group, formed in FortiLink mode by giving the ISL ports the `default-auto-mclag-icl` LLDP profile; the FortiLink Guide asks for at least two links.
- **IGMP snooping** — a switch feature that listens to IGMP and forwards each IPv4 multicast group only to ports with a listener; enabled per VLAN from the FortiGate (`switch-controller-igmp-snooping`), and on at most five VLANs on the FS-1xx models (feature matrix note 23).
- **IGMP-snooping querier** — a managed switch sending IGMP queries in a VLAN with no external querier, so hosts keep reporting and groups do not age out; it needs snooping and the IGMP-snooping proxy on the VLAN and `local-override` on the switch.
- **Initial-config template** — a FortiOS template (`config switch-controller initial-config template`) from which the switch controller creates a FortiLink VLAN interface for one traffic type; templates can be created only before any switch is authorized.
- **inner-tunnel (FreeRADIUS)** — the FreeRADIUS virtual server that checks the user inside a PEAP or TTLS tunnel; its reply attributes reach the outer Access-Accept only when its `post-auth` copy block is enabled (`if (1)`).
- **Inter-VLAN routing offload** — a FortiOS 7.4.1 and later feature in which the controller programs a managed switch to route trusted traffic between chosen VLANs without FortiGate inspection; it needs an advanced features license and is supported only on the 10xx, 2048F, and 3032 models in the 7.6.7 feature matrix.
- **Internal interface (FortiSwitch)** — the switch's own interface into its switching fabric (`internal`), which carries the management address on models without a dedicated management port and is the parent of SVIs.
- **intrazone** — the `config system zone` setting that allows or denies traffic between interfaces of the same zone; the default is `deny`.
- **IP source guard** — a per-port switch feature that forwards IPv4 traffic only from source addresses bound by DHCP snooping or by a static binding entry; supported on the 200 Series, not on the FS-108F or FS-124F.
- **ISL (inter-switch link)** — a trunk between two managed FortiSwitch units; it forms automatically when a switch is cabled to any port of a switch already in FortiLink mode whose LLDP profile allows auto-ISL, or is created by hand as a static ISL.
- **IST (internal spanning tree)** — MSTP instance 0, which connects all switches of a region, holds every unmapped VLAN, and is the only instance that sends BPDUs.
- **LACP fallback mode** — a FortiOS 7.4.4 and later LAG option (`fallback-port`) that keeps one member up for a device that is not yet sending LACP, such as a PXE client.
- **Learning-limit violation log** — a FortiSwitch log of the first MAC address that exceeded a port or VLAN learning limit (`log-mac-limit-violations`), read with `mac-limit-violations`; the 7.6.7 feature matrix marks it standalone-only on the 1xx and 200 Series.
- **LGDP (loop guard data packet)** — the broadcast probe that a FortiSwitch port with loop guard enabled sends periodically; if the sending port receives one back, a loop exists downstream.
- **LLDP (Link Layer Discovery Protocol)** — the IEEE 802.1AB protocol with which directly connected devices advertise their identity and capabilities in TLVs; FortiLink also uses it for neighbor detection and automatic ISLs.
- **LLDP profile (FortiSwitch)** — a per-port profile that sets the LLDP TLVs a managed port sends and whether it may form an automatic ISL (`default-auto-isl`, the managed-port default) or an MCLAG ICL (`default-auto-mclag-icl`).
- **LLDP-MED** — Media Endpoint Discovery (ANSI/TIA-1057), the LLDP extension for media endpoints such as IP phones: network policy, location, extended PoE, and inventory TLVs; a FortiSwitch sends the TLVs of a network connectivity device.
- **LLDP-MED network policy** — one of eight fixed policies in an LLDP profile (`voice`, `voice-signaling`, `guest-voice`, `guest-voice-signaling`, `softphone-voice`, `video-conferencing`, `streaming-video`, `video-signaling`) that advertises a VLAN, priority, and DSCP value; it is sent only while its VLAN is native or allowed on the port, tagged unless native or untagged there.
- **Local-access policy** — a FortiOS object (`config switch-controller security-policy local-access`) that sets the administrative protocols allowed on a managed switch's management and internal interfaces, bound with `access-profile`.
- **Lockdown ISL (static ISL)** — converting automatically formed ISLs and ICLs into static ones (`static-isl enable`) so that they cannot be deleted by accident; a Security Rating recommendation.
- **Loop guard** — Fortinet's loop detector (`loop-guard`, `loop-guard-timeout`, default 45 minutes): a port that detects a downstream loop through its LGDP probes is taken out of service; distinct from STP loop protection.
- **MAB (MAC authentication bypass)** — authenticating a device that cannot run a supplicant by sending its MAC address to RADIUS as user name and password (`mac-auth-bypass enable`); `auth-order mab` makes it MAB-only, with no EAP.
- **MAC learning limit** — a cap of 1–128 dynamic MAC addresses per port (`learning-limit`) or per VLAN (`switch-controller-learning-limit` on the FortiGate VLAN interface); 0 means no limit; feature matrix note 3 lists the models without the per-VLAN limit.
- **MAC policy** — the FortiOS object (`config switch-controller mac-policy`) that a NAC policy applies to a matched device: its VLAN, optional traffic policy, packet counting, and whether the port bounces.
- **MAC-based authentication (802.1X)** — the `802.1X-mac-based` security mode, in which each MAC address on a port authenticates separately (up to 20 per port) and receives its own dynamic VLAN.
- **Managed-switch entry** — a FortiSwitch's record under `config switch-controller managed-switch`, created by discovery or in advance, named after the serial number unless renamed.
- **MCLAG (multichassis link aggregation group)** — a LAG whose member links terminate on two peer switches, so a downstream device stays connected if one switch fails.
- **MCLAG peer group** — the two FortiSwitch units joined by an ICL that act as one switch toward MCLAG trunks; both should be the same model on the same software version.
- **MCLAG split-brain** — the state in which the two MCLAG peers lose contact over the ICL; with `mclag-split-brain-detect enable`, the switch with the lower `mclag-split-brain-priority` (or the lower MAC address on a tie) goes dormant.
- **MCLAG trunk** — an 802.3ad LAG whose members are on both switches of an MCLAG peer group, configured on each peer with the same trunk name and `mclag enable`.
- **mgmt (management port)** — the dedicated out-of-band management interface of FortiSwitch models that have one; it is not a switch port, and on those models `internal` defaults to VLAN 4094.
- **MLD snooping** — the IPv6 counterpart of IGMP snooping; not supported on the FS-1xx or 200 Series models in the 7.6.7 feature matrix and absent from the FortiOS 7.6.0 managed-switch options.
- **Monitor mode (open-auth)** — an 802.1X security policy setting (`open-auth enable`) that lets traffic flow even when authentication fails, for testing a rollout.
- **MST region** — a set of switches with the same MSTP region name, revision, and VLAN-to-instance map; the map is summarized by the configuration digest.
- **MSTP (Multiple Spanning Tree Protocol)** — IEEE 802.1s spanning tree, built on RSTP, in which each instance carries the VLANs mapped to it; FortiSwitch runs it, configured in FortiLink mode by `config switch-controller stp-settings` and `stp-instance`.
- **Multi-VDOM mode** — the FortiGate mode (`set vdom-mode multi-vdom` under `config system global`) in which the configuration is split into a global context (`config global`) and per-VDOM contexts (`config vdom`, `edit <VDOM>`).
- **NAC LAN segment** — a group of NAC VLANs without Layer 3 settings that share one primary interface's address and DHCP server, so a device keeps its address when NAC moves it; one per FortiGate, with extra limits on note 12 models such as the FS-108F.
- **NAC policy** — a FortiOS object (`config user nac-policy`) that matches devices on NAC-mode ports by MAC address, vendor, type, family, operating system, user group, EMS tag, FortiVoice tag, or vulnerability, in list order, and applies a MAC policy.
- **Native VLAN** — the VLAN a managed-switch port assigns to untagged incoming frames and sends untagged (`vlan` under `config ports`).
- **NetFlow and IPFIX** — flow-record export formats (NetFlow versions 1, 5, and 9, and the IETF IP Flow Information Export) that a collector receives from a flow-exporting device.
- **NSE5_FSW_AD-7.6** — the exam code for Fortinet NSE 5 - FortiSwitch 7.6 Administrator.
- **On-switch packet capture** — a FortiSwitchOS capture profile (`config system sniffer-profile`) started and read on the switch itself (`execute system sniffer-profile`, `get system sniffer-profile capture`); on the 1xx models only the FS-124F, FS-148E, and FS-148F have it.
- **Onboarding VLAN** — the VLAN (auto-created `onboarding`, 4089) in which a device on a NAC-mode port waits until it matches a NAC policy; set by `onboarding-vlan` under the FortiLink interface's `nac-ports`.
- **Option 82** — the DHCP relay agent information option; with snooping, the switch inserts a circuit ID (by default interface, VLAN, and mode) and a remote ID (by default the MAC address) into clients' requests.
- **PCI passthrough** — giving a VM direct control of a physical PCI device, here a NIC, so the FortiGate-VM sees every frame on the FortiLink link; the host can no longer use the device.
- **PEAP** — Protected EAP: EAP inside a TLS tunnel set up with the server's certificate, here with MSCHAPv2 as the inner method.
- **Perpetual PoE** — a PoE port power setting (`poe-port-power perpetual` or `perpetual-fast`) that keeps power on through a soft or hard switch reboot; normal mode drops power while the switch restarts.
- **Platform notes** — the line before each lab's steps saying which options run it hands-on, which use read-and-predict, and any option-specific differences.
- **Port-based authentication (802.1X)** — the default `802.1X` security mode, in which one authenticated client authorizes the whole port.
- **Pre-provisioned managed switch** — a managed-switch entry created on the FortiGate before the switch has ever connected; Chapter 02 tests whether FortiOS 7.6 accepts per-port settings for one.
- **Pre-volume configuration** — the controller backup Chapter 02 took before the lab changed anything (`c1-fgt-base-174.conf`, `fgt1-pre-174.conf`, `fgt-c3-pre-174.conf`), which the teardown compares with or restores.
- **Private VLAN** — a standalone FortiSwitchOS feature that splits a primary VLAN into one isolated and any number of community secondary VLANs; hosts reach promiscuous ports and, in a community, each other; standalone-only on the 200 Series and not supported on the FS-1xx models.
- **Promiscuous port** — a private-VLAN port (`private-vlan promiscuous`) that can exchange traffic with every port of the primary VLAN and its secondary VLANs, typically the gateway side.
- **Proxy ARP** — the FortiGate answering ARP requests for other addresses with its own MAC address (`config system proxy-arp`), used with intra-VLAN blocking so that hosts send each other's traffic through the FortiGate.
- **QinQ (VLAN stacking)** — carrying customer VLANs inside a service VLAN with a second 802.1Q header, so each customer VDOM can have its own VLAN range; not on the FS-1xxE/F models, and not combinable with STP, DHCP snooping, or IGMP snooping.
- **QoS marking** — rewriting the CoS or DSCP value that a packet carries; on a FortiSwitch, done with the `remark-cos` and `remark-dscp` ingress ACL actions in standalone mode on the 200 Series.
- **QoS policy (FortiOS)** — the FortiOS object (`config switch-controller qos qos-policy`) that bundles the trust maps, the default CoS, and a queue policy and is applied to a managed port with `qos-policy`; on the switch it becomes interface settings.
- **Quarantine (FortiSwitch)** — isolating hosts by MAC address from the FortiGate (`config user quarantine`); in the default by-VLAN mode the switch binds the MAC address to the quarantine VLAN 4093, and in by-redirect mode the traffic is redirected to the FortiGate within its VLAN.
- **Queue policy** — the FortiOS egress policy (`config switch-controller qos queue-policy`) with a scheduling mode and per-queue rates, weights, and drop policy; the switch calls the same object its QoS policy (`config switch qos qos-policy`).
- **Read-and-predict** — an exercise that shows a real documented output shape, asks the reader to predict a field, then gives the answer and the reason.
- **Remote log (FortiSwitch)** — the controller setting (`config switch-controller remote-log`, entries `syslogd` and `syslogd2`) that makes managed switches send their logs to one or two syslog servers over UDP.
- **Rogue DHCP server** — an unauthorized DHCP server on an access port; DHCP snooping drops its replies because the port is untrusted, and the switch logs the first packet it sees from it.
- **Root guard** — a per-port protection (`stp-root-guard`) that ignores or drops superior BPDUs so that the port cannot become the path to a new root; never enabled on the root port.
- **RPVST+ (Rapid PVST+)** — a per-VLAN rapid spanning tree used by other vendors; a managed FortiSwitch port interoperates with it when `rpvst-port` is enabled (from FortiOS 6.4.2).
- **RSPAN** — remote SPAN: a FortiSwitch mirror that carries copies of traffic in VLAN 4092 to the FortiGate's `rspan` interface, where they are captured (`traffic-sniffer`, `set mode rspan`); 200 Series and larger in this volume.
- **S1, S2, S3** — this volume's switch options: an FS-108F (S1), an FS-124F (S2), and a pair of FS-2xx switches (S3).
- **Scheduling mode (QoS)** — how a port serves its eight egress queues: strict (higher queue first), round-robin (one packet per backlogged queue), or weighted round-robin (weights 0–63, where weight 0 makes a queue strict).
- **Server-timeout VLAN** — the VLAN (`authserver-timeout-vlan`, or the tagged `authserver-timeout-tagged` variant in MAC-based mode) for clients whose RADIUS server does not answer within `authserver-timeout-period`.
- **Services policy (P2)** — the controller policy from the FortiLink interface (later also `FSW-LAN`) to the services segment for RADIUS, syslog, SNMP traps, and sFlow; it uses source NAT on C1 only.
- **set-standalone** — `execute switch-controller switch-action set-standalone`, which returns a managed switch to factory defaults in standalone mode and keeps the controller from detecting and authorizing it automatically.
- **sFlow** — a sampling protocol (version 5 on FortiSwitch) that sends packet samples and interface counters as UDP datagrams, port 6343 by default, to a collector; enabled per port with `packet-sampler` and a controller collector address.
- **Shared VLANs (multi-tenancy)** — the multi-tenancy model in which VLANs of several VDOMs are assigned to the same switch ports by the controlling VDOM's administrator, with traffic separation but no administrative separation.
- **SNMPv3** — the version of SNMP with user-based security: authentication (for example SHA-256) and privacy (for example AES) per user, set for managed switches with `config switch-controller snmp-user`.
- **Software-based routing** — Layer 3 forwarding done by the switch CPU rather than in hardware; the 7.6.7 feature matrix marks the FS-1xx models as software-based routing only and the 200 Series as hardware-based.
- **SPAN (port mirror)** — local port mirroring: copies of the traffic entering or leaving source ports are sent to a destination port on the same switch (`config mirror` under a managed-switch entry).
- **Split port** — one 40G or 100G QSFP port broken out into four (or two) subports, named `portN.1` and so on, with `config switch phy-mode`; it removes the original port's configuration, needs a reboot, and is supported only on the 500 Series, 10xx, and 3032 models.
- **Stack (FortiLink)** — all the FortiSwitch units managed through one FortiLink interface, which act as one Layer 2 stack while the FortiGate manages each switch separately; not a chassis stack with stacking cables.
- **Staged firmware** — a FortiSwitchOS image written to a managed switch's storage but not yet running; `get-upgrade-status` shows it in Next-boot until the switch restarts.
- **Standalone mode** — a FortiSwitch configured through its own FortiSwitchOS CLI or GUI, or from FortiEdge Cloud, rather than by a FortiGate.
- **Static route (FortiSwitchOS)** — an entry in `config router static` with `dst`, `gateway`, and `device`; in standalone mode it also provides the default route for remote management.
- **Sticky MAC** — a port setting (`sticky-mac enable`) that keeps dynamically learned MAC addresses when the port goes down and up; they survive a reboot only if saved with `sticky-mac save`; not used on a port with 802.1X.
- **Storm control** — a switch feature that drops broadcast, unknown unicast, or unknown multicast traffic above a packets-per-second rate; set globally, per switch, or per port through a storm-control policy.
- **Storm-control policy** — a FortiOS object (`config switch-controller storm-control-policy`) applied to a managed-switch port, whose `storm-control-mode` is `global` (use global or switch settings), `override` (its own rate and traffic types), or `disabled`.
- **STP loop protection** — a FortiSwitchOS interface option (`stp-loop-protection`) that keeps an alternate or backup port blocking even if it stops receiving BPDUs; not the same as loop guard, and not in the FortiOS 7.6.0 managed-switch `ports` table.
- **Supplicant** — the 802.1X client software on an endpoint, such as `wpa_supplicant` on Linux.
- **SVI (switch virtual interface)** — a routed interface on a switch associated with a VLAN; from FortiOS 7.6.4 the controller can configure SVIs and static routes on managed switches (`config system-interface`, `config router-static` under the switch's entry).
- **Switch controller** — the FortiGate function that manages FortiSwitch units over FortiLink.
- **Switch profile** — a FortiOS object (`config switch-controller switch-profile`) that applies settings such as the admin password override, console login, and revision backups to the switches bound to it.
- **Switch-controller reserved network** — the address block (`switch-controller-reserved-network`, default `10.255.0.0/16`) from which the switch controller assigns /24 subnets to auto-created VLAN interfaces.
- **switch-on-deauth** — a FortiLink Guide 7.6.7 option under `config switch-controller global` that resets a switch (`factory-reset`) or keeps its password (`no-op`) when it is deauthorized; not in the FortiOS 7.6.0 CLI Reference.
- **TCAM** — ternary content-addressable memory, the switch hardware table in which ACLs are matched; it is allocated best-fit in classifier order, so an ACL can fail to install when it is fragmented.
- **Teardown** — the final lab of the volume: retiring the switches, returning the controllers to their pre-volume configuration, and undoing the Proxmox, Nexus, VM, and host changes.
- **Tenant VDOM** — a VDOM that receives switch ports or VLANs from the VDOM that controls the FortiLink interface; in this volume `tenant-174`, holding `fsw-tenant` (VLAN 1745).
- **TLV (type-length-value)** — the encoding of each piece of information in an LLDP frame, such as the system name, the port VLAN ID, or an LLDP-MED network policy.
- **Track 1 / Track 2** — the two lab paths: a real FortiSwitch managed by a FortiGate (Track 1), and the FortiGate alone with switch behavior read-and-predict (Track 2).
- **Trunk (FortiSwitch)** — a link aggregation group (802.3ad), static or LACP, configured as a `type trunk` entry under `config ports`; not an 802.1Q tagged port.
- **Trust (QoS)** — a port setting (`trust-dot1p-map`, `trust-ip-dscp-map`) that makes the switch classify incoming traffic by its 802.1p or DSCP value through a map; if both are trusted, DSCP decides.
- **Trusted host (FortiSwitchOS)** — an address and mask (`trusthost1` to `trusthost10` under `config system admin`) from which an administrator account may log in over the network.
- **Tunnel-Private-Group-Id** — RADIUS attribute 81, which carries the dynamic VLAN as an ID or a name; a managed FortiSwitch matches a name against the VLAN description that the controller copies from the FortiGate interface name (up to 15 characters).
- **Unregistered multicast** — multicast to a group no port has joined; with IGMP snooping it is forwarded only to multicast router ports and ports with `mcast-snooping-flood-traffic`, unless `flood-unknown-multicast` is enabled.
- **Untagged VLANs** — the VLANs a managed-switch port sends without a tag on egress (`untagged-vlans`); each must also be an allowed VLAN.
- **VDOM (virtual domain)** — an independent virtual firewall inside one FortiGate, with its own interfaces, policies, and routing; Volume CIX found that the FortiGate-VM evaluation allows only one traffic VDOM.
- **Virtual port pool** — a FortiOS object (`config switch-controller virtual-port-pool`) into which the controlling VDOM places ports with `export-to-pool`, for a tenant VDOM to claim with `execute switch-controller virtual-port-pool request`.
- **Virtual switch (tenant)** — the managed-switch entry a tenant VDOM sees for exported ports, with `type virtual` and `owner-vdom` naming the VDOM that owns the switch.
- **VLAN optimization** — the switch-controller default that lets automatically formed FortiLink and inter-switch trunks carry only user-defined VLANs instead of VLANs 1–4093.
- **VLAN policy** — a FortiOS object (`config switch-controller vlan-policy`) holding a native VLAN, allowed and untagged VLANs, and a discard mode, applied to a port by a dynamic port policy rule.
- **vmbrFL** — the plain Proxmox Linux bridge (not VLAN-aware, STP off, `group_fwd_mask` 16384) that carries FortiLink from a FortiGate-VM to the switch on C2 and C3.
- **voice-qos** — the predefined FortiOS QoS policy for voice (`voice-dot1p`, `voice-dscp`, and the weighted queue policy `voice-egress`), which sends DSCP 46 to queue 1 and DSCP 24 and 26 to queue 2.
- **Wildcard serial number** — a pre-authorization serial of the form `PREFIX****nnnnnn` that the first matching discovered switch consumes, taking the entry's settings.
- **Zero-touch management** — the FortiSwitchOS 7.2.0 and later factory behavior in which a new or reset switch looks for a manager on its own, with DHCP on its management interfaces and FortiLink discovery enabled.
