# Volume CXLIX — UiPath Certification Tracks

> The UiPath Certified Professional program — verified 5 August 2026 on `uipath.com/learning/certification`. The
> program is **role-based**: rather than one linear ladder, it offers certifications aligned to real automation-team
> roles across two levels, **Associate** and **Professional** — Automation Developer (Associate/Professional),
> **Agentic Automation** (Associate/Professional), Specialized AI Professional, Automation Solution Architect
> Professional, Automation Business Analyst Professional, and a testing track moving from the retiring Software
> Testing Engineer Professional to the new **Test Cloud Architect Professional**. The **Automation Developer
> Associate** is the common entry point (its exam is **60 multiple-choice and multi-select questions**).
> Credentials issued **since February 2026 are valid for three years**. Preparation is **free** through **UiPath
> Academy**; exams are **proctored** and paid via **vouchers**; holding multiple certifications earns the
> **Automation Catalysts** badge. The legacy **UiRPA** and **UiARD** certifications (retired 2023) **expire 15
> October 2026** and were relaunched as the Automation Developer Associate/Professional. The whole program reflects
> the shift from **RPA to agentic automation**. Every lab runs free in Python; a Community Edition is free to learn.

## Overview

UiPath is the leader in **business process automation** — building software **robots** that operate applications the
way a person does, and, increasingly, **AI agents** that reason and act. UiPath pioneered **RPA** (Robotic Process
Automation) and has expanded into **agentic automation**, combining robots, AI agents, and humans. Its
certifications validate the skills to build, deploy, and govern automation at enterprise scale, within a **Center of
Excellence**.

Chapter 02 covers the **RPA-to-agentic shift** — deterministic robots, their limits, and AI agents that handle
judgment. Chapter 03 covers the **platform** — Studio, Orchestrator, Robots. Chapter 04 covers **building
automations** — workflows, selectors, exception handling. Chapter 05 covers **attended vs unattended and
Orchestrator** — queues, the REFramework, credentials. Chapter 06 covers **Document Understanding and Communications
Mining** — intelligent document processing and confidence thresholds. Chapter 07 covers **process discovery** —
mining and ROI-based prioritization. Chapter 08 covers **solution architecture, testing, and governance**. Chapter
09 closes on choosing a path.

A theme runs through it: **automate the right work, robustly** — choose high-ROI processes, build for failure, use
robots for the deterministic bulk and agents for judgment, and govern the robot workforce.

## What you will be able to do

- Read the role-based program and certify for your automation-team seat, Associate to Professional.
- Explain the RPA-to-agentic shift and orchestrate robots, agents, and humans by their strengths.
- Trace an automation through Studio, Orchestrator, and Robots.
- Build robust automations — stable selectors, retry-transient/route-business exception handling.
- Choose attended vs unattended, and scale volume with queues and the REFramework.
- Apply confidence thresholds for straight-through processing in Document Understanding.
- Prioritize automation candidates by ROI using Process and Task Mining.
- Govern a robot fleet with least privilege, centralized credentials, and regression testing.

## Prerequisites

- Basic programming logic and familiarity with business applications helps.
- A Linux or macOS host with `python3`. **UiPath Academy is free**, and a **Community Edition** of the platform is free for learning.
