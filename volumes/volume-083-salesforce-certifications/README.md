# Volume LXXXIII — Salesforce Certification Tracks

> The whole Salesforce certification program in one volume — the Administrator, Platform App Builder,
> Developer, Consultant, and Architect tracks (to CTA) plus the new Agentforce/AI wave — on the
> Salesforce CRM/low-code platform, with hands-on labs, verified against salesforce.com and Trailhead.

## Overview

Volume LXXXIII maps the **Salesforce** certification program — one of the largest certification
ecosystems in tech — for the leading CRM and low-code application platform. The tracks run from
**Associate** (including the **AI Associate**) through the flagship **Administrator (ADM-201)**,
**Platform App Builder**, and **Platform Developer I/II**, the **Consultant** clouds (Sales, Service,
Experience, Marketing), and the **Architect** pyramid (Application Architect and System Architect) to
the elite **Certified Technical Architect (CTA)** — with the newest, highest-demand **Agentforce** and
**AI** credentials on top. It closes the encyclopedia's Cloud & platform cluster alongside ServiceNow
(LXXX), SUSE (LXXXI), and Alibaba Cloud (LXXXII).

This is a **certification-tracks** volume, like the other vendor volumes (XXXIX–LXXXII): it maps the
program — the tracks and credentials — and teaches each with a hands-on walkthrough. Every track and
platform area was **verified against salesforce.com and Trailhead on 29 July 2026**.

Chapters follow the track ladder:

- **Chapter 01** frames the program — the tracks, Trailhead, the Agentforce/AI wave, and the release cadence.
- **Chapters 02–04** take the **Administrator** and **App Builder**: data model; security/sharing; declarative automation.
- **Chapter 05** takes the **Platform Developer**: Apex and LWC.
- **Chapter 06** takes the **Consultant** clouds (Sales/Service/Experience).
- **Chapter 07** takes the **Architect** track (Application and System, to CTA).
- **Chapter 08** takes **Agentforce and AI**.
- **Chapter 09** covers certification prep, currency, and career.

Every chapter follows the standard structure defined in
[templates/chapter.md](../../templates/chapter.md) and enforced by
[EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md](../../EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md), including per-topic hands-on labs and
knowledge checks.

> **Scope.** Salesforce administration and development are authorized platform work — configuring,
> coding, and securing your own org, with responsible, grounded AI for Agentforce.
