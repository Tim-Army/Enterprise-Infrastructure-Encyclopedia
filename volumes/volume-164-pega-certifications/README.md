## Overview

Pega is a leader in **low-code** enterprise software — a platform for **business process management (BPM) and
case management**, **customer engagement (CRM)**, and **AI-driven decisioning**. Its distinctive approach is
**model-driven development**: you model an application visually and Pega generates it, so business and IT build
together and adapt quickly. Pega sits alongside the enterprise-platform and automation vendors this shelf
covers ([ServiceNow LXXX](../volume-080-servicenow-certifications/README.md),
[Salesforce LXXXIII](../volume-083-salesforce-certifications/README.md),
[UiPath CXLIX](../volume-149-uipath-certifications/README.md)).

Chapter 02 covers **the Pega Platform** (low-code, model-driven, everything-is-a-rule, guardrails). Chapters
03–08 take the platform and roles: **case management** (dynamic cases), **building an application** (the System
Architect), **reusability and the situational layer cake**, **decisioning and Next-Best-Action**, **Pega
Robotics and GenAI**, and **the Business Architect and DCO**. Chapter 09 closes on choosing a path.

A theme runs through it: **model** (low-code), **orchestrate** (dynamic cases), **reuse** (the layer cake),
**decide** (Next-Best-Action), and **automate** (robotics and GenAI) — enterprise automation built for change.

## What you will be able to do

- Read the Pega tracks and the prerequisite-gated System Architect ladder, including the two-part CLSA.
- Explain low-code, model-driven development — App Studio vs Dev Studio, everything is a rule, guardrails.
- Model work as dynamic cases — stages, steps, and adaptive lifecycles.
- Build a Pega application — data model, model-driven UI, decisions, and integrations.
- Design reuse with the situational layer cake — specialize by layer without forking.
- Apply decisioning and Next-Best-Action — real-time 1:1 AI in the Customer Decision Hub.
- Use Pega Robotics (attended/unattended RPA) and Pega GenAI (AI-assisted design).
- Capture requirements the Pega way with DCO (Directly Capture Objectives).

## Prerequisites

- Familiarity with enterprise applications, business processes, and basic development concepts helps.
- A Linux or macOS host with `python3`. **Pega certifications** are delivered via Pega Academy (version-specific).
