# Chapter 23: Appendix — Snowflake Certifications and Course Access

The **Snowflake** certification program — the SnowPro Associate, the required
SnowPro Core, and the five role-based SnowPro Advanced tracks — organized by tier,
with each credential's focus, level, and the training and practice model. The
lineup was verified on **27 July 2026** from **learn.snowflake.com/certifications**
— the same source that anchors
[Volume XLIX — Snowflake Certification Tracks](../../volume-49-snowflake-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works at Snowflake.** Training is delivered through **Snowflake
University** — free on-demand courses and paid instructor-led training — with free
**exam study guides** on each certification page. Practice is free on a **Snowflake
free trial** (a 30-day credit-limited account). Exams are delivered through
**Pearson VUE**, online-proctored or at a test center.

> **Currency.** Snowflake revises exam guides as the platform evolves — **SnowPro
> Core moved from COF-C02 to COF-C03** (live 16 February 2026), the **SnowPro
> Associate: Platform** entry tier was added, and **Cortex** AI content now appears
> across the exams under the **AI Data Cloud** branding. Confirm the current guide
> and code (Core is **COF-C03**) on learn.snowflake.com before registering.

## Free training and entry points

- **[Snowflake Certifications](https://learn.snowflake.com/en/certifications/)** —
  the authoritative catalog and per-exam study guides
- **Snowflake University** — free on-demand learning paths and paid ILT
- **Free trial** — a 30-day account to practice warehouses, SQL, loading, and Cortex
- **Hands-On Essentials / badges** — free guided courses that build toward Core

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees (confirm at registration):** **SnowPro Core (COF-C03)** is **US$175**;
  **SnowPro Advanced** exams are the higher band; the **Associate** is the lower
  band.
- **Delivery:** Pearson VUE (online-proctored or test center). Core is **100
  questions / 115 minutes**, passing **750/1000**. Each certification has a
  published **study guide** with weighted domains.
- **Prerequisites:** the five **SnowPro Advanced** exams **require SnowPro Core**;
  the Associate and Core have no prerequisite.
- **Validity and renewal:** certifications are valid for **two years** and renewed by
  **passing a recertification exam** (or a higher/newer exam) — no
  continuing-education model.

## Selected exam-guide focus

- **SnowPro Associate: Platform:** foundational platform concepts — architecture,
  storage/compute separation, and basic operations.
- **SnowPro Core (COF-C03):** the AI Data Cloud architecture, access and security
  (RBAC), performance, data loading/transformation, protection/sharing, and
  pipelines.
- **Advanced: Architect (ARA-C01):** account/data architecture, secure data
  sharing, security, performance, and business continuity.
- **Advanced: Data Engineer (DEA-C02):** ingestion (Snowpipe/streams),
  transformation (Snowpark/UDFs/procedures), optimization, modeling, and security.
- **Advanced: Data Analyst (DAA-C01):** advanced analytic SQL, semi-structured data,
  data quality, and consumption/visualization.
- **Advanced: Data Scientist (DSA-C03):** feature engineering, Snowpark ML, model
  deployment, and Cortex ML/AI functions.
- **Advanced: Administrator (ADA-C01):** account/security, cost management (resource
  monitors), governance, performance monitoring, and replication/failover.

## Notes

- **Core is the gate.** All five Advanced exams require a current SnowPro Core.
- **Exam guides are the study scope.** Download the current per-exam guide; confirm
  the Core code is **COF-C03** (COF-C02 is retired).
- **Practice is free** on a Snowflake trial; Cortex and Snowpark are available to
  exercise the AI content.
- **Renew by exam** within the two-year validity window.

  (Databricks)**, data/observability in **Volume XLV (Splunk)**, and the cloud
