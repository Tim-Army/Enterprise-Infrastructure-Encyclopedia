# Chapter 20: Appendix — NVIDIA Certifications and Course Access

The **NVIDIA** certification program — the AI-infrastructure, operations,
networking, data-science, and generative/agentic-AI credentials — organized by
level, with each certification's focus, price, and delivery. The lineup was
harvested on **26 July 2026** from **nvidia.com/learn/certification** — the same
source that anchors
[Volume XLVI — NVIDIA Certification Tracks](../../volume-46-nvidia-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works at NVIDIA.** Official training is delivered through the **NVIDIA
Deep Learning Institute (DLI)** — self-paced and instructor-led courses, many with
hands-on GPU lab environments — plus free **exam blueprints** on each certification
page. Exams are delivered through **Pearson VUE** (online proctored). Credentials
appear as verifiable digital badges.

> **Currency.** NVIDIA expands the program frequently — confirm the current exams
> and blueprints on nvidia.com before registering. Recent additions captured here:
> **NCP-AAI (Agentic AI)**, **NCP-OUSD (OpenUSD Development)**, **NCA-GENM
> (Generative AI Multimodal)**, and **NCP-AIN (AI Networking)**, alongside the
> established NCA-AIIO/ADS/GENL and NCP-AII/AIO/ADS/GENL exams.

## Free training and entry points

- **[NVIDIA Certification](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/learn/certification/)** —
  the authoritative catalog and per-exam blueprints
- **Exam blueprints** — free per exam; the weighted study scope
- **[NVIDIA DLI](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/training/)** — self-paced and
  instructor-led courses with hands-on GPU labs
- **NGC catalog** — validated containers, models, and Helm charts for practice

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees (confirm at registration):** **Associate (NCA)** exams are **$125** (50
  questions / 60 minutes). **Professional (NCP)** exams are **2 hours**, priced by
  exam: NCP-AII **$400**, NCP-AIO **$500**, NCP-AIN **$400**, and NCP-ADS/GENL/AAI/
  OUSD **$200** each.
- **Delivery:** **Pearson VUE**, online-proctored, governed by a published
  **exam blueprint** per exam.
- **Recommended experience:** the Professional infrastructure exams (NCP-AII/AIO)
  assume **1–3 years** of hands-on experience.
- **Validity and renewal:** certifications are valid for a defined period (commonly
  **two years**) and renewed by **retaking the current exam** (or a higher one),
  reflecting the fast-moving technology.

## Blueprint focus by exam

- **NCA-AIIO:** AI/ML/DL fundamentals; NVIDIA software (CUDA, NGC, NIM); AI
  infrastructure (DGX, NVLink, InfiniBand); AI operations (MIG, monitoring,
  orchestration).
- **NCA-GENL / NCA-GENM:** LLM fundamentals, prompting, RAG, fine-tuning,
  evaluation; multimodal (vision-language, diffusion).
- **NCA-ADS / NCP-ADS:** RAPIDS (cuDF, cuML, cuGraph); GPU pipelines; scaling with
  Dask.
- **NCP-AII:** hardware deployment (DGX/HGX, InfiniBand); Slurm; Kubernetes GPU
  Operator; Base Command Manager; validation (DCGM/NCCL).
- **NCP-AIO:** monitoring (DCGM); troubleshooting (GPU/driver/fabric/jobs, Xid/ECC);
  optimization (MIG, time-slicing, capacity).
- **NCP-AIN:** InfiniBand; Spectrum-X (RoCE); RDMA/GPUDirect; NCCL; BlueField DPUs.
- **NCP-GENL / NCP-AAI / NCP-OUSD:** production LLM development (NeMo/NIM/
  TensorRT-LLM); agents (tools, orchestration, multi-agent, safety); OpenUSD and
  digital twins.

## Notes

- **Two levels.** Associate (NCA) is foundational/knowledge; Professional (NCP) is
  advanced and often hands-on (infra exams assume real experience).
- **The program expands fast.** Agentic AI, OpenUSD, Multimodal, and AI Networking
  are recent — confirm the current catalog on nvidia.com.
- **Blueprints are the study scope.** Download the per-exam blueprint; train with
  DLI and practice on real GPUs (workstation, DGX, or cloud GPU).
- **Renew by exam** within the validity window, since the technology moves quickly.
- **Related practice** in the encyclopedia: containers/platform in **Volume VIII**,
  automation in **Volume IX**, observability in **Volume XI**, and the cloud
  volumes for GPU cloud instances.
