# Chapter 13: Appendix — CompTIA Certifications and Course Access

CompTIA's **vendor-neutral** certification program, organized by pathway, with
each credential's current exam code, the official training that prepares it, and
the Continuing Education renewal model. The lineup and exam codes were harvested
on **26 July 2026** from **comptia.org** — the same source that anchors
[Volume XXXIX — CompTIA Certification Tracks](../../volume-39-comptia-certification-tracks/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works at CompTIA.** Official training is delivered through
**CompTIA CertMaster** — **CertMaster Learn** (self-paced e-learning),
**CertMaster Labs** (hands-on virtual labs for the performance-based
questions), **CertMaster Practice** (adaptive practice), and **CertMaster CE**
(continuing education) — sold per certification, often bundled with the exam
voucher. Free resources include the downloadable **exam objectives (blueprint)**
on each certification page, exam-detail sheets, and the certification roadmap.
Exams are delivered through **Pearson VUE** (test center or **online-proctored**)
and earn a shareable digital badge (Credly-style).

> **Currency.** CompTIA **renames and renumbers exams often**, so the exam code
> is the currency signal — confirm each certification's code, name, and status
> on comptia.org before registering. Recent examples in this appendix:
> **CASP+ became SecurityX (CAS-005)**, **ITF+ became Tech+ (FC0-U71)**, A+
> moved to the **220-1201/220-1202 Core Series**, Network+ to **N10-009**,
> Security+ to **SY0-701**, Linux+ to **XK0-006**, Data+ to **DA0-002**, and
> CySA+ to **CS0-004 (V4, June 2026)**.
> Newer hands-on material sits alongside the classic exams in three lines: the
> **Xpert Series** certifications **CloudNetX (CNX-001)** and **DataAI
> (DY0-001)**; the **Expansion Series** security certifications **SecAI+
> (CY0-001, GA Feb 2026)** and **SecOT+ (SOT-001, GA Dec 2026)**; and the **"Pro"
> training courses** (Hybrid Server Pro, CyberDefense Pro, Ethical Hacker Pro,
> Linux Pro, Security Pro) that pair a course with a competency assessment and
> ladder into a CompTIA or vendor certification.

## Free training and entry points

- **[CompTIA Certifications catalog](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/)** —
  the authoritative certification pages, each with a downloadable exam-objectives
  blueprint and exam details
- **Exam objectives (blueprints)** — free PDF per certification; the definitive
  study scope and domain weights
- **Certification roadmap** — the pathway map from Core through the specialist
  and Xpert tracks
- **[CompTIA CertMaster](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/training/)** — official
  paid Learn, Labs, Practice, and CE training (the hands-on complement for the
  performance-based questions)
- **CompTIA Continuing Education** — the renewal program and CEU catalog

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fee band (US pricing; varies by country — confirm at registration):**
  entry exams such as **Tech+** are the lowest; **A+** requires **two exams**
  (Core 1 + Core 2), each around the mid-hundreds; **Network+**, **Security+**,
  **Cloud+**, **Linux+**, **Server+**, and the **Data** exams sit in the upper-
  hundreds band; advanced exams such as **SecurityX (CAS-005)** are the highest.
  Confirm current pricing (and voucher/CertMaster bundles) at Pearson VUE.
- **Delivery:** **Pearson VUE**, in a test center or **online-proctored**. Exams
  blend multiple-choice with **performance-based questions (PBQs)** that require
  completing a task in a simulated environment. A+ is a **two-exam Core Series**.
- **Accreditation:** many CompTIA certifications are **ISO/ANSI (ISO/IEC 17024)**
  accredited and recognized under the **U.S. DoD 8140/8570** directive.
- **Validity and renewal:** most certifications are valid for **three years**
  and renew through the **Continuing Education (CE) program** by earning
  **CEUs** (and paying the annual CE fee) — **no re-exam required**. Because
  certifications **stack**, renewing a **higher** certification renews the lower
  ones beneath it in the same track. Some credentials (historically Project+ and
  older certs) have carried non-expiring status — confirm each on comptia.org.

## Notes

- **Exam codes are the currency signal.** CompTIA renumbers on re-scope, so a
  changed code means a changed blueprint. Recent changes captured here: **ITF+ →
  Tech+ (FC0-U71)**, **CASP+ → SecurityX (CAS-005)**, **N10-008 → N10-009**,
  **SY0-601 → SY0-701**, **XK0-005 → XK0-006**, **DA0-001 → DA0-002**, plus the
  new Xpert certifications (**CNX-001**, **DY0-001**), the Expansion Series
  (**SecAI+ CY0-001**, **SecOT+ SOT-001** for December 2026), and the "Pro"
  course line.
- **A+ is two exams** — both **220-1201 (Core 1)** and **220-1202 (Core 2)**
  must be passed to certify.
- **Fees are US pricing and approximate** and vary by country and tax — confirm
  at Pearson VUE registration, and look for **CertMaster + voucher** bundles.
- **Renewal is via Continuing Education** over a three-year cycle (CEUs, no
  re-test); renewing a **higher** stacked certification renews the lower ones.
- **The Xpert, Expansion, and "Pro" lines are hands-on and performance-based** —
  prepare with **CertMaster Labs** and hands-on practice, not reading alone. Note
  the **"Pro" items are courses with a competency assessment**, not standalone
  CompTIA exams (Hybrid Server Pro ladders to Microsoft AZ-800/AZ-801).
- **Hands-on practice** for the CompTIA-adjacent skills lives across the vendor
  volumes: Security+/CySA+ under the security volumes (X, XVI, XIX, XXV, XXXV),
  Network+ under the networking tracks (III, XXXI), Linux+ under Volumes XIV/XXI,
  Cloud+ under the cloud volumes (XVII, XXXIII, XXXIV), and Server+ under Volume
  XXXVI.
