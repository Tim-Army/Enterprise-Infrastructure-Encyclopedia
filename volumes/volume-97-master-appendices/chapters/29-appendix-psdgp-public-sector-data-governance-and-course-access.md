# Chapter 29: Appendix — PSDGP Public Sector Data Governance and Course Access

The **Public Sector Data Governance Professional (PSDGP)** certification — the **ICCP**
credential for running a data governance program in government — with its content areas, exam
mechanics, prerequisites, and the training and delivery model. The program was verified on
**28 July 2026** from **ther2c.com** and **iccp.org** — the same sources that anchor
[Volume LXIII — Public Sector Data Governance (PSDGP)](../../volume-63-public-sector-data-governance/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** Certification is issued by the **Institute for the Certification of
Computing Professionals (ICCP)**. Training is delivered by **R2C (TheR2C)** and **Buchanan &
Edwards** as a **three-day virtual, instructor-led course** ($1,495) that ends in a
**proctored certification exam** on day three. You do **not** need to be an ICCP member to sit
the exam. The credential is **valid three years** and renewed through **Continuing
Professional Development (CPD)**.

> **Currency.** PSDGP rests on statute (FOIA, the Privacy Act, the Federal Records Act, FISMA,
> the OPEN Government Data Act), which evolves faster than the exam. Confirm the current content
> areas on ther2c.com and iccp.org, and track the legal landscape, before registering.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[TheR2C — Public Sector Data Governance](https://www.ther2c.com/psdg)** — the authoritative
  course and certification page (content areas, schedule)
- **[ICCP](https://www.iccp.org/)** — the certifying body: PSDGP, the DGSP core credential, and
  certification levels
- **[datagovernance.education](https://datagovernance.education/)** (Buchanan & Edwards) — the
  virtual course and ICCP exam
- The labs in Volume LXIII need only a shell, `python3`, and `sqlite3` — free practice for the
  governance artifacts

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** R2C three-day course **US$1,495** (includes the exam); a certification fee applies.
- **Delivery:** **virtual, instructor-led** (three days, 9AM–4PM EST) ending in a **proctored**
  exam of **100 questions in 90 minutes** at a third-party exam center.
- **Prerequisites:** an **Associate's (AA/AS) degree or higher** AND **more than two years of
  work experience**, OR a **'core'-level ICCP certification** (e.g., DGSP).
- **Validity and renewal:** valid **three years**; renewed via **Continuing Professional
  Development (CPD)** (not CEUs or re-examination).

## Content areas

The exam blueprint is the four content areas of the R2C course:

- **Public Sector Data Governance Mission Drivers:** service delivery; data-as-asset / open
  data (OPEN Government Data Act); transparency and accountability (FOIA/audit); risk and
  compliance drivers.
- **Public Sector Data Governance Deliverables:** governance charter; policies and standards;
  data catalog/inventory and metadata; data-quality framework and scorecard; stewardship
  operating-model artifacts.
- **Data Governance Roles and Responsibilities:** governing bodies (council/board); roles
  (CDO, data owner, steward, custodian); RACI; stakeholder engagement and change management.
- **Legal and Regulatory Environment:** FOIA / open records; Privacy Act / PII; records
  management and retention (Federal Records Act / NARA); security and compliance (FISMA / NIST
  800-53 / FedRAMP); data sharing and openness (Evidence Act / OPEN Government Data Act).

## Notes

- **Governance, not tooling.** PSDGP certifies the ability to build and run a program; the
  study scope is policy, law, and deliverables — reproducible with a shell and `python3`.
- **Content areas are the study scope.** Follow the R2C/Buchanan & Edwards course.
- **Prerequisite paths.** Degree-plus-experience or a core ICCP credential (DGSP).
- **Recertify** within the three-year window through CPD.
- **Related practice** in the encyclopedia: ISC2 governance/CGRC in **Volume XL**, ISACA
  CDPSE/governance in **Volume XLIV**, Enterprise Cybersecurity in **Volume X**, and NetBox
  data management in **Volume LII**.
