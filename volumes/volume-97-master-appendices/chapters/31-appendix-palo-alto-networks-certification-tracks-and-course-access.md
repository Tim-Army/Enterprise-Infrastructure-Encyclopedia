# Chapter 31: Appendix — Palo Alto Networks Certification Tracks and Course Access

The **Palo Alto Networks** role-based certification program — the credentials for securing,
operating, and defending with Palo Alto's platform (**PAN-OS/NGFW**, **Cortex XDR/XSIAM/XSOAR**,
**Prisma/Cortex Cloud**) — organized by level and track, with the training and delivery model.
The program was verified on **28 July 2026** from **paloaltonetworks.com** — the same source that
anchors
[Volume LXV — Palo Alto Networks Certification Tracks](../../volume-65-palo-alto-networks-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

This appendix is the **cert-tracks companion** to the product-focused
[Palo Alto Networks appendix (Chapter 05)](05-appendix-palo-alto-networks-certifications-and-course-access.md)
and [Volume XVI](../../volume-16-palo-alto-networks-security/README.md); it records the current
**role-based** framework.

**How access works.** Training is delivered through **Palo Alto Networks Education Services**
(instructor-led, digital learning, and the Beacon platform), and exams are delivered by
**Pearson VUE**. Palo Alto certifications typically carry a **two-year** validity, renewed by
passing a current (or higher-level) exam.

> **Currency.** Palo Alto **restructured the program in 2025**, retiring the legacy code-based
> exams (PCNSA, PCNSE, PCCSE, PCDRA, PCSAE, PCSFE, PCCET) and replacing them with the role-based
> framework below. The platform (PAN-OS, Cortex, Prisma/Cortex Cloud) evolves quickly. Confirm
> the current levels, tracks, and blueprints on paloaltonetworks.com before registering.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[Palo Alto Networks Certification](https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/services/education/certification)**
  — the authoritative role-based framework and blueprints
- **Palo Alto Networks Education Services / Beacon** — courses and digital learning
- **[Cybersecurity Academy](https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/services/education/academy)** —
  student/educator resources
- Practice on **VM-Series/PAN-OS** and **Cortex/Prisma trials** in an **authorized** lab

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** per-exam fees via Pearson VUE; course fees separately. Confirm current pricing on the
  portal.
- **Delivery:** exams at **Pearson VUE**; training through Education Services / Beacon.
- **Levels:** **Foundational → Professional → Specialist → Architect** (start Foundational).
- **Validity and renewal:** typically **two years**; renew by passing a current or higher exam.

## Track focus

- **Network Security:** PAN-OS/NGFW with App-ID/User-ID/Content-ID, Security Profiles, Panorama,
  HA, NAT/decryption, Prisma SD-WAN, and Prisma Access/SASE (SSE).
- **Security Operations:** Cortex XDR (detection/hunting/IR), XSIAM (AI-driven SOC), and XSOAR
  (playbook automation), queried with XQL.
- **Cloud Security:** Prisma/Cortex Cloud CNAPP (CSPM, CWPP, CIEM, IaC scanning), queried with
  RQL.
