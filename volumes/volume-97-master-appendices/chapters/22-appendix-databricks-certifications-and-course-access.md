# Chapter 22: Appendix — Databricks Certifications and Course Access

The **Databricks** certification program — the role-based data, ML, and AI
credentials plus the free accreditations — organized by role, with each
credential's focus, level, and the training and practice model. The lineup was
harvested on **26 July 2026** from **databricks.com/learn/certification** — the
same source that anchors
[Volume XLVIII — Databricks Certification Tracks](../../volume-48-databricks-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works at Databricks.** Training is delivered through **Databricks
Academy** — free self-paced courses and paid instructor-led training — with free
**exam guides** on each certification page. Practice is free on **Databricks Free
Edition / Community Edition**. Proctored exams are delivered online (Kryterion/
Webassessor); free **accreditations** are taken in Databricks Academy.

> **Currency.** Databricks revises exam guides as the platform evolves (e.g., DLT →
> Lakeflow Declarative Pipelines, Feature Store → Feature Engineering in Unity
> Catalog) — confirm the current guide on databricks.com before registering. Recent
> changes captured here: the new **Generative AI Engineer** and **Context Engineer**
> certifications and the **AI Agent Fundamentals** accreditation; the **Hadoop
> Migration Architect** certification retired 1 August 2024.

## Free training and entry points

- **[Databricks Certification](https://www.databricks.com/learn/certification)** —
  the authoritative catalog and per-exam guides
- **Databricks Academy** — free self-paced learning paths and paid ILT
- **Free/Community Edition** — a free workspace to practice Spark, SQL, Delta, and MLflow
- **Fundamentals accreditations** — free badges (Databricks, Generative AI, AI Agent)

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees (confirm at registration):** the **Associate** certification exams are the
  lower band; **Professional** exams are higher; **accreditations are free**.
- **Delivery:** online-proctored (Kryterion/Webassessor) for certifications;
  Databricks Academy for accreditations. Each certification has a published **exam
  guide** with weighted sections.
- **Prerequisites:** none enforced, though Professional exams assume the Associate
  skill set.
- **Validity and renewal:** certifications are valid for a defined period (commonly
  **two years**) and renewed by **retaking the current exam** — no
  continuing-education model.

## Selected exam-guide focus

- **Data Analyst Associate:** Databricks SQL & warehouses, analytic SQL,
  visualizations/dashboards, governance.
- **Data Engineer Associate:** Lakehouse Platform; ELT (Spark SQL/Python);
  incremental (Delta/Streaming/Auto Loader); production pipelines (Jobs/DLT); Unity
  Catalog governance.
- **Data Engineer Professional:** tooling, processing/optimization, data modeling
  (CDC/SCD), security/governance, monitoring, testing/deployment (Asset Bundles).
- **Machine Learning Associate:** ML workflows, MLflow, AutoML, Feature Store,
  Spark ML.
- **Machine Learning Professional:** experimentation, model lifecycle, deployment
  (batch/streaming/real-time), monitoring (drift).
- **Generative AI Engineer:** RAG, Vector Search, model selection, evaluation,
  governance, deployment. **Context Engineer:** context assembly, tools, memory for
  agents.
- **Apache Spark Developer Associate:** the Spark DataFrame API, architecture, and
  tuning.

## Notes

- **Accreditations vs certifications.** Fundamentals and Platform accreditations are
  free; the role-based exams are proctored certifications.
- **Exam guides are the study scope.** Download the current per-exam guide; the
  program adds and revises exams (GenAI Engineer, Context Engineer are recent).
- **Practice is free** on Databricks Free/Community Edition.
- **Renew by exam** within the validity window.
- **Related practice** in the encyclopedia: data/observability in **Volume XLV
  (Splunk)**, AI infrastructure in **Volume XLVI (NVIDIA)**, automation in **Volume
