# Chapter 50: Appendix — Elastic Certifications and Course Access

The **Elastic** certification program — the credentials for building on the Elastic Stack
(Elasticsearch, Kibana, Elastic Agent, and Elastic Security) for search, analytics, observability, and
security — organized by certification, with the exam model, the training model, and the version model.
The program was verified on **29 July 2026** from **elastic.co** (the certification hub and the
individual exam pages) — the same sources that anchor
[Volume LXXXVI — Elastic Certification Tracks](../../volume-86-elastic-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works.** Elastic offers **free on-demand training** and paid instructor-led courses through
Elastic Training, plus extensive free documentation (which is permitted during the performance-based
exams). Three of the four exams — **Certified Engineer**, **Certified Analyst**, and **Certified
Observability Engineer** — are **hands-on, performance-based**, completed on live Elasticsearch clusters
while a remote proctor watches; the **Certified SIEM Analyst** is a **cognitive** exam (multiple-choice,
select-all, fill-in-the-blank, true/false). Hands-on practice uses a **free** self-managed Elastic Stack
(or Docker, or an Elastic Cloud trial). Exams are online-proctored, and the Engineer and Observability
exams are priced at **$500 USD** each.

> **Currency.** The **Certified Engineer** exam updates from **Elastic 8.15 to 9.3 on 1 September 2026**
> (August 2026 candidates take 8.15; those scheduled near the transition may reschedule). The 9.3 update
> adds ES|QL, semantic search, and RBAC/security topics. Study the current version and confirm the
> catalog on elastic.co.

## Free and low-cost resources and entry points

- **[Elastic Certification](https://www.elastic.co/training/certification)** — the authoritative
  certifications and exam pages
- **[Certification FAQ](https://www.elastic.co/training/certification/faq)** — exam policy, delivery,
  and procedural guidance
- **[Elastic Training](https://www.elastic.co/training)** — **free** on-demand courses plus
  instructor-led training
- **Free Elastic Stack** — self-managed, Docker, or an Elastic Cloud trial for hands-on practice
- **Elastic documentation** — comprehensive and permitted during the performance-based exams

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees:** the Certified Engineer and Observability Engineer exams are **$500 USD**; much of the
  training is **free**. Confirm current pricing on elastic.co.
- **Delivery:** online-proctored. Three exams are **performance-based** (hands-on on a live cluster);
  the SIEM Analyst is a **cognitive** exam.
- **Prerequisites:** none formally required, though the recommended training and hands-on experience are
  strongly advised.
- **Validity and renewal:** re-certify against the current major version (the Engineer exam moves to 9.3
  on 1 September 2026).

## The certifications

Verified against elastic.co on 29 July 2026.

| Certification | Focus and format |
| --- | --- |
| Elastic Certified Engineer | Elasticsearch cluster + search development; performance-based; $500; 8.15→9.3 (1 Sep 2026) |
| Elastic Certified Analyst | Kibana data visualization + advanced analysis; performance-based |

## Notes

- **Practice, don't memorize.** Three of the four exams are performance-based on live clusters.
- **Free training + free Stack.** Much of the preparation is available at no cost.
- **9.3 transition.** Study ES|QL, semantic search, and RBAC for the Engineer exam after 1 September 2026.
- **Defensive framing.** The SIEM Analyst path — detection, investigation, and threat hunting in Elastic
  Security — is defensive security operations on your own environment.
- **Related coverage** in the encyclopedia: the observability volumes (**XI**, **LIII** LibreNMS,
  **LIV** OpenTelemetry, **LV** Prometheus) and Splunk (**XLV**) for adjacent analytics and SIEM skills.
