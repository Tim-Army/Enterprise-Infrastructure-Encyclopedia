# Chapter 25: Appendix — Nutanix Certifications and Course Access

The **Nutanix University** certification program — the four-level ladder (Associate,
Professional, Master, Expert) across infrastructure, automation, database, storage,
and cloud tracks — organized by level, with each credential's focus, blueprint
sections, and the training and delivery model. The lineup was verified on **27 July
2026** from **nutanix.com/support-services/training-certification** — the same source
that anchors
[Volume LI — Nutanix Certification Tracks](../../volume-51-nutanix-certifications/README.md).
Third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources.

**How access works at Nutanix.** Training is delivered through **Nutanix University**
(self-paced and instructor-led courses, learning paths) with free **Exam Blueprint
Guides** on each certification page. Practice is free on **Community Edition (CE)**.
Exams are delivered through **Pearson VUE** (remote-proctored or at a test center).

> **Currency.** Nutanix revises exams by **version** and refreshes the program
> regularly — version **7.5** launched for **NCA** and **NCP-MCI** in 2026 (NCA 7.5
> appointments from 28 July 2026), and since **1 August 2025 all certifications are
> valid three years** (up from two). Confirm the current version and blueprint on
> nutanix.com before registering.

## Free training and entry points

- **[Nutanix Training & Certification](https://www.nutanix.com/support-services/training-certification/certifications)** —
  the authoritative catalog and per-exam Exam Blueprint Guides
- **Nutanix University** — self-paced and instructor-led courses and learning paths
- **Community Edition (CE)** — a free AOS/AHV build to practice `ncli`/`acli`/Prism
- **Test Drive** — hosted hands-on trials of Nutanix features

## Fees, delivery, and renewal

- **Fees (confirm at registration):** **NCA US$99**, **NCP US$199** (each track),
  **NCM-MCI US$299**.
- **Delivery:** **Pearson VUE** (online-proctored or test center). Each exam has a
  published **Exam Blueprint Guide** with sections (Section 3 = detailed objectives).
- **Prerequisites:** none enforced; higher tiers assume the lower skill set.
- **Validity and renewal:** all certifications are valid **three years** (since 1
  August 2025) and renewed by **passing the current version** of the exam.

## Blueprint sections

- **NCA:** Recognize Nutanix Solutions and Tools; Describe Platform Administration;
  Describe Cluster Configuration and Maintenance; Understand Platform Health and
  Monitoring.
- **NCP-MCI:** Manage Cluster/Nodes/Features; Manage Cluster Storage; Configure
  Networking and Network Security; Analyze/Remediate Performance; Configure/Analyze/
  Remediate Alerts and Events; Manage VM Deployment and Configuration.
- **NCP-MCA:** Automation Concepts and Principles; Deploy/Configure Self-Service;
  Validate Blueprints, Runbooks, Playbooks, and Automation Settings.
- **NCP-DB:** Deploy and Configure an NDB Solution; Monitor Alerts and Storage Usage;
  Operate and Maintain; Administer an NDB Environment.
- **NCP-US:** Deploy and Upgrade; Configure and Utilize; Analyze and Monitor;
  Troubleshoot Nutanix Unified Storage.
- **NCP-CI-AWS / NCP-CI-Azure:** Planning; Deploying; Configuring; Managing an NC2
  environment.
- **NCM-MCI:** Administration and Planning; Storage; Data Protection; Security;
  Workload Management; Networking.
- **NCX-MCI:** Customer Consultation; Conceptual/Logical Design; Physical Design
  (design-and-defend).

- **Practice is free** on Community Edition; Test Drive offers hosted trials.
