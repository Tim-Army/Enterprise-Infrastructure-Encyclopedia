# Volume XC — Datadog Certification Tracks

> The whole Datadog certification program in one volume — the Datadog Fundamentals, APM & Distributed
> Tracing, Log Management, Database Monitoring, and Cloud SIEM certifications — across the observability
> platform (Agent, metrics, dashboards, monitors, traces, and logs), with hands-on Agent, API, and
> pipeline labs, verified against datadoghq.com.

## Overview

Volume XC maps the **Datadog** certification program — the credentials for monitoring and observing
modern systems on the **Datadog** platform. Datadog **relaunched** its certification program on a new
learning platform, offering **Datadog Fundamentals** (the core platform), **APM & Distributed Tracing
Fundamentals**, and **Log Management Fundamentals**, plus the newer **Database Monitoring** and **Cloud
SIEM for AWS** certifications. This volume continues the encyclopedia's DevOps & observability cluster
alongside OpenTelemetry (LIV), Prometheus (LV), Splunk (XLV), and Elastic (LXXXVI).

This is a **certification-tracks** volume, like the other vendor volumes (XXXIX–LXXXIX): it maps the
program — the credentials and exam domains — and teaches each with a hands-on walkthrough. Every
certification and exam detail was **verified against datadoghq.com and the Datadog Learning Center on 29
July 2026** (the certification overview and exam pages; third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as
sources).

Chapters follow the certification ladder:

- **Chapter 01** frames the program — the certifications, the $100/90-question exams, and the free Datadog Learning Center.
- **Chapter 02** covers the **platform and Agent** — installation, metric types, tags, and DogStatsD.
- **Chapter 03** covers **infrastructure monitoring** — integrations, the metrics explorer, and the host map.
- **Chapter 04** covers **dashboards and data visualization**.
- **Chapter 05** covers **monitors and alerting** — thresholds, notifications, SLOs, and downtimes.
- **Chapter 06** takes **APM & Distributed Tracing Fundamentals**.
- **Chapter 07** takes **Log Management Fundamentals**.
- **Chapter 08** covers **Database Monitoring and Cloud SIEM**.
- **Chapter 09** covers **certification prep, currency, and career**.

Every chapter follows the standard structure defined in
[templates/chapter.md](../../templates/chapter.md) and enforced by
[EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md](../../EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md), including per-topic hands-on labs and
knowledge checks.

> **Scope.** Datadog monitoring, tracing, logging, and security analytics are authorized observability
> work — instrumenting, visualizing, and defending your own systems. The Cloud SIEM material (detection
> rules and signal triage) is framed as defensive security operations on your own telemetry.
