# Volume XLIII Glossary

Definitions for terms used in **Volume XLIII — Offensive Security (OffSec)
Certification Tracks**, alphabetized. See also the [volume index](INDEX.md) and
the [master glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md) for cross-volume terminology.

**Authorization** — Explicit permission (and defined scope) to test a system; the
first and non-negotiable control before any offensive activity. Used in Chapters
01 and 03.

**KLCP (Kali Linux Certified Professional)** — OffSec's Kali Linux administration
credential (course PEN-103). Used in Chapter 02.

**MITRE ATT&CK** — A knowledge base of adversary tactics and techniques (TTPs)
used to structure detection and hunting; MITRE ATLAS is its AI-focused
counterpart. Used in Chapters 07 and 08.

**OSAI (OffSec AI Red Teamer)** — OffSec's AI-security credential (course AI-300):
prompt injection, adversarial ML, and AI supply-chain risks. Used in Chapter 08.

**OSCC (OffSec CyberCore Certified)** — OffSec's foundational credential (courses
SEC-100 and SJD-100). Used in Chapter 02.

**OSCE³ (OffSec Certified Expert 3)** — An expert umbrella designation earned by
holding OSEP, OSWE, and OSED. Used in Chapter 08.

**OSCP / OSCP+** — OffSec's benchmark penetration-testing credential (course
PEN-200); the lifetime OSCP and the renewable three-year OSCP+ are both earned by
passing the exam. Used in Chapter 03.

**OSDA (OffSec Defense Analyst)** — OffSec's detection credential (course
SOC-200). Used in Chapter 07.

**OSED (OffSec Exploit Developer)** — OffSec's Windows user-mode exploit-development
credential (course EXP-301); part of OSCE³. Used in Chapter 06.

**OSEE (OffSec Exploitation Expert)** — OffSec's top exploit-development credential
(course EXP-401). Used in Chapter 06.

**OSEP (OffSec Experienced Penetration Tester)** — OffSec's advanced pentest
credential (course PEN-300), focused on evasion and breaching defenses; part of
OSCE³. Used in Chapter 04.

**OSIR (OffSec Incident Responder)** — OffSec's incident-response credential
(course IR-200). Used in Chapter 07.

**OSTH (OffSec Threat Hunter)** — OffSec's threat-hunting credential (course
TH-200). Used in Chapter 07.

**OSWA (OffSec Web Assessor)** — OffSec's black-box web-assessment credential
(course WEB-200). Used in Chapter 05.

**OSWE (OffSec Web Expert)** — OffSec's white-box web-exploitation credential
(course WEB-300); part of OSCE³. Used in Chapter 05.

**OSWP (OffSec Wireless Professional)** — OffSec's wireless credential (course
PEN-210). Used in Chapter 04.

**PICERL** — The incident-response lifecycle: Preparation, Identification,
Containment, Eradication, Recovery, Lessons Learned. Used in Chapter 07.

**Practical exam** — An OffSec exam solved by compromising or defending machines
in a lab over many hours, followed by a professional report. Used in Chapters 01
and 03.

**Prompt injection** — An attack where untrusted content supplies instructions a
model may follow; defended by treating input as data and constraining tool
permissions. Used in Chapter 08.

**"+" renewal model** — OffSec's three-year renewal for OSCP+, OSCC, OSTH, OSIR,
and OSAI+ (via CPE, re-exam, or another qualifying exam); other credentials do not
expire. Used in Chapters 01 and 09.

**ROP (Return-Oriented Programming)** — Chaining existing executable code
fragments to bypass DEP; a concept covered in exploit development. Used in Chapter
06.
