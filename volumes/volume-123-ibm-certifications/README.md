# Volume CXXIII — IBM Certification Tracks

> The certification map for **IBM** — the largest single-vendor program in the encyclopedia, **62 current
> certifications** across seven portfolios, verified 3 August 2026 on the IBM Training credentials catalog
> and Pearson VUE. The volume first untangles the three things called "IBM credentials" — proctored
> **certifications** (this volume), **TechXchange badges**, and Coursera **Professional Certificates** —
> then maps the catalog by portfolio: **AI/watsonx and quantum** (watsonx.ai/data/governance, Assistant,
> Orchestrate, Data Scientist, Qiskit), **security** (the QRadar SIEM V7.5 ladder, Guardium, Verify
> Access), **data platforms** (Db2 for z/OS and LUW, Informix), **analytics** (Cognos, Planning
> Analytics, Cloud Pak for Data), **integration and messaging** (MQ, ACE, API Connect, DataPower,
> Sterling), **automation/observability/AIOps** (Cloud Pak for Business Automation, Business Automation
> Workflow, FileNet, Turbonomic, Instana, Cloud Pak for AIOps), and **systems and asset management**
> (z/OS, AIX, IBM i, WebSphere, Maximo, TRIRIGA). Six **"PLUS" combination** certifications bundle a
> **Red Hat Certified Specialist** (OpenShift) exam. Exams are delivered by **Pearson VUE / OnVUE**,
> badged on **Credly**, version-pinned, and flagged **"Retiring soon"** in the catalog. Where a free
> engine exists — **Qiskit**, **Db2 Community Edition**, **IBM MQ Advanced for Developers** — the labs
> are runnable; elsewhere they are design-level with free-primitive models.

## Overview

Volume CXXIII is a **certification-tracks volume** for a program too large to teach exam-by-exam, so it
teaches **portfolio-by-portfolio**: each chapter takes one product family, states what each product does
(the certifications differ by product, not by slogan), and drills the concept each exam tests with a
walkthrough lab — runnable on a free engine where one exists, design-level with a free model where the
product is commercial.

Its standing disciplines are the three-credential distinction (certification vs badge vs Coursera
certificate), version pinning, and the catalog's "Retiring soon" flags — all folded into the currency
plan in Chapter 09.

## What you will be able to do

- Distinguish IBM certifications from TechXchange badges and Coursera certificates.
- Navigate all 62 certifications by portfolio and choose a role-aligned path.
- Run real labs on free IBM engines (Qiskit, Db2 Community Edition, MQ for Developers).
- Explain what each major IBM product does and which certification validates it.
- Handle the six Red Hat "PLUS" combinations and keep a plan current against version and retirement churn.
