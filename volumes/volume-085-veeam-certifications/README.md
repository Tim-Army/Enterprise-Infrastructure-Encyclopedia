## Overview

Volume LXXXV maps the **Veeam** certification program — the credentials for protecting, recovering, and
securing data with the **Veeam Data Platform**. The program was rebuilt for 2026: the long-standing
**VMCE** (Veeam Certified Engineer) exam runs only until **31 March 2026** and the **VMCA** architect
credential was **retired on 30 November 2025**; in their place is the new flagship **VMCE+ (Veeam
Certified Engineer Plus)** — live now and aligned to **Veeam Backup & Replication v13** — with the
**VMCSE (Veeam Certified Security Expert)** arriving in **Q2 2026**. This volume opens onto the Data,
storage & backup cluster alongside NetApp (LXXXIV).

This is a **certification-tracks** volume, like the other vendor volumes (XXXIX–LXXXIV): it maps the
program — the credentials, the required trainings, and the platform components — and teaches each with a
hands-on walkthrough. Every credential and product area was **verified against veeam.com on 29 July
2026** (the certification and Veeam University Pro pages, plus the official Veeam Community Resource
Hub; third-party exam-dump sites were excluded as sources).

Chapters follow the VMCE+ and VMCSE curriculum:

- **Chapter 01** frames the program — the VMCE→VMCE+ transition, the retired VMCA, the coming VMCSE, Veeam University Pro, Pearson VUE delivery, and Credly badges.
- **Chapter 02** takes the **Veeam Data Platform** architecture — the backup server, proxies, repositories, and editions.
- **Chapter 03** takes **backup jobs** — configure, manage, and recover, with GFS retention and application-aware processing.
- **Chapter 04** takes **replication and recovery** — replication jobs, failover/failback, and Instant Recovery.
- **Chapter 05** takes **scale-out repositories and cloud** — SOBR tiers, object-storage immutability, Data Cloud Vault, and the 3-2-1-1-0 rule.
- **Chapter 06** takes **Veeam ONE** — monitor, manage, and analyze with alarms, reports, and capacity planning.
- **Chapter 07** takes **Veeam Recovery Orchestrator** — scale, automate, and secure with recovery plans and orchestrated DR testing.
- **Chapter 08** takes **cyber resilience and security** (the VMCSE) — immutability, malware and ransomware detection, the Security and Compliance Analyzer, and Zero Trust Data Resilience.
- **Chapter 09** takes **certification prep, currency, and career**.

Every chapter follows the standard structure defined in
[templates/chapter.md](../../templates/chapter.md) and enforced by
[EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md](../../EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md), including per-topic hands-on labs and
knowledge checks.

> **Scope.** Veeam backup, replication, recovery, and cyber resilience are authorized, **defensive**
> data-protection work — protecting and recovering **your own** data. The security material
> (immutability, ransomware detection, compliance analysis, threat hunting) is framed as defensive
> protection of your own backups.
