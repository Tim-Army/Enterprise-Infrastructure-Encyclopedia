# Volume LXIII Glossary

Definitions for terms introduced in **Volume LXIII — Public Sector Data Governance (PSDGP)**.
See the [master glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md) for cross-volume terms.

- **CDO (Chief Data Officer)** — the accountable executive for data as an asset; often legally mandated in government agencies.
- **CPD (Continuing Professional Development)** — the professional-development activity through which PSDGP is renewed every three years (not CEUs or re-examination).
- **Data custodian** — the IT role that operates the systems storing and processing data (distinct from the business data owner).
- **Data owner** — the business role accountable for a data domain, approving access and classification.
- **Data steward** — the role responsible for day-to-day data quality, metadata, and issue resolution.
- **DGSP (Data Governance and Stewardship Professional)** — ICCP's general (non-public-sector) data governance credential; a 'core'-level cert that underpins PSDGP and satisfies its alternate prerequisite.
- **Evidence Act** — the Foundations for Evidence-Based Policymaking Act, which (with the OPEN Government Data Act) requires open, machine-readable federal data and data inventories.
- **FedRAMP** — the Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program; the authorization required to host federal data in the cloud.
- **FISMA** — the Federal Information Security Modernization Act, setting the federal security-management baseline (implemented via NIST SP 800-53).
- **FOIA (Freedom of Information Act)** — the law requiring agencies to disclose records on request within deadlines, subject to exemptions.
- **Golden record** — the single authoritative version of a master-data entity, chosen by a survivorship rule.
- **ICCP (Institute for the Certification of Computing Professionals)** — the vendor-neutral body that issues PSDGP and DGSP.
- **MDM (Master Data Management)** — the practice of maintaining one authoritative version of core entities (person, place, organization).
- **NARA (National Archives and Records Administration)** — the body whose schedules govern federal records retention and disposition, under the Federal Records Act.
- **PII (Personally Identifiable Information)** — personal data (SSN, DOB, address, etc.) protected under the Privacy Act and related rules.
- **Privacy Act of 1974** — the law governing federal collection, use, and disclosure of personal records.
- **PSDGP / CPSDGP (Public Sector Data Governance Professional)** — the ICCP credential mapped by this volume; a 100-question, 90-minute proctored exam, valid three years.
- **RACI** — a responsibility-assignment matrix marking, per decision, who is Responsible, Accountable (exactly one), Consulted, and Informed.
- **R2C (TheR2C)** — the training partner that delivers the three-day PSDGP preparation course ending in the ICCP exam.
- **Survivorship rule** — the MDM logic that selects which attribute value wins when records conflict (e.g., most recent update).
