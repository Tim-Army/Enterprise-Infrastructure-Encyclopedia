# Volume XXXIX — CompTIA Certification Tracks

> The whole CompTIA program in one volume — the vendor-neutral foundation the
> rest of the encyclopedia is built on: the classic Core, Infrastructure,
> Cybersecurity, and Data "plus" certifications, the new hands-on **Xpert
> Series** ("Pro") credentials, and the AI and Essentials microcredentials,
> with every current exam code verified against comptia.org.

## Overview

Volume XXXIX catalogs **CompTIA**, the industry's foundational,
**vendor-neutral** certification program. Where the rest of the encyclopedia's
certification volumes cover a single vendor's advanced tracks — Cisco (III,
XXV, XXVII–XXX), Juniper (XXXI), Palo Alto (XVI), Fortinet (XIX), AWS (XVII),
Azure (XXXIII), Google Cloud (XXXIV), Microsoft (XXXVI–XXXVIII), Zscaler
(XXXV) — CompTIA is the **on-ramp beneath all of them**: the credentials that
prove someone can support a PC, run a network, secure a system, administer
Linux, and analyze data before they specialize on any one vendor's stack.

This is a **certification-tracks** volume, like the Juniper, Dell, Azure,
Google Cloud, and Microsoft-beyond-Azure volumes. Its job is to map the
program — the pathways, credentials, current exam codes, blueprint domains,
prerequisites (CompTIA recommends but rarely enforces them), stackable
certifications, renewal, and how each credential feeds the encyclopedia's
deeper vendor volumes.

CompTIA's program was **substantially restructured** after its 2024–2025
acquisition and relaunch, so the lineup is larger and more layered than older
study maps show. It now runs on **two parallel lines**, plus a microcredential
tier:

- the **classic exam-based "plus" certifications** (Tech+, A+, Network+,
  Security+, Cloud+, Linux+, Server+, CySA+, PenTest+, SecurityX, Data+,
  DataSys+, Project+; the business-cloud Cloud Essentials+ retired in September
  2025, replaced by the Cloud Essentials course);
- the new **Xpert Series** of hands-on, performance-based **"Pro"**
  credentials (Security Pro, CyberDefense Pro, Ethical Hacker Pro, Linux Pro,
  Hybrid Server Pro, CloudNetX, and the AI/OT security certs SecAI+ and
  SecOT+); and
- a broad **Essentials** microcredential tier (AI Essentials, Business
  Essentials, and more).

Every exam code in this volume was **verified against comptia.org on 26 July
2026** — which matters, because CompTIA has renamed and renumbered across the
board: **CASP+ became SecurityX (CAS-005)**, **ITF+ became Tech+ (FC0-U71)**,
A+ moved to the **220-1201/220-1202 Core Series**, Network+ to **N10-009**,
Security+ to **SY0-701**, Linux+ to **XK0-006**, and Data+ to **DA0-002**.

Chapters are organized by pathway:

- **Chapter 01** frames the program — vendor-neutral value, the classic-vs-Xpert
  structure, delivery, stackable certifications, and renewal.
- **Chapters 02–05** cover the classic pathways: Core, Infrastructure,
  Cybersecurity, and Data and AI.
- **Chapter 06** covers the new **Xpert Series** "Pro" credentials.
- **Chapters 07–08** cover professional and Essentials credentials, and
  Continuing Education and renewal.
- **Chapter 09** builds a CompTIA career path and maps it to the encyclopedia's
  vendor volumes.

Every chapter follows the standard structure defined in
[templates/chapter.md](../../templates/chapter.md) and enforced by
[EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md](../../EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md), including a hands-on
exam-preparation lab and knowledge checks.

## Certification alignment

This volume *is* the certification map for CompTIA, recorded in
[CERTIFICATION_BLUEPRINTS.md](../../CERTIFICATION_BLUEPRINTS.md). The full
catalog with pathways, exam codes, the free/for-cost training options, and the
Continuing Education renewal model is in the
[CompTIA certification appendix](../volume-97-master-appendices/chapters/13-appendix-comptia-certifications-and-course-access.md)
(Master Appendices, Volume XCVII). CompTIA credentials are the foundation that
the vendor volumes build on — Security+ under the security tracks, Network+
under the networking tracks, Linux+ under Volumes XIV/XXI, Cloud+ under the
cloud volumes, and Server+ under Volume XXXVI.

## Lab coverage

Every chapter carries hands-on labs, and the pathway chapters go **per topic**:
there is **one walkthrough lab for every weighted exam domain of every
certification** — Core (25 labs across Tech+, A+ Core 1/2, Network+, Security+),
Infrastructure (15: Cloud+, Linux+, Server+), Cybersecurity (13: CySA+, PenTest+,
SecurityX), Data and AI (15: Data+, DataSys+, DataAI), the Xpert/Expansion certs
(14: CloudNetX, SecAI+, SecOT+), and Project+ (4), plus program, renewal, and
career-planning labs. Each is a real command-line walkthrough — objective,
runnable commands, expected result, a negative test, and cleanup — ending with a
**`**Lab verified by:** *pending*`** sign-off. (The "Pro" competency-assessment
courses have no published weightings; their hands-on practice lives in their
ladder cert's chapter or vendor volume.)

## Software and platform baseline

This volume references **comptia.org** (the certification catalog and exam
pages), the **Pearson VUE** exam-delivery platform, **CompTIA CertMaster** and
the **Continuing Education (CE)** program, and Credly-style digital badging.
Exam codes and program structure were verified against comptia.org on 26 July
2026; CompTIA renumbers and renames frequently, so confirm the current exam
version and status on comptia.org before scheduling.
