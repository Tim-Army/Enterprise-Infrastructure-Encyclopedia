# Volume LXXV — EC-Council Certification Tracks

> The whole EC-Council certification program in one volume — the flagship CEH v13 (with AI) plus the
> pentest, network-defense, forensics, SOC/IR/intel, cloud/DevSecOps/AppSec, executive, and AI-security
> tracks — with hands-on, defensive labs, verified against eccouncil.org.

## Overview

Volume LXXV maps the **EC-Council** certification program — the International Council of E-Commerce
Consultants, best known for the **Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH)**, now at **v13 with AI**.
EC-Council programs are **ANSI 17024** accredited and CEH/CND/CHFI meet **US DoD 8140/8570** baseline
requirements, with practical exams delivered in the **iLabs Cyber Range**. It joins the
encyclopedia's security volumes (OffSec XLIII, GIAC LXXIV, ISC2 XL, CompTIA XXXIX, ISACA XLIV,
Check Point LXXIII, Enterprise Cybersecurity X).

This is a **certification-tracks** volume, like the other vendor volumes (XXXIX–LXXIV): it maps the
program — the tracks and their credentials — and teaches each with a hands-on walkthrough. Every
track and credential was **verified against eccouncil.org on 28 July 2026**, including CEH v13's AI
integration and the new AI Security & Management family (CAIPM, COASP, CRAGE).

Chapters follow the tracks:

- **Chapter 01** frames the program — CEH v13, the tracks, ANSI/DoD 8140, and the ECC/iLabs exam model.
- **Chapter 02** takes **Foundations and Essentials** (CCT, CSCU, ECSS, the Essentials series).
- **Chapter 03** takes **CEH — Certified Ethical Hacker** as authorized methodology plus countermeasures.
- **Chapter 04** takes **Network Defense** (CND, ICS/SCADA).
- **Chapter 05** takes **Penetration Testing** as authorized methodology (CPENT, WAHS, LPT Master).
- **Chapter 06** takes **Digital Forensics** (CHFI).
- **Chapter 07** takes **SOC, Incident Handling, and Threat Intelligence** (CSA, ECIH, CTIA).
- **Chapter 08** takes **Cloud, DevSecOps, Application Security, and Encryption** (CCSE, ECDE, CASE, ECES).
- **Chapter 09** takes **Executive and AI Security**, plus currency and career (CCISO, CAIPM, COASP, CRAGE).

Every chapter follows the standard structure defined in
[templates/chapter.md](../../templates/chapter.md) and enforced by
[EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md](../../EDITORIAL_STANDARDS.md), including per-topic hands-on labs and
knowledge checks.

> **Scope.** EC-Council's "ethical hacking" and penetration-testing content is **authorized,
> educational methodology only** — every offensive technique is gated on authorization and scope and
> paired with its defensive countermeasure. Every lab is defensive (hardening, detection, forensics,
> response) or authorized assessment (scope, rules of engagement, safe local commands) — never an
> operational attack.
